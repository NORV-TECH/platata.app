What Platata does

The app isn't out yet. This is what it does.

  • Sell

    Open the register, ring up a sale in seconds, and close out at the end of the day. A favorites grid for what you sell most and barcode scanning for everything else. Cash or card at the counter, with tips and dining options where you need them.

  • Keep it straight

    Discounts and fees, sales tax, and refunds that require a passcode, with per-employee control over who can issue one. Receipts you decide on: numbering, your own branding, and which lines actually print.

  • Your catalog

    Products, categories, modifiers, option pickers, combos, and units of measure. Modifier triggers let you set a rule once: if this, then offer that. That way, nobody behind the counter has to remember it.

  • Your team

    Employee accounts with passcodes and roles, so each person sees what they should and nothing more. Employee pay is tracked in the app, not on paper.

  • Your customers

    A client list that shows who actually spends and how much, with tags and points. Not just a list of names and numbers.

  • Your numbers

    Reports on the day, the week, and the month. Stock on hand, suppliers, and a supplier invoice you file by taking a photo of it. When the time comes, hand the whole thing to your accountant in one step.

  • Your devices

    One phone is enough to start. Add a counter register, give staff their own phone registers, put a tablet on the floor, connect printers, and run a kitchen order screen, including one that works over WhatsApp. They pair over your own network.

  • Your businesses

    More than one shop, or a branch of the one you have. Each keeps its own catalog and its own team, and you switch between them in the app. Back up a business and restore it later.

Who it's for

Restaurants and cafés. Shops, minimarkets and pharmacies. And service businesses, from hair salons and barbers to plumbers, locksmiths, phone repair, car washes, dog grooming and movers.

Registers

Several registers on the same sale.

Phone register

Every waiter carries a register

  • Your waiters just download Platata on their phone and sign in with their own PIN.
  • They take the order at the table, and it goes straight to the kitchen.
  • And if card payments are turned on, they take the card right there.
Phone register

Your register goes where you go

  • For people who work on the go: mobile hairdressers, cleaners, route sellers.
  • Turn any phone into a register. No counter needed.
  • No signal? Keep selling. Everything syncs when it comes back.
Counter register

Your main register, on a tablet

  • Your main checkout station. Best for tablet stands, barcode scanners and receipt printers.
  • Open Platata on the tablet, enter a code, and it is connected to your account.
  • Each employee picks their profile and signs in with their PIN.
Kitchen display

No more lost tickets

  • Replace paper tickets with a digital kitchen display.
  • Orders go straight to each station, showing how long they have been waiting.
  • No tablet? Every order can reach your kitchen's WhatsApp, at no cost and with no extra hardware.
Printers

Every ticket prints in the right place

  • Connect receipt and kitchen printers.
  • Orders and receipts print automatically at the right station.
  • Print checks and receipts on a Bluetooth receipt printer.

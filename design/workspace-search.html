Workspace Search

A search box at the top of My business that finds settings, the menu, clients, the team, connections, sales and invoices as you type, grouped by kind, and lets you change a setting right in the results. Every phone below is live: type, tap the chips, flip the switches.

Built against the app's code on claude/payroll-us (the newest line: sim/usa-canada plus payroll). The My business panel, the Settings page, Connections, employee phones and every setting name are the real ones. The business is the demo's Burger House in Colombia.

How search decides

The rules the phones above run on. Simple on purpose, so they're easy to build and easy to predict.

  1. Every word typed must match the start of a word. Case and accents don't matter: andres finds Andrés, ham pol finds Hamburguesa de Pollo. With 3+ letters it also matches inside a word. Fold text with the app's existing foldForSearch (utils/TextFold.kt).
  2. More than names are searched. Each setting has aliases in English, Spanish, French and Portuguese, plus the words people actually use: modifiers → Add-ons, dark mode → Appearance, receipt printer → Printers and other connections. Records match on secondary fields: a client's phone, a sale number, the employee, client and dishes on a sale, the options inside an add-on, the employee behind a connected phone.
  3. Scores. Exact name 100 · name starts with it 92 · words found 84 down to 76 (earlier is better) · alias 6 less than the same on the name · keyword 55 · secondary field 45. Sales ×0.85, connections ×0.85 and invoices ×0.9, so they never bury settings or people. Frequently used things get up to +2.5.
  4. Best match only when there's a clear winner: the top result scores 85 or more and leads the next by 6 or more. Two people called Andrés tie, so nothing is picked for you. An employee as Best match shows their phone connection and its next step.
  5. Sections. Create actions first (at most 2). When the Best match is a page, what's inside it comes next (Taxes → each tax, Connections → each device). Then sections by best score, in steps of 10; ties use one fixed order: taxes, payment methods, connections, printers and screens, add-ons, settings, my business, menu, categories, combos, clients, team, sales, invoices. Short configured lists keep their own order, and problems ("Not connected") go first. In All, 3 rows per section and "See all N".
  6. Settings act in place, with the same rules as their screen. A switch in a result is the same switch: in Colombia turning on IVA turns off Impoconsumo (with Undo), and Tables can't be on with For Here. Every setting shows its path (Settings › Getting paid) so people learn where it lives.
  7. Only what this person may open. Settings, Connections and Invoices are owner-only today, so they never appear for a staff PIN session or on an employee's phone.
  8. Never a dead end. No results: the closest matches using the first 3 letters of each word, "New dish …" with what was typed, and a way into Settings.
  9. Speed (proposal). Settings, menu, clients, team and connections are on the device and update on every keystroke. Sales older than today search the database about 250 ms after typing stops.

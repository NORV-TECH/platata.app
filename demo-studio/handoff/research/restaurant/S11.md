# S11 — Reportes (Reports), as seen by a RESTAURANT

Source: /home/claude/suma-plus-android @ d1b7698d (read only). Paths below are under `app/src/main/java/com/example/`.

## 1. What it does, who, where
- One page with the shop's numbers for a period you choose with chips: a sales chart, Sales − Refunds = Net sales, number of sales, average sale, how you got paid, sales by category, by dining option, top items, sales by employee.
- **Owner's phone ("Tu teléfono")**: full report, plus the "Enviar al contador" button (S12) and the held-sales warnings.
- **Employee phone (Mini POS)** and a **staff PIN session**: only if the owner granted "Ver reportes". They see the same page **without** "Ventas por empleado", the held-sales banner, "Faltan N" and the accountant button; they see one grey line "Solo ves las ventas de esta caja." instead.
- Counter tablet (MamaPos) and kitchen screen: no Reports entry found in code for a restaurant (the tablet has no Reports row; the route gate `maySeeReports` exists but nothing on MamaPosScreen navigates to it — not found in code).

## 2. Setup before the demo
- Nothing to switch on for the owner. Opens on **Hoy** every time Reports is opened.
- Seed a few paid sales today (cash + QR + a card in CO/US/CA), at least one refund, at least two dining options used (e.g. "Para aquí" and "Mesas") so "Ventas por tipo de consumo" appears (hidden with fewer than 2), a couple of categories, and sales by 2+ people (owner + a waiter phone) so "Ventas por empleado" has rows.
- For the staff view: Team → employee → Permisos → "Ver reportes" (off by default for every employee; `Employee.canSeeReports` default false).
- Week starts Monday (`data/ReportRange.kt:20`). The "Hoy" window ends at the moment Reports is opened (see problem P1).

## 3. Country differences
Same screens and words everywhere. Only money format and two rows change.
- Money format (`utils/CurrencyUtils.kt:204`, golden file `functions/test-vectors/money-format.json`, value 1234.5):
  - CO `$ 1.234` (0 decimals, non-breaking space; 1234.5 rounds half-even) · CL `$1.234` (0 dec.) · MX `$1,234.50` · PE `S/ 1,234.50` · EC `$1.234,50` · AR `$ 1.234,50` · PA `USD1,234.50` (see P9) · BR `R$ 1.234,50` · US `$1,234.50` · CA `$1,234.50`.
  - Money follows the COUNTRY, not the app language (a US shop in Spanish still prints `$1,234.50`).
- "Redondeo en efectivo / Arredondamento em dinheiro / Cash rounding" row appears only where cash rounding exists and only when non-zero: **CA** (0.05), **CL** (10), **PE** (0.10) (`utils/CashRounding.kt:47-52`).
- "Cómo te pagaron" rows follow the methods used: card row only CO (Wompi) / US, CA (Stripe); in **CA** an Interac card sale is its own row "Interac" (`data/PosDao.kt:998-1015`).
- **US/CA**: card tips (Stripe tip screen) are inside the sale totals; there is no tips row (see P10).
- "Cobrados dos veces: N" (double charges) only shows if it ever happens (multi-register).
- Chart y-axis labels are short with no symbol: "119k", "1.2M" (`ReportsSalesChart.kt:283`).

## 6. UX/UI problems (verified in code)
- **P1 — "Hoy" freezes at the moment Reports opens.** The range end is `now` at open (`data/ReportRange.kt:98`, end exclusive in every query, `data/PosDao.kt:938-958`) and is only rebuilt on a chip tap or reopening. A sale made (or synced from a waiter phone) while the page is open never appears. Fix: for TODAY/THIS_* presets use an open end (or refresh `now` on each emission / every minute).
- **P2 — Dining option names show in English in Spanish/Portuguese.** The order stores the raw seeded name "For Here / To Go / Delivery / Tables" (`ui/pos/CartActions.kt:729`, seeds `data/repo/SeedStore.kt:110-114`) and Reports prints it as is (`ReportsScreen.kt:347-349`); the Settings screen translates by systemKey but Reports does not. Fix: store/look up the dining `systemKey` and show `dining_system_*` strings.
- **P3 — "N tx" is jargon** under each employee (`ReportsScreen.kt:383`, `reports_tx_count` "%d tx" in es/pt/en). Fix: "N ventas / N vendas / N sales" as a plural.
- **P4 — Hard-coded Spanish "Sin nombre"** for a sale with no employee name in the SQL (`data/PosDao.kt:1091`), shown to pt/en users too. Also an owner with a blank Google name stamps "" (row with no label). Fix: return NULL and use a translated string.
- **P5 — Comped dishes count as revenue in item/category lists.** "Cortesía" sales are saved at $0 (`ui/pos/CheckoutActions.kt:375`) but "Artículos con más ingresos" and "Ventas por categoría" sum `quantity × price` of every PAID order (`data/PosDao.kt:1215-1227`), so their totals exceed Net sales. Fix: exclude comp orders (or use the paid amount) and say "by revenue" only for paid money.
- **P6 — "Faltan N" doesn't say what is missing** (`held_owner_missing` "Faltan %1$d"). Fix: "Faltan N ventas de otras cajas".
- **P7 — Chart y-axis abbreviations ignore locale**: "1.2M" uses a dot even in CO/BR/AR (`ReportsSalesChart.kt:283-293`). Fix: format with the country's decimal mark.
- **P8 — Staff line may be untrue** (possible): "Solo ves las ventas de esta caja." is shown to every non-owner viewer, including a staff PIN session on the owner's phone, which holds every register's sales (`ReportsScreen.kt:164-178`). Fix: show it only on a secondary device.
- **P10 — US/CA card tips are counted as sales, with no tip line.** The Stripe tip is added into the order total (`ui/pos/CheckoutActions.kt:375-377`, `Order.tipAmount` kept separately, `data/Order.kt:279`) and every Reports query sums `totalAmount` (`data/PosDao.kt:938-958`), so Ventas/Net sales, average sale and "Tarjeta" include tips that belong to staff. Fix: subtract `tipAmount` from sales and show a "Propinas / Gorjetas / Tips" row.
- **P9 — Panama money prints "USD12.00"** (golden vector `functions/test-vectors/money-format.json` PA; affects every money figure, not only Reports). Fix: override PA to "$" / "B/." like the COP override.

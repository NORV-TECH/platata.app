# S20 — Vender en línea (as seen by a RESTAURANT)

Code: commit d1b7698d. App paths under `app/src/main/java/com/example/`; customer page under `public/store*`.

## 1. What it does, who, where
- The owner turns on a free web page with the menu at **`platata-app.web.app/<address>`** (`sync/OnlineStoreSlug.kt:22,107`). Customers order **for pickup** ("Para recoger") and, outside US/CA, **pay at pickup**. The order lands on the owner's phone (and every register) as an open ticket tagged "EN LÍNEA", rings, and appears on the kitchen screen like any open order (KDS shows every not-bumped order, `data/PosDao.kt:1023`). The cashier charges it like an open ticket.
- Settings: owner's phone only (panel row "Vender en línea", `ui/screens/home/HomeScreen.kt:1551`, gate `sync/SellOnline.kt` `SellOnlineGate.showRow`). Orders: owner phone, counter tablet, waiter phone (not the kitchen screen) ring (`sync/OnlineOrderAlertRules.kt`).

## 2. Setup / unlock rule
- **Unlock = "store open"** (`ui/pos/ConnectionsGate.kt:37`, `sync/ConnectionAllocator.kt` `storeOpen = take >= 1`): US/CA always open; elsewhere the business must have **a device actually using a PAID connection** = owner phone + included device + one paid device = **3 connections in use**. Buying a connection without a second device does NOT open it.
- Locked tap → `PosViewModel.openSellOnlineDoor` (`ui/PosViewModel.kt:3417-3421`) raises the generic "Necesitas 1 conexión más" dialog (`ui/app/ConnectionEventsHost.kt:42-47`).
- Once open: switch ON → step 1 address → step 2 hours → live. Needs categories with priced dishes. Not sold online: no category, no price/$0, suggested price, sold by weight, dishes with the options builder, dishes with chained/nested add-ons (`sync/OnlineStoreSellable.kt:61-70`).
- Alerts: Android notification permission asked once when the store is switched on.

## 3. Country differences
- Eligible: all 35 Americas countries incl. CO MX PE CL EC AR PA BR US CA (`sync/OnlineStoreRules.kt` `eligible`). Restaurant `onlineStoreEligible = true`.
- Payment row (`SellOnlineGate.cardOnlineRow`): US/CA with Stripe ready → switch "Tarjeta en línea — Tus clientes pueden pagar con tarjeta al hacer el pedido" (default ON); US/CA not ready → "Primero conecta los pagos con tarjeta ›"; **CO → grey "Tarjeta en línea · Pronto"** (Wompi online not built); others → no card row, pay at pickup only. No Pix online for BR.
- US/CA order card adds "más impuesto al recoger"; the customer page says "El impuesto se suma al recoger." (`sync/OnlineOrder.kt` `taxAddedAtPickup`).
- Customer page language = shop language (es / pt / en; `public/store/strings.js`), not the browser's. Money in shop currency (see S16 §3).
- Unlock: US/CA free ("La tienda online está incluida en tu país."); others need the 3rd connection.

## 5. Demo path (CO restaurant with 3 connections, ~10 taps + browser)
1. Panel › "Vender en línea" › switch ON.
2. Step 1: accept "la-parrilla-chapinero" › "Siguiente · tu horario".
3. Step 2: 12:00–22:00, prep 20 › "Guardar" → status "Abierta, recibiendo pedidos", link card.
4. "Compartir"/"QR" → open the link on a second phone (customer).
5. Customer: tap "Hamburguesa" › "Agregar al pedido" › "Ver pedido" › name + cell › "Revisar pedido" › "Hacer pedido" → "PEDIDO #3FA9C1".
6. Owner phone (app open) rings "Nuevo pedido en línea · #3FA9C1"; kitchen screen shows the ticket.
7. Owner: "Abiertas" › "Pedido en línea · #3FA9C1" › "Cobrar" › Efectivo → sale done.
US demo: same, plus "Tarjeta en línea" switch so the customer pays by card and the ticket says "PAGADO · TARJETA" → "Recogido".

## 6. UX/UI problems
1. **Locked store shows the wrong dialog**: tapping "Vender en línea" without the 3rd connection raises "Necesitas 1 conexión más — Todas tus conexiones están en uso. Agrega una y puedes conectar de inmediato." (`ui/PosViewModel.kt:3419`, `ui/app/ConnectionEventsHost.kt:42-47`) even with free connections/0 devices; the store line shows only if paid==0 and ≥1 device. Fix: a store-specific dialog "Tu tienda online se abre cuando usas 3 conexiones".
2. **Paying alone doesn't unlock**: store opens only when a paid connection is USED by a device (`ConnectionAllocator.kt` `storeOpen = take >= 1`), but copy says "Agrega tu tercera conexión" (`conn_store_lock_body`, `conn_dlg_store`). An owner who buys one with no second device stays locked. Fix: "Conecta un tercer equipo" or unlock on purchase.
3. **Link is `platata-app.web.app/...`** (`OnlineStoreSlug.kt:22`), not the brand domain platata.app — possible marketing mismatch.
4. **Orders are heard only while Platata is open on a device** (`SellOnlineScreen.kt:311`; server push not deployed) — risky for the demo; keep the owner phone app open.
5. **Colombia: "Tarjeta en línea · Pronto"** (`SellOnlineScreen.kt:332`) — card online exists only in US/CA; don't promise it in LatAm videos.
6. **Dishes with options/sizes builder or chained add-ons silently drop off the page** (`OnlineStoreSellable.kt:67-68`); only the per-category screen explains. Many restaurant menus lose dishes. Fix: say "N platos no salen en línea" on the main page.
7. Customer page shop name is iOS blue #007AFF (`public/store/store.css` `.shop`), not the restaurant/Platata colour (possible).

# R1 — El menú con IA desde una foto (build notes)

## 1. Built
- Empty restaurant register → "Tomar una foto" → camera (tips card the first time, one photo per page, "Listo (2)") → reading/AI waiting phases → review (every dish ticked, one misread price fixed by typing) → "Agregar N artículos" → back on the register with the dishes, stock photos where the matcher finds one, and the snackbar "N platos agregados".
- Device: owner phone only. Countries: all 10 (dishes, prices, money format from R.C; the misread price is the real price / 10). Stock photos follow utils/MenuImageMatcher.kt rules (so some countries show photos, some not).
- Checked at app-head 6736ddfb (pt camera tip "Cardápio completo" updated; review Save rules of MKT2-3 do not change this flow).

## 2. Left out
- The PDF / gallery door (system file picker), the camera permission dialog and the notification permission ask (Android system UI).
- Sizes in the review (only the AI can create them; the sample menu has none).
- The "¿Guardar sin precio?" ask and the empty-name block (MKT2-3) — the demo leaves no price or name empty.

## 3. New UX/UI problems
1. Stock-photo matcher still gives wrong photos: "Cheeseburger" gets fish_burger, "Mac & cheese" gets cheesecake, "Lemonade" gets limonada_de_coco, "Soda" gets guaraná, because wordsMatch accepts a word INSIDE another word (cheeseburger ⊃ burger, cheesecake ⊃ cheese). utils/MenuImageMatcher.kt:70-74. Fix: accept a contained word only as a plural/singular (small + "s"/"es"), never inside a compound. **BLOCKS THE DEMO** (US video shows a fish burger on the Cheeseburger).
2. Camera "Listo (N)" is the dark primary SumaButton on the black camera panel: almost invisible, and with 0 photos the disabled grey is lost. ui/screens/items/MenuCameraScreen.kt:195-205. Fix: white/brand-filled button on the camera panel. small.
3. Review button says "Agregar 8 artículos" in a restaurant (menu_review_add_btn); everything else on the page says platos. values-es/pt strings `menu_review_add_btn`. Fix: "Agregar 8 platos" / "Adicionar 8 pratos". small.
4. The Support strip sits under the camera viewfinder (menu_camera is LEVEL_2_PLUS), taking 56 dp from the camera page. reports/ReportLevels.kt:47. Fix: make menu_camera NEVER (like the other capture/keypad screens). small.

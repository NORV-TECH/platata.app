# R5 "Comer aquí, llevar o domicilio" — build notes

## 1. Built
- Owner phone: Opciones de servicio page — switch on Mesas and type the number, switch on Para llevar, switch on Domicilio and give it a delivery charge with the pencil; then Home, add a dish, Cobrar, the dining gate "¿Cómo sale este pedido?", pick Domicilio and the pay screen shows the dish + charge as the total. 10 taps, 6 beats.
- Device: owner phone only.
- Differs by country: money and charge (CO $ 3.000, MX $ 30, BR R$ 3,00, US $3.00…), the pay-screen setup link (CO "Configurar QR o tarjeta", BR "Configurar Pix", US/CA "+ Tarjeta · Configurar", others "Configurar QR"), keypad 000 vs decimal.

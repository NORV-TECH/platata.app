# S4 — Cobrar con QR (restaurant)

Code: /home/claude/suma-plus-android @ d1b7698d (sim/usa-canada). All paths below are under `app/src/main/java/com/example/`.

## 1. What it does, who, where
The customer pays by scanning the shop's own bank QR code with their bank app. Platata never sees the money: the cashier checks the bank and taps "Pagó". In Brazil the QR shown already carries the sale total (Pix).
- Setup: OWNER only, on the owner's phone ("Tu teléfono"), Payment methods.
- Charging: any device that sells — owner phone, counter tablet (MamaPos), waiter phone (Mini POS) — all use the same pay screen `CheckoutSheetBody` (MamaPosScreen.kt:1416, MiniPosSellScreen.kt:503). The tablet shows the QR in its own pane (`onQrPayment`).
- Staff devices get the QR picture through the synced reference (choke point 15, QR-2), so the owner sets it up once.

## 2. Setup before the demo
- Factory: QR method seeded INACTIVE, no picture (data/repo/SeedStore.kt:97, PaymentConfigStore.kt:457). So a new restaurant shows NO QR button at checkout.
- Owner must: Mi negocio (bottom tab) > Ajustes > "Métodos de pago" > switch "Pago QR" ON > read and tick the warning page > "Entendido, activar pagos QR" > "Fotografiar código QR" or "Subir código QR" > toast "Código QR guardado."
- Optional (accordion "Código QR y nota del cajero"): "Foto de recibo obligatoria" switch (hides "Pagó · Sin foto"), cashier note field (see problem 1: never shown).
- Brazil: the picture must be the bank's REUSABLE Pix QR (Pix > Receber > QR Code). A non-Pix or one-time Pix picture is refused, nothing saved.
- Have a real bank QR photo ready (the app has no sample QR on purpose, C-07).

## 3. Country differences
| Country | QR at checkout | Amount in QR |
|---|---|---|
| CO MX PE CL EC AR PA | Yes (after setup) | No — static bank QR, customer types the amount |
| BR | Yes | YES — Pix BR Code rewritten with the sale total (ui/pos/PixCheckoutPayload.kt, utils/PixBrCode) |
| US, CA | NO. QR row hidden in Payment methods (PaymentMethodsScreen.kt:287-293) and never drawn at checkout (sync/PayWaysVisibility.kt:55 `!stripeCountry`) | — |
(The old notes said US/CA have a bank-QR photo: wrong, verified.)

Money format on the QR screen = `formatCurrency(grandTotal)`: CO `$ 21.600` (0 decimals, nbsp), MX `$1,234.50`, PE `S/ 1,234.50`, CL `$21.600`, EC `$1.234,50`, AR `$ 1.234,50`, PA `USD1,234.50`, BR `R$ 1.234,50`. QR is never cash-rounded (CashRounding.kt:7).

Owner set-up link at checkout (no QR set up yet, owner only): CO "Configurar QR o tarjeta" (QR_AND_CARD); MX PE CL EC AR PA BR "Configurar QR"; US/CA none.

## 5. Demo path (owner phone, Colombia, ≤10 taps)
1. Mi negocio > Ajustes > Métodos de pago (3 taps). 2. Switch "Pago QR" → warning page. 3. Tick the box. 4. "Entendido, activar pagos QR". 5. "Subir código QR" → pick bank QR image → toast "Código QR guardado." 6. Back to Caja, add dishes, "Cobrar $X". 7. Tap "QR" → big total + bank QR. 8. "Pagó · Sin foto" → "¡Venta completada!".
Brazil variant: same, with a reusable Pix QR; at step 7 the QR already holds R$ total — say "o cliente não digita o valor".
Waiter phone / tablet: same QR button appears automatically once the owner set it up (the picture travels in the synced reference).

## 6. UX/UI problems (verified in code)
1. Dead setting: owner can type a "Nota para el cajero" (PaymentMethodsScreen accordion, `qr_message_hint`) but the overlay never shows it — the block is `if (false)` at QrPaymentOverlay.kt:211. Fix: show it under the hint, or remove the field and rename the row "Código QR".
2. Brazil says "QR" everywhere, never "Pix" (`pay_qr_short` = "QR", `payment_method_qr` pt = "Pagamento QR"). Brazilians look for "Pix". Fix: in BRL show "Pix" on the pay button and the method name.
3. Wording mismatch for the same label: QR overlay "TOTAL A COBRAR" vs Stripe sheet "Monto a cobrar" (`stripe_total_label`) vs Wompi "Total a cobrar". Fix: one string.
4. QR overlay back arrow has no content description (QrPaymentOverlay.kt:122-128). Fix: `common_back`.
5. pt: settings hub row says "Formas de pagamento" (`settings_hub_payment_methods`) but the page title says "Métodos de pagamento" (`pay_methods_title`). Fix: one term.
6. Possible: the default note text `qr_payment_default_message` "No olvides tomar una foto del recibo." contradicts the warning page ("No confíes en la pantalla del cliente…") — a photo of the customer's screen is not proof. Fix: reword to "Revisa tu banco antes de entregar".
7. Possible: before setup the owner sees no QR button at all, only a small grey underlined link under the pay row ("Configurar QR") that is gone forever after "No, gracias" (PayWaysVisibility.kt:62). For a demo/new owner the feature is hard to discover. Fix: keep a way back (Payment methods still works) — or mention it in onboarding.
8. Dead code: `BankQrSetup` (inline setup from checkout) and the muted "+ QR · Configurar" button (CheckoutPayBlock.kt:347-368) can no longer be reached because `showQr` requires a saved photo. Not user-visible; just do not demo it.

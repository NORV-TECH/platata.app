# S19 — Otro celular o tableta: "Conexiones" (as seen by a RESTAURANT)

Code: commit d1b7698d. Paths under `app/src/main/java/com/example/`.

## 1. What it does, who, where
- Each device that SELLS (counter tablet = "caja", employee phone) uses a **connection**. Owner's phone = connection 1 (included); the **first** extra device = connection 2 (included); every later one is a **paid connection** (one yearly Google Play subscription in 8 steps). Kitchen screens and printers never use one.
- Cap: **10 connections per business** (owner phone + 9 devices) in every country (`sync/ConnectionAllocator.kt` `MAX_DEVICES = 9`, `MAX_CONNECTIONS_TOTAL = 10`; Play ladder max 8 paid).
- US and Canada pay nothing (`sync/Entitlement.kt:22` `FREE_COUNTRIES = US, CA`).
- Owner's phone only: Settings › "Tu negocio" › "Conexiones" (route `registers_and_printers`, `ui/navigation/RegisterNavGraph.kt:107-115`). Other roles are sent back.
- A device beyond the paid pool is listed "Sin conectar" and freezes on its side ("Fuera de servicio") — nothing is deleted.

## 2. Setup / defaults
- New business: 0 devices → hero "1 de 2" with "Tienes 2 conexiones incluidas…".
- **Prices come only from Google Play** (`billing/PlayConnectionsGateway`); while Billing / the 8 products are not live, the app shows counts and the word "De pago" with **no money** (`ConnectionParts.kt:86, 149`). Planned price in the code notes: 120 USD a year per connection (shown as Play's yearly price ÷ 12 per month, in local currency). For a demo with prices, Play products must exist, or the debug override (long-press the title of "Mi cuenta", debug build only) only changes the count.
- Nudge "Conecta tu segunda caja" shows to every owner with zero devices (`sync/ConnectNudgeRule.kt`), "Ahora no" hides it 7 days.

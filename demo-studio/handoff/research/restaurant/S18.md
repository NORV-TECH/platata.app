# S18 — Combos (as seen by a RESTAURANT)

Code: commit d1b7698d. Paths under `app/src/main/java/com/example/`.

## 1. What it does, who, where
- Owner builds a combo from **categories**: slot 1 "Producto principal" (e.g. Hamburguesas) + up to 2 "Acompañante" slots (e.g. Bebidas, Papas), each "any item of the category" or "only these items", with an optional **% discount** (no fixed combo price).
- At the till, tapping a dish that belongs to a combo adds the plain dish and shows a **green bar** "Hamburguesa está en un combo · Hacerlo combo" for 5 s. Tapping it asks for the side(s) and, if there is a discount, takes the % off the group.
- Build: owner's phone ("Tu menú" › COMBOS). Use: all three tills — owner phone (`HomeScreen.kt:908,1112`), counter tablet (`MamaPosScreen.kt:835`), waiter phone (`MiniPosSellScreen.kt:217`). Rules: `RestaurantRules.kt:39-40` (`combosEngineRuns`, `combosManagementUiShown` = true).

## 2. Setup / defaults
- No combo seeded. Needs categories with dishes (e.g. "Hamburguesas", "Bebidas").
- Discount defaults: switch OFF; when ON, "10" pre-filled; chips 5 / 10 / 15 %.

## 3. Country differences
- Same everywhere (restaurant). Only money format of prices/discount lines (CO "$ 8.000", MX "$80.00", BR "R$ 8,00", US "$8.00" — see S16 §3). Note: test screenshots show "$8.000" without space (JVM locale), the device prints "$ 8.000" per the money vector.

## 6. UX/UI problems
1. **Summary page title says "Combo creado" before anything is saved** (`NewComboScreen.kt:655`); Back loses it. Fix: "Revisa tu combo" and save on the button.
2. **Promise of "a un precio" but only a % exists** — empty line "Vende varios productos juntos a un precio." (`ComboListScreen.kt:68`, also `combo_tab_empty_line`), yet the builder only offers % off (`ComboSummaryParts.kt:91-105`). Restaurants usually price combos as a fixed amount ("Combo $ 25.000"). Fix: wording "con descuento" or add a fixed combo price.
3. **"Guardar" is a small top-bar text link** on the summary (`NewComboScreen.kt:658`) and "Siguiente" too (`:278`) — easy to miss; no bottom button. Fix: full-width bottom "Guardar".
4. **Can save a slot with "Solo artículos específicos" and nothing ticked** ("NINGUNO ELEGIDO" red badge) — no save guard (`ComboSaving.kt` drops nothing). Possible dead combo. Fix: block Next/Save.
5. **Discount ON with the box cleared saves a combo with no discount** (`ComboSaving.kt` `toIntOrNull` → null) — the list then shows no % badge silently. Fix: keep Save off while blank.
6. **Side picker shows no prices and no "sin combo" choice** (`ComboLinkedItemPickerSheet.kt:44-66`); rows ~40dp high (8dp padding). Fix: show price, 48dp rows, a "Cancelar" row.
7. **Badge "ACTIVO" always shown** — there is no way to pause a combo (`ComboRowCard.kt:92-104`). Fix: drop the badge or add a switch.
8. **Green bar only lives 5 s** and is the main discovery point; the cart-line link is the fallback (OK). The product tile shows no combo badge (`comboBadgeForProduct` param is passed but unused in `ProductLibraryView.kt:191`). Fix: small "Combo" badge on tiles.
9. Combo discount is merged into the generic "Descuento" line, no "Descuento combo" name in cart/receipt (`CartSheetContent.kt:910`), while the online page names it. Fix: name the line.

# S16 — Descuentos y cargos (as seen by a RESTAURANT)

Code: commit d1b7698d. Paths are under `app/src/main/java/com/example/`.

## 1. What it does, who, where
- The owner makes **rules** that change the bill by themselves: a **Descuento** (money off) or a **Recargo** (money added, e.g. service charge / propina). There is NO manual "give 10% to this table" button at the till: discounts apply automatically when a linked dish is in the sale (`CartSheetContent.kt:881-882` comment "No manual discount picker").
- A Recargo can be **"Preguntar al cobrar"** (ask at checkout). That one is a whole-bill % or amount; the till asks "Sí / No" before the pay screen.
- Setup: **owner's phone only** (Settings › Cobros › "Descuentos y cargos"). Rules sync to the tablet and waiter phones via the reference snapshot (`data/repo/BranchSnapshotStore.kt:335`).
- At the till: all three tills apply automatic rules. **The ask-at-checkout question appears ONLY on the owner's phone** (see problem 1).

## 2. Setup / factory defaults
- **Seeded rule (every business, every country):** "Tarifa de servicio" — 15 %, Recargo, ask-at-checkout, whole bill, **paused** (`data/repo/SeedStore.kt:289-309`). Name by `business_language`: es "Tarifa de servicio", pt "Taxa de serviço", en "Service charge". It is created by `checkAndSeedData`, which runs at **app start**, not at business creation (`ui/PosViewModel.kt:1761/1783/1803`) — so a brand-new demo business shows it only after the app is reopened (possible demo surprise).
- The owner must switch it on (switch on its card) to use it; it has no items to pick.
- Ideas offered to a restaurant (`data/business/RestaurantRules.kt:73-76`, numbers in `sync/DiscountIdeas.kt:22-30`):
  - Descuentos: "Promo nuevo cliente" 10 % (only with a client), "Descuento por Pago en Efectivo" 3 % (cash only), "Recompensa VIP de Lealtad" 5 %.
  - Recargos: "Recargo por Tarjeta de Crédito" 3 % (card only; shown only in card countries CO/US/CA), "Recargo nocturno" 15 %, "Propina para grupo grande" 10 %.
  - NOT offered to restaurants: "Liquidación / producto dañado". Strings exist for "Cargo por empaque ecológico" and "Recargo por entrega local" but **no idea uses them** (`DiscountIdeas.all` has 7 rows) — the old notes were wrong.

## 3. Country differences
- Same screens everywhere. Only differences:
  - "Recargo por Tarjeta de Crédito" idea only where a card processor exists (CO, US, CA) (`DiscountsManagementScreen.kt:96`, `DiscountIdeas.offered`).
  - Fixed-amount pill shows the currency symbol (`currencySymbol()`, `DiscountFormScreen.kt:135`); amount field label "Monto (ej. <example>)".
  - US/CA also have the separate Stripe tip screen (not this feature).
- Money format (from `functions/test-vectors/money-format.json`, same formatter): CO "$ 12.346", MX "$12,345.60", PE "S/ 1,234.50", CL "$12.346", EC "$12.345,60", AR "$ 12.345,60", PA "USD12,345.60" (no space — looks odd, possible issue), BR "R$ 12.345,60", US/CA "$12,345.60". Percent: `formatTaxRate` uses the country decimal mark ("12,5%" in CO).
- Fees are NOT taxed (tax base = discounted subtotal, `ui/pos/PosViewModelTypes.kt:838-843`).

## 5. Demo path (owner phone, CO restaurant, ~10 taps)
1. Panel › "Configuración" › "Descuentos y cargos".
2. On "Tarifa de servicio + 15%" tap the switch ON (or "+ Nuevo" › "Recargo" › name "Propina sugerida" › "%" › 10 › "Preguntar al cobrar" ON › "Guardar").
3. Back to Caja, tap 2 dishes, "Cobrar".
4. Pick "Mesa"/dining option → card "Tarifa de servicio 15%" → "Sí".
5. Pay screen / cart shows "Cargo: Tarifa de servicio $ …"; tap "Quitar" to show the waiter can remove it; "Restaurar".
6. Charge cash → receipt shows the fee line.
Optional discount: "+ Nuevo" › "Descuento" › "Combo almuerzo 10%" › "Elegir artículos" › tick a category › "Listo" › "Guardar"; ring that dish → "Descuento -$ …" appears by itself.
Hand-off: waiter phone / tablet will apply automatic rules but will NOT ask the "Sí/No" fee (problem 1).

## 6. UX/UI problems
1. **Ask-at-checkout fee is never asked on the counter tablet or the waiter phone** — gate is drawn only `if (!gateRequired && optionalFeeGate != null && !inlineCheckout)` (`CheckoutSheetBody.kt:1139`); tablet/waiter pass `inlineCustomerEditing = true` (`MamaPosScreen.kt:1438`, `MiniPosSellScreen.kt:503`), and an unanswered fee is simply not charged (`PosViewModelTypes.kt:756-760`). Fix: show the same Sí/No step in the tablet/waiter step machine.
2. **Seeded default is 15 %** (`SeedStore.kt:301`). Colombia's suggested service tip (propina voluntaria, Ley 1935/2018) is capped at 10 %; demoing 15 % in CO is wrong (possible legal). Fix: seed 10 % (or per country).
3. **Seeded fee appears only after an app restart** (`checkAndSeedData` runs at launch, not in creation) — possible. Fix: also run the seed in `initializeEmptyBusiness`.
4. **Wrong toast after saving a new rule**: "Los vínculos del ajuste 'X' fueron actualizados exitosamente!" (`DiscountsManagementScreen.kt:187`), also missing "¡". Fix: a "Guardado" toast for new rules.
5. **Three names for one thing**: form "Recargo", cart "Cargo: …", seed "Tarifa de servicio", fallback "Cargo por Servicio", and the **dining upcharge line also says "Cargo por Servicio (Domicilio)"** (`home_dining_upcharge`, `CartSheetContent.kt:954`). A restaurant sees two different "Cargo por servicio"s. Fix: dining line "Recargo (Domicilio)"; one word for charges.
6. **Removing a fee asks "¿Aplicar descuento? / Confirma para aplicar un descuento"** in the fingerprint prompt (`CartSheetContent.kt:883-884, 936`). Fix: "¿Quitar cargo?".
7. **Discount line has no name** ("Descuento" only, `CartSheetContent.kt:910-916`) while fees are named — cashier can't tell which promo applied. Fix: one line per discount name (like fees).
8. **No manual discount at the till** (by design comment `CartSheetContent.kt:881`); a restaurant can't comp 10% for a complaint except "Cortesía"/whole comp. Product gap — confirm with Monti.
9. Gate card shows only "Tarifa de servicio  15%" with no amount or explanation (`CheckoutSheetBody.kt:1328-1334`); the cashier can't read the money aloud. Fix: add "= $ 6.000".
10. Possible: Panama money prints "USD12,345.60" (no space) in the money vector (`money-format.json` PA).

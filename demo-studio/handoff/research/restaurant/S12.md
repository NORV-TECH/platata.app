# S12 — Enviar al contador (Send to the accountant), as seen by a RESTAURANT

Source: /home/claude/suma-plus-android @ d1b7698d (read only). Paths under `app/src/main/java/com/example/`.

## 1. What it does, who, where
- One page with the period's numbers an accountant asks for (total sales, taxes collected, cash, card/electronic, card fees, paid to suppliers, supplier invoices, paid to employees), sent as plain text to WhatsApp or as a one-page PDF. No tax is calculated.
- **Owner's phone only.** The only door is the black "Enviar al contador" button pinned at the bottom of Reports (S11). A staff member with "Ver reportes" never sees the button, and the route sends any non-owner back (`ui/navigation/ReportsNavGraph.kt:38-53`).
- The dates are the dates of the Reports chip that was selected (Hoy, Este mes, Personalizado…). Back returns to Reports with the same chip.

## 2. Setup before the demo
- Sales in the period (cash + at least one non-cash). Pick a whole month chip ("Mes pasado") so the employee-pay figure counts (pay counts only months fully inside the range, `ui/pos/EmployeePayActions.kt:294-295`).
- Optional, to fill every row: a few supplier invoices scanned (panel row "Facturas"), employee pay tracking on with at least one confirmed month (S15), a tax set up (S17) so "Impuestos cobrados" is not $0.
- To show the orange "months not recorded" link: tracking on + an employee + a finished month with no pay answer.
- WhatsApp installed on the demo phone (else the Android share sheet opens).

## 3. Country differences
- Same rows and words everywhere. Money format per country as in S11 (CO `$ 1.234`, BR `R$ 1.234,50`, US `$1,234.50`, PA `USD1,234.50`…).
- **Card fees row** ("Comisiones de tarjeta (las cobra el procesador)") appears only when the period had a card charge: CO (Wompi), US/CA (Stripe). Stripe fees are filled from the server when the page opens; **Wompi fees are never known**, so in Colombia the row is always `$ 0` with the orange line "N ventas con tarjeta aún sin dato de comisión — no están sumadas arriba" (`EmployeePayActions.kt:330-333`, CLAUDE.md "Wompi … unreconciled").
- "Ventas fiado (a crédito)" is NOT shown for a restaurant (tabs are retail/pharmacy only), unless old fiado sales exist (`ShareAccountantScreen.kt:162`).
- US/CA: card tips are inside "Ventas totales" and "Ventas con tarjeta y electrónicas" (see problem A6).
- Dates use the app language: "1 octubre – 19 octubre 2026" (es, no "de") / "1 outubro – 19 outubro 2026" / "1 October – 19 October 2026" via `DateTimeFormatter "d MMMM yyyy"` (`EmployeePayActions.kt:349-355`).

## 5. Demo path (owner phone, 6 taps)
1. Flag → "Reportes".
2. Tap chip **Mes pasado**.
3. Tap black **Enviar al contador** → page "Compartir con tu contador · 1 septiembre – 30 septiembre 2026" with the 7–8 rows.
4. Long-press "Ventas totales" → toast "Copiado".
5. Tap **Enviar a mi contador** → WhatsApp opens with the text; pick the accountant's chat → send.
6. Back → tap **Compartir PDF** → share sheet → WhatsApp / Gmail.
(Optional) tap the orange "2 meses no registrados (ago, sep)" → Team › Meses anteriores → Back returns to the sheet.

## 6. UX/UI problems (verified in code)
- **A1 — "1 meses / 1 facturas" plural bugs.** `accountant_gap_months`, `accountant_msg_months`, `accountant_msg_suppliers(_clean)` and `accountant_gap_card_fees_unknown` are plain strings with `%1$d` (`res/values*/strings.xml`), so 1 reads "1 meses no registrados", "(1 facturas)", "1 ventas con tarjeta…". Fix: make them `<plurals>`.
- **A2 — Three names for one action, two names for one number.** Reports button "Enviar al contador" → page "Compartir con tu contador" → button "Enviar a mi contador"; and Reports says "Ventas netas" for the exact figure this page calls "Ventas totales" (`ReportsScreen.kt:233`, `EmployeePayActions.kt:338`). On a video the owner sees two labels for the same $. Fix: one name ("Ventas netas") on both, one verb.
- **A3 — "Pagado a empleados $ 0" on short ranges.** Pay only counts months fully inside the range (`EmployeePayActions.kt:294-295`), so "Hoy" or "Esta semana" always says $ 0 even if the owner pays staff. The accountant reads it as "paid nothing". Fix: show "—  (solo por meses completos)" when no full month is inside.
- **A4 — Colombia: card fee row is always $ 0 with a permanent warning** (Wompi fees never filled). Fix: hide the row for Wompi, or say "Wompi descuenta sus comisiones en su liquidación".
- **A5 — Silent fallbacks.** If WhatsApp and the share sheet fail, or the PDF fails, the text is copied to the clipboard with no message (`ShareAccountantScreen.kt:383, 407-408`). Fix: show "Copiado — pégalo donde quieras".
- **A6 — US/CA tips counted as sales** (same root as S11 P10): "Ventas totales" and "Ventas con tarjeta" include Stripe tips (`ShareAccountantScreen.kt:108-115` sums `totalAmount`). Fix: subtract `tipAmount` and add a "Propinas" row.
- **A7 — PDF is a raw text dump**: no wrapping (a long business name or note runs off the 595-pt page), one page only, fixed name `accountant.pdf` (`:390-397`). Fix: wrap lines, add title/date in the file name ("Platata-La-Cazuela-2026-09.pdf").
- **A8 — Long-press copy is invisible.** Rows look tappable (ripple on tap, `combinedClickable(onClick = {})` :317) but a tap does nothing and nothing says "mantén presionado para copiar". Fix: remove the tap ripple and add a one-line hint, or a small copy icon.
- **A9 — "Card and electronic" includes "Cortesía" ($0) and QR.** Harmless for money but the label hides QR/transfer; an accountant may want QR apart. Possible improvement, not a bug.
- **A10 — Gendered/odd copy:** intro "él se encarga del resto" / "he'll take care of the rest" assumes a male accountant; en uses "not recorded" on screen but "not tracked" in the sent text (`accountant_gap_months` vs `accountant_msg_months`). Fix: "tu contador se encarga del resto"; one word.

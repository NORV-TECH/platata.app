# S21 — Funciona sin internet — as seen by a RESTAURANT

Code: /home/claude/suma-plus-android @ d1b7698d. Paths under `app/src/main/java/com/example/`.

There is NO "offline mode" screen. Offline is how the app is built: every device keeps its own database on the device. What changes with no internet is (a) what can travel between devices and (b) three small signals: the card "Sin conexión" button, the held-sales strip on tablets/waiter phones, and the owner's "faltan" lines.

## 1. What works offline, device by device (precise)

Two kinds of "no internet" matter for a restaurant: **router on but internet down** (Wi-Fi between devices still works) and **no Wi-Fi at all**.

| Device | With no internet | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Tu teléfono** (owner, book of record) | Sells, cash, open/park tickets, tables, add-ons, discounts, split payment, receipts on a Bluetooth/Wi-Fi printer, opens/closes the register, Reports of its own data, menu edits, refunds of cash sales. | Local Room database (`data/PlatataRepository.kt`). Its cloud writes (sales, menu "reference") queue and go out later. |
| **Pantalla de cocina** (KDS) | Keeps getting tickets **rung on the owner's phone** as long as both are on the same Wi-Fi (router up, internet down is fine). Bump/recall work. | KDS is LAN-only, never cloud (`ui/app/DeviceSyncEffects.kt:51-59`; LAN messages `sync/LanSync.kt:28-49`: catalog, kitchen feed, bump, unbump). With NO Wi-Fi it gets nothing. |
| **Tablet de mostrador** (MamaPos) | Keeps selling and taking cash; its sales are saved on the tablet and shown as held ("Sin sincronizar: 3 ventas guardadas en esta caja"). | Its sales go to the owner through the CLOUD (Firestore `synced_orders`, `ui/pos/OrderSyncController.kt`), not over Wi-Fi. So offline they do **not** reach the owner's totals or the kitchen until internet is back. |
| **Teléfono de empleado** (waiter, Mini POS) | Same as the tablet: sells/saves, held strip shows. | Cloud only. Tickets reach the kitchen only via owner phone (already flagged #6) — so offline, a waiter's order does NOT reach the kitchen. |

### What syncs later, by itself (no button)
- Tablet/waiter sales → cloud → owner phone → owner's reports. The strip disappears the moment the count is 0 (`sync/HeldSalesNotice.kt:30`).
- Owner's menu/price edits → tablets/waiter phones (reference push).
- Register open/close shared state, cash in/out movements (`CashMovementSync`: saved locally first, sent when the gate is open).
- Google Drive backup: waits for a network (every 6 h; manual "Respaldar ahora" waits too, see S22).
- "Reportar un problema" reports: kept in an outbox, sent later ("Guardado, lo enviaremos cuando tengas conexión").

### What does NOT work offline (honest list)
- **Card**: Colombia (Wompi, typed card) and US/Canada (Stripe, tap on phone). The Tarjeta button turns grey "Sin conexión" and only explains (`sync/CardCheckoutDecision.kt:81`). Card refunds through Stripe also fail ("No pudimos comunicarnos con Stripe…").
- **Online store** orders and its notifications (cloud).
- **Waiter-phone and tablet orders reaching the kitchen** and the owner's totals (cloud path).
- Push notifications, Drive backup/restore, Google sign-in, creating a new business, restoring, pairing a device through the cloud, AI menu import / invoice reading, WhatsApp delivery (WhatsApp opens; it sends when online).
- A brand-new tablet/waiter phone that never got online: may show "Casi listo — Conéctate a internet una vez para terminar de configurar este dispositivo. Tus artículos aparecerán aquí automáticamente." (`sync/CatalogEmptyState.kt`, `catalog_awaiting_connection_*`).
- The owner can't see that an OFFLINE register is holding sales: its heartbeat also goes through the cloud, so it shows "sin reporte reciente", not "pendiente" (fail-open by design, `ui/screens/cash/YourRegistersScreen.kt`).

QR payments still work offline on the shop side (the saved QR picture is shown from the phone; in Brazil the Pix code with the amount is built on the phone, `ui/pos/PixCheckoutPayload`). The customer's phone needs internet.

## 2. Setup before the demo
- Owner phone with a restaurant, a kitchen screen paired on the same Wi-Fi, a counter tablet and/or waiter phone paired (see their features).
- For the card nudge: a card set up (CO Wompi or US/CA Stripe). Other countries never show a card button.
- To film: turn off the router's WAN cable (keep Wi-Fi on), or airplane mode + Wi-Fi on a router with no internet.

## 3. Country differences
- Card nudge only in CO, US, CA (the only card countries). Everywhere else the pay screen has no card button at all, so nothing changes offline.
- BR: Pix QR with amount works offline (built on the phone).
- Held amount on the owner's registers page uses the country format: CO "$ 75.800 pendientes", MX "$1,250.00", PE "S/ 25.00", BR "R$ 62,50 pendentes", US "$18.65 waiting".

## 5. Demo path (≤10 taps, 3 devices)
1. Router: unplug the internet cable (Wi-Fi stays). Owner phone: ring "2 Bandeja paisa" for Mesa 4 → Guardar / send to kitchen → **kitchen screen shows the ticket** (Wi-Fi only).
2. Owner phone: Cobrar → pay block shows grey "Tarjeta · Sin conexión" (CO/US/CA) → tap → toast "Los pagos con tarjeta necesitan conexión a internet" → tap Efectivo → "Exacto" → sale done, receipt prints.
3. Counter tablet: sell a coffee in cash → strip "Sin sincronizar: 1 venta guardada en esta caja · Se enviarán solas…".
4. Plug the internet back → strip disappears by itself; owner phone Reports now include the tablet's sale.
Honest caption for step 3: the tablet's sale reaches the owner (and kitchen) only after internet returns.

## 6. UX/UI problems (verified)
1. The strip promises "Se enviarán solas al teléfono del dueño", but they go to the cloud; if the OWNER's phone is the one offline they still don't arrive. `held_sales_strip_body`. Possible fix: "Se enviarán solas cuando vuelva internet."
2. A waiter-phone/tablet order made offline never reaches the kitchen screen until internet returns, and nothing on the waiter phone says "la cocina aún no lo tiene". `DeviceSyncEffects.kt:51-59` + held strip. Fix: add "La cocina lo recibe cuando vuelva internet" when held count > 0 on a restaurant (relates to flag #6, but this is the offline wording gap).
3. Owner sees nothing for an offline register: totals are silently short; the "Faltan N" line needs the register's heartbeat, which also needs internet. `ReportsScreen.kt:155,246`, `RegisterHeartbeatRule`. Fix: in Reports, show "1 caja sin reporte reciente" when a register is UNKNOWN.
4. The owner's phone has no offline signal at all (only the card button changes); backup "Respaldar ahora" spins forever offline (S22 #5). Possible fix: one small "Sin internet — todo se guarda en el teléfono" line in the panel.
5. "Sin sincronizar" is technical and starts negative; fix: "3 ventas guardadas en esta caja, se enviarán solas".

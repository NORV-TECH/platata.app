# S1 — Crear el negocio (as a RESTAURANT)

Source: /home/claude/suma-plus-android, commit d1b7698 (read only). Paths are under `app/src/main/java/com/example/`.

## 1. What it does, who, which device
First run on the OWNER's phone ("Tu teléfono"): welcome → country → business type → name → Google sign-in → "Configurando tu negocio…" → "¡Tu negocio está listo!" → lands on the register's **Catálogo** tab with an empty menu. Only the owner's phone creates a business; tablets, kitchen screens and waiter phones go through "Ya uso Platata" (join/connect) instead.

## 2. Setup before the demo
- Fresh install (or clear app data). A Google account on the phone (else "No hay una cuenta de Google en este dispositivo…").
- Country is pre-picked from SIM → network → phone locale (`CountryPickerShared.kt:104 detectCountry`). To film another country, change it on the country step.
- What creation seeds (`data/repo/SeedStore.kt:48-136`): roles Propietario/Cajero/Mesero/Cocina; payment methods Cash (ON), QR (off), Fiado (off), Card (off), Comp/"Cortesía" (ON, `PaymentConfigStore.kt:462`); dining options For Here / To Go / Delivery / Tables all **off**; owner employee with an auto PIN; Security ON. **No taxes, no dishes, no register shift open.**
- After landing, the Home shows up to three bars (see problem 6): backup warning, "Caja cerrada · Abrir", "Conecta tu segunda caja". Tap "Ahora no" on the nudge before filming (snoozes 7 days).

## 3. Country differences
- Currency = country (`MONEDA_QUE_SE_USA` overrides: PA→USD). EC→USD. Money format follows the COUNTRY, not app language (`CurrencyUtils.kt:189`). Golden examples (functions/test-vectors/money-format.json):
  CO `$ 1.234` (0 decimals) · MX `$1,234.50` · PE `S/ 1,234.50` · CL `$1.234` · EC `$1.234,50` · AR `$ 1.234,50` · PA `USD1,234.50` (JDK vector; possible ugly on device, verify) · BR `R$ 1.234,50` · US `$1,234.50` · CA `$1,234.50`.
- Type tiles: Restaurante / Tienda / Servicios everywhere; **Edificios only in CO** (`BusinessTypeOptions.kt:395`). CO shop tile says "droguerías".
- Restaurant pitch line 3 (`BusinessTypeOptions.kt:336 lineaDePago`):
  - CO, US, CA: "Efectivo, QR y tarjeta · …" | "Dinheiro, QR e cartão" | "Cash, QR and card".
  - others: "Efectivo y QR · Gratis, para siempre." | "Dinheiro e QR · Grátis, para sempre." | "Cash and QR · Free, forever."
  - US/CA price: "Gratis. Solo pagas una comisión en pagos con tarjeta." | "Grátis. Você só paga uma comissão nos pagamentos com cartão." | "Free. You only pay a fee on card payments."
- Language of the UI follows the phone (es / pt-BR / en).

## 6. UX problems (verified)
1. Country subtitle promises taxes, none are set: "Así ponemos tu moneda y tus impuestos" (`country_step_subtitle`, used `BusinessStartScreen.kt:1001`); no tax is created per country (taxes only via `TaxDiningActions.kt:25`, by hand). Fix: "Así ponemos tu moneda." 
2. US/CA restaurant pitch says "Efectivo, QR y tarjeta" but QR is never offered in US/CA at checkout (`PayWaysVisibility.kt` showQr = !stripeCountry). Source `BusinessTypeOptions.kt:340`. Fix: "Cash and card" for US/CA.
3. Sign-in says "Para que tu negocio nunca se pierda / queda guardado en tu cuenta de Google" (`GoogleAuthScreen.kt:376-390`), then the first Home shows the orange "Esta tienda no tiene respaldo · Conectar" bar (Drive is no longer asked at sign-in, `GoogleAuthScreen.kt:160-165`; bar `HomeScreen.kt:387-396`). Contradiction on first impression. Fix: soften the sign-in promise or connect Drive in the same step.
4. Backup bar says "Esta tienda / Esta loja / This shop" for a restaurant (`backup_off_banner_title`). Fix: "Este negocio no tiene respaldo".
5. "Configurando…" line 3 "Preparando tu caja" never ticks (`SettingUpScreen.kt:127`) and the register is NOT opened at the end — owner lands on a closed register. Fix: tick it, or word it "Tu caja está lista para abrir".
6. First Home stacks three bars above the menu: backup warning (top), "Caja cerrada · Abrir" (amber), "Conecta tu segunda caja · Conectar · Ahora no" (`HomeScreen.kt:648-659`). Busy first screen. Fix: show only the closed-register bar on day 1.
7. Panama money may print "USD1,234.50" (golden vector for PA). Possible; verify on a device. Fix: force "$" or "B/." display for PA.

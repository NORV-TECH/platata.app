# S17 — Impuestos (as seen by a RESTAURANT)

Code: commit d1b7698d. Paths under `app/src/main/java/com/example/`.

## 1. What it does, who, where
- Owner types tax rates by hand (name + %) and says whether the tax is **already in the prices** (included) or **added on top**. Owner's phone only (Settings › Cobros › "Impuestos"). Rates ride the reference snapshot to tablets/waiter phones.
- At the till every item marked "Cobrar impuesto sobre este artículo" (default ON) is taxed. Fees/service charge are not taxed.

## 2. Setup / defaults
- **No tax is seeded in any country** (no `TaxRate(` in seeds; only `CatalogSnapshotStore.kt:414` copying). Empty list until the owner adds one.
- **No country presets**: no IVA 19 %, no **impoconsumo/INC 8 %** (searched "impoconsumo", "INC", "IGV", "ITBMS": not found in code or strings).
- A new tax is saved **ON** (`TaxDiningActions.addTaxRate`, S9-B18).
- Item form row "Cobrar impuesto sobre este artículo" appears only when at least one tax exists (`sync/ShopSwitches.showTaxCheckbox`).

## 3. Country differences
- Form default answer (`sync/TaxPriceDefault.kt`): **US, CA → "No, se suma al precio"** (added on top); every other country → "Sí, ya está incluido".
- US/CA tax rounding per line HALF_UP at the cent (`data/TaxMath.kt:74,103`); others unchanged.
- US/CA online orders show "más impuesto al recoger" (`sync/OnlineOrder.kt` `taxAddedAtPickup`).
- Receipt inclusive line text "IVA incluido" (es) / "Imposto incluído" (pt) / "Tax included" (en) (`receipt_tax_included`) — same word "IVA" in every Spanish country (see problems).
- Typical rates the owner would type (not in app): CO restaurant impoconsumo 8 % (included); MX IVA 16 %; PE IGV 18 % (+ often 10 % restaurant ICBPER no); CL IVA 19 %; EC IVA 15 %; AR IVA 21 %; PA ITBMS 7 %; BR none at the till typically; US/CA local sales tax added on top.
- Money examples: see S16 §3 (CO "$ 1.234", MX "$1,234.00", BR "R$ 1.234,00" …). Percent uses country decimal mark: CO "8%", "12,5%".

## 5. Demo path (CO restaurant, owner phone, 8 taps)
1. Panel › "Configuración" › "Impuestos" › "+ Nuevo".
2. Name "Impoconsumo", "8", leave "Sí, ya está incluido" (CO default) › "Guardar" → snackbar, card "Impoconsumo ACTIVO · 8% · incluido en los precios".
3. Back to Caja, ring a $ 32.000 dish › "Cobrar" › open "Desglose": "Impoconsumo · 8% · Incluido $ 2.370", total still $ 32.000.
4. Charge cash › receipt shows "IVA incluido: $ 2.370" (see problem 1).
US variant: name "Sales tax", 8.875, default "No, add it on top" → total grows.

## 6. UX/UI problems
1. **Receipt says "IVA incluido" for any included tax** (`receipt_tax_included`, `ui/PosViewModel.kt:4725-4726`) — wrong for a Colombian restaurant (impoconsumo is not IVA), Peru (IGV), Panama (ITBMS). Fix: print "<tax name> incluido" from the rate name.
2. **Receipt additive line is the generic "Impuesto"** (`ReceiptActions.kt:108`), not the rate's name/percent. Fix: one line per rate name.
3. **Comma in the percentage fails**: field has no filter, `toDoubleOrNull("8,5")` → "Por favor ingresa un porcentaje válido." (`TaxesManagementScreen.kt:130-139, 190-193`). Spanish/Portuguese users type commas. Fix: reuse `filterPercentInput` (accepts "," and caps 100) like the discount form.
4. **No upper cap** on % (any ≥0 accepted, `:193`). Fix: cap at 100.
5. **Cannot edit a tax** — only toggle/delete (`:289-307`). Fix: tap card to edit.
6. **Cart line shows included tax like a charge**: "Impuesto (Impoconsumo) $ 2.370" with no "incluido" word (`CartSheetContent.kt:946-948`), unlike the Desglose which says "Incluido". Fix: add "· incluido" when the rate is not additive.
7. No country preset (no impoconsumo/IVA suggestion) — owner must know the law; possible improvement: one-tap "Impoconsumo 8%" idea in CO.
8. Empty-state text "Toca + para agregar uno." while the button reads "+ Nuevo" (`:229`) — fine-ish; possible fix "Toca + Nuevo".

Code: /home/claude/suma-plus-android @ d1b7698d. Paths under `app/src/main/java/com/example/`.

## 1. What it does, who, which device
- One owner phone can hold up to **10 businesses** (each a separate database: own menu, sales, team, country/currency) and up to **5 branches per business** (`ui/pos/PosViewModelTypes.kt:349-350`).
- A branch is a NEW business that starts with a copy of the parent's menu & prices (and optionally team, clients, receipt+security settings). Sales and open tickets never carry over. It gets its own cloud key, its own registers and its own devices (choke 6/10 in CLAUDE.md).
- Switching = pick another row; the app opens that business and lands on its register.
- **Owner's phone only.** Counter tablet and kitchen screen have no switcher by rule (dedicated hardware). Waiter phone has no business list.

## 2. Setup before the demo
- Owner signed in with a real Google account (creating needs it: `ui/app/PlatataApp.kt:258-274`; restoring too: `HomeScreen.kt:1858-1868`).
- One restaurant already created (e.g. "La Fonda"). For a richer list, create a second business and one branch beforehand.
- Each business/branch needs its own connections for devices (US/CA free). A branch starts with NO registers or paired devices.

## 3. Country differences
- Same screens everywhere. A new business runs the normal setup (country, type, name), so one owner can have a Colombian restaurant and a Peruvian one side by side, each with its own money format (CO "$ 75.800", PE "S/ 25.00", BR "R$ 62,50", US "$18.65").
- A branch inherits the parent's country and currency (`createBranchFromWorkspace`, choke 6) — you can't make a branch in another country.
- No money appears on these screens. The active row shows the register pill "Abierta"/"Cerrada" (pt "Aberto"/"Fechado", en "Open"/"Closed").

## 6. UX/UI problems (verified)
1. "Tus otros negocios · 1 de 10" counts ALL businesses, including the one shown above in the branches section, so with one restaurant it says "otros … 1 de 10" over an empty list. `ContextSwitcher.kt:333-338`. Fix: put "1 de 10 negocios" on its own line or count only the rows below.
2. A new branch gets Security OFF unless "Ajustes de recibo y dispositivo" is switched on (default off), with no way to turn it back on (see S23 #7). `BranchSetupScreen.kt:75`, `SeedStore.kt:199`. Fix: default the switch on, or always seed Security ON.
3. "Celulares del equipo" in the partial-branch dialog is not the app's name for the place (the device is "Teléfono de empleado"). `branch_created_partial_needs_codes` (es). Fix: name the real screen/row.
4. The branch name is forced "Padre-Sufijo" with a hyphen and no space ("La Fonda-Centro"); the prefix can't be edited. `BranchSetupScreen.kt:71,256`. Possible fix: "La Fonda Centro" or allow a free name (rename works later).
5. Deleting a business can silently fail on a phone without fingerprint, and the panel closes before the prompt (see S23 #6). `HomeScreen.kt:394-396,508-524`.
6. Only the open business shows a register pill; other rows show nothing, so the owner can't see if the branch's till is open. `ContextSwitcher.kt:133-137,172-175` (known, flagged in code). Fix: later.
7. The close button is an X while every other list page uses a back arrow (G-08 rule). `ContextSwitcher.kt:195-197`. Fix: arrow.

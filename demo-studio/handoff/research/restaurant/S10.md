# S10 — Cerrar la caja (close register)

Source: commit d1b7698, read only. Paths under `app/src/main/java/com/example/`.

## 1. What it does, who, which device
End of day: count the cash in the drawer, compare with what should be there, close the shift for every device, see/share the "Caja cerrada" summary.
- Owner phone: Mi negocio → "Caja abierta" card → Register screen → "Cerrar caja". Sees expected cash, difference and summary.
- Waiter phone (Mini POS) with permission "Cerrar turno" (`canCloseShift`): "Cerrar caja · Contar el efectivo y cerrar el turno" row in its panel (`MoreScreen.kt:146-153`) → same Close screen but BLIND (no expected, no difference; summary = title + Listo).
- Counter tablet: no close door found in code.
- Closing writes a `shift_history` doc and deletes `shift/current`, so tablets/waiter phones lock to "Caja cerrada" (`ShiftController.kt:139`).

## 2. Setup before the demo
- Register open with a few cash sales (S2, S3), maybe a cash out (S9).
- Optional receipt printer → "Imprimir" on the summary. WhatsApp installed → "Enviar por WhatsApp" opens it (else share sheet, else clipboard).

## 3. Country differences
Only money format and the keypad (cents style for 2-decimal currencies, "000" for COP/CLP; see S2). Expected cash already includes cash rounding in CL/PE/CA (sales are stored at the rounded amount). Wording es "Cerrar caja / Faltante / Sobrante / Cuadrado", pt "Fechar caixa / Falta / Sobra / Fechado exato", en "Close register / Short / Over / Balanced".

## 6. UX problems (verified)
1. A restaurant can close the till with unpaid open tables (saved tickets) and gets no warning; the only guard is for tablets holding unsent sales (`CloseRegisterScreen.kt:150-161`, `sync/RegisterHeartbeat.kt:119`). Fix: "Tienes 3 mesas abiertas sin cobrar" warning before close.
2. Same figure, two names: "Esperado" on Close (`register_close_expected_label`, `CloseRegisterScreen.kt:375`) vs "Debe haber en la caja" on Register screen and summary. Fix: one label.
3. "Cerrado por / Abierta por" shows the English literal "Owner" on the owner's phone, also inside the WhatsApp text (`ShiftController.kt:90,147`). Fix: owner's name.
4. WhatsApp/print summary has no business name (`ShiftSummaryText.kt:47-79`), so an owner with two restaurants can't tell which till the message is about. Fix: add the business name under the title.
5. Gender mix on the summary: "Abierta …" then "Cerrado …" (visible in the screenshot); pt "Aberta em" with masculine "caixa". Fix: consistent wording.
6. Permission is called "Cerrar turno / Fechar turno / Can close shift" in Team (`TeamManagementScreen.kt:1217,1350`) but the action everywhere is "Cerrar caja". Fix: rename the permission "Cerrar la caja".
7. pt "Sobrante" on the Register screen vs "Sobra" on Close/summary (`register_status_over` vs `register_close_over`). Fix: "Sobra".

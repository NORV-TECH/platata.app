## 1. What it does, who, where
An "adición" (add-on, code name ModifierSet) is a question asked when a dish is sold: "Salsas" (pick several, some cost extra), "Término de cocción" (pick one), a free-text "Solicitudes especiales". The owner builds them from 11 ready ideas or from zero, chooses on which dishes they appear, and the cashier/waiter answers them in a chip sheet before the dish enters the sale. A choice can open a follow-up question ("Luego preguntar…"), and can be flagged to show in red on the kitchen screen.
- WHO builds: owner, on the owner's phone ("Tu teléfono"). WHO uses: anyone selling — owner phone, counter tablet ("Tablet de mostrador"), waiter phone ("Teléfono de empleado"): all three tills use the same sheet (`ui/screens/home/ProductSheetHost.kt:122-146`). The chosen add-ons print on the kitchen ticket / kitchen screen.
- "Opciones" (option sets = sizes/variants, `OptionsPickerPage.kt`) are NOT reachable for a restaurant: the dish form hides the sizes row (`RestaurantRules.kt:26`, `ItemsScreen.kt:1323`) and the options-management route has no door anywhere (`CatalogNavGraph.kt:290-300`, no navigate call). For a restaurant, "opciones" in practice = add-ons; sizes only arrive through the AI import (R1).

## 6. UX/UI problems found
1. Same idea, two different rules. From the ideas page the editor starts every new add-on as "Elegir VARIOS" (`ModifiersManagementScreen.kt:123`) and never reads the idea's one/many rule; for "Término de cocción" the one/many cards are hidden (line 631), so it is saved as pick-many: a waiter can tick "Poco hecho" AND "Bien cocido". "Nivel", "Tamaño", "Proteína", "Estilo de preparación", "Tipo de pan" also start pick-many. From the dish picker's Ideas tab the same idea is saved pick-one and not required (`AddOnIdeasTab.kt:97-107`), while the ideas page makes "Término" required (line 122). Fix: seed isRequired/allowMoreThanOne from the template in both paths.
2. "Término de cocción" and "Solicitudes especiales" created from the ideas page have no "¿En qué platos?" row (`ModifiersManagementScreen.kt:462`), so they end on no dish; reopening from the list shows the row (templateKey is null then). Fix: always show the row.
3. Ideas page is titled "Mis adiciones" and has no tabs; back from it leaves the whole section instead of returning to the list (`CategoryHubScreen.kt:177-185`). Fix: title "Ideas", draw the two tabs.
4. Every idea card wears "DESACTIVADO — toca para configurar", even "Tu propia adición", and all use the same 🍽️ emoji (`CategoryHubScreen.kt:209-235, 414-437`). Fix: drop the pill on ideas; use per-idea icons.
5. Two editors, two vocabularies: idea editor "¿Cuántos pueden elegir?" / "Elegir VARIOS" / "Elegir UNO", "Requerido" hidden under "Avanzado"; custom editor "Elegir VARIAS" / "Elegir UNA", "Requerido" visible (`ModifiersManagementScreen.kt:642-706` vs `1075-1116`). Fix: one editor, "¿Cuántas pueden elegir? Varias / Una".
6. An optional pick-one choice cannot be un-ticked at the till: tapping the chosen chip again does nothing (`ModifierSelectionLogic.kt:73-76`). A waiter's mis-tap on an optional "Término" stays. Fix: allow deselect when the group is not required.
7. Quantity −/+ are 34 dp (`ModifierSheetContent.kt:420-431`). Fix: 48 dp.
8. Spanish grammar/word issues: pill "Activo" for "adición" -> "Activa"; "se quitará de todos los artículos que lo usan" -> "la usan"; dishes row says "producto(s)" under a title "¿En qué platos?" (`mod_on_no_item_tap`, `mod_on_n_of_m_items`); idea "Nivel" does not say of what (-> "Nivel de picante"); Término words are Spain-style ("Poco hecho", "Medio crudo") not LatAm ("Azul", "Tres cuartos"); "Chico" is Mexican; dips "Ranch" / "Queso azul" are US-style. Fix: restaurant LatAm wording.
9. "Elegir adiciones" rows show only the add-on name, not its choices or prices (`ItemsScreen.kt:1713-1717`), so "Salsas" vs "Salsas 2" can't be told apart. Fix: subtitle with first choices.
10. Cart line prefixes every choice with "+", so a free choice like "+ Término medio" reads like an extra charge (`CartSheetContent.kt:680-686`). Possible. Fix: "+" only when there is a price.
11. Restaurants cannot make sizes: options picker unreachable (see 1). The "Tamaño" idea as an add-on only adds an extra price, it cannot replace the dish price. Possible confusion in a demo. Fix: decide whether restaurants get sizes in the dish form.
12. Stale screenshot: `ModifiersManagementScreenKt.ModifierEditorScreenPreview.WITH_BACKGROUND.png` shows the old purple look, title "Modifier" and "Affects nothing yet"; current code is "Nueva adición", "Aún no está en ningún producto…", green pills. Re-shoot before using as reference.

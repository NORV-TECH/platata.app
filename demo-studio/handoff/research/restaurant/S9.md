# S9 — Meter y sacar efectivo (cash in / cash out)

Source: commit d1b7698, read only. Paths under `app/src/main/java/com/example/`.

## 1. What it does, who, which device
Records money put into or taken out of the drawer during an OPEN shift (pay a supplier, owner takes cash, add change). It changes "Debe haber en la caja" and shows on Close and on the closed summary. **v1: owner phone only.** Allowed = owner or no PIN session (`mayHandleDrawerCash && maySeeRegisterMoney`, `RegisterStatusScreen.kt:122`; `data/CashMovement.kt:73`). Tablet and waiter phone have no door to it. Movements sync to other devices (`Platata_sync/{bizKey}/registers/{token}/shift_movements/{uuid}`).

## 2. Setup before the demo
- Register open (S2). Nothing to switch on.
- Reach the Register screen: bottom tab "Mi negocio" (flag) → green card "Caja abierta · Ventas $ X" (`HomeScreen.kt:1508-1540`).

## 3. Country differences
Same everywhere except money: keypad is CENTS style for 2-decimal currencies ("00" key): 2,0,0,0,0 → MX `$200.00`, BR `R$ 200,00`, US `$200.00`; COP/CLP use "000" and whole pesos: CO `$ 20.000`, CL `$20.000`. No rounding is applied to movements.

## 6. UX problems (verified)
1. Header date uses a fixed US pattern "MMM d" (`RegisterStatusScreen.kt:101`): Spanish/Portuguese read "Hoy · oct 19" instead of "19 oct". Fix: localized skeleton (`DateFormat.getBestDateTimePattern`).
2. Movement author saved as English "Owner" on the owner phone (`ui/pos/CashMovementActions.kt:32`). Fix: owner name / localized word.
3. Gender mix on one screen: pill "● ABIERTO/CERRADO" (masculine) vs rows "Abierta / Abierta por" (feminine) vs "Cerrado / Cerrado por"; pt "Aberta em" for the masculine "caixa" (`register_status_pill_*`, `register_status_opened_*`). Fix: "Caja abierta / cerrada" wording everywhere.
4. Cash in/out is impossible on the counter tablet or waiter phone even for a manager with "Cerrar turno" (gate needs owner, `RegisterStatusScreen.kt:122`). Known v1 limit; say so in the demo.
5. Same keypad grammar issue as S2/S3 (cents here, whole units at checkout).

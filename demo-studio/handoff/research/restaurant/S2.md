# S2 — Abrir la caja (as a RESTAURANT)

Source: commit d1b7698, read only. Paths under `app/src/main/java/com/example/`.

## 1. What it does, who, which device
Opens the shared till (one "shift") with the starting cash in the drawer. Selling is blocked while the till is known closed. **Only the owner's phone can open it.** The counter tablet and the waiter phone show a locked "Caja cerrada" page and cannot open it (`MamaPosScreen.kt:427-433`, `MiniPosHomeScreen.kt:235-258`). Opening writes `Platata_sync/{bizKey}/registers/{token}/shift/current` (create-if-absent), so every device sees "open" (choke point 5).

## 2. Setup before the demo
- A business exists (S1). A new business starts with the register CLOSED.
- No permission to set: the screen has no permission check (`ShiftController.kt:79 openShift`).
- Online is needed when the shop has a register token + cloud; offline/no-cloud opens locally (`OpenRegisterLogic.kt:44`). A failed shared write shows the error and Retry, never a silent local open.

## 3. Country differences
Only the money format and the keypad's extra key:
- 0-decimal currencies (CO COP, CL CLP): key "000", digits are whole pesos: 5,0,0,0,0 → CO `$ 50.000`, CL `$50.000`.
- 2-decimal (MX, PE, EC, AR, PA, BR, US, CA): key "00", digits are CENTS: 5,0,0,0 → MX `$50.00`, PE `S/ 50.00`, EC `$50,00`, AR `$ 50,00`, BR `R$ 50,00`, US/CA `$50.00` (`currencyKeypadDivisor`, `CurrencyUtils.kt:51`).
Wording: es "Abrir caja", pt "Abrir caixa", en "Open register". Same everywhere else.

## 6. UX problems (verified)
1. Two keypad grammars in one flow: Open/Close/Cash in-out treat digits as cents with an "00" key (`OpenRegisterScreen.kt:49-50`), the pay screen treats them as whole units with a decimal key (`CartActions.kt:668`, `CheckoutPayBlock.kt:271`). In MX/PE/EC/AR/PA/BR/US/CA typing "500" gives $5.00 here but $500 at checkout. Fix: one entry style for all money keypads.
2. pt-BR calls the same thing "Troco inicial" here and "Dinheiro inicial" on the Register screen (`register_open_amount_hint` vs `register_status_starting_cash`). Fix: one word ("Troco inicial").
3. The person who opens from the owner phone is saved as the English literal "Owner" (`ShiftController.kt:90`), so the Register screen shows "Abierta por: Owner" in Spanish/Portuguese. Fix: store the owner's name or a localized label.
4. Possible: an employee PIN session on the owner's phone is sent to Open register and can open it — no permission check in `openShift` (`ShiftController.kt:79`; gate `HomeScreen.kt:286`). Fix: require owner or canCloseShift.
5. The Register screen (`RegisterStatusScreen.kt`) has no "Abrir caja" button for a closed till — it only shows the last closed shift; open is only on Home/panel. Minor dead end if reached by history. Fix: show "Abrir caja" when no shift is open.

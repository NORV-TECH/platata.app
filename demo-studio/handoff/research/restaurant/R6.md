# R6 — "Meseros con su celular" (waiters on their own phone, Mini POS)

Checked against code at commit d1b7698d. Paths under `app/src/main/java/com/example/`.

## 1. What it does, who, where
- The owner turns an employee's own phone into a register ("Teléfono de empleado"). The waiter installs Platata, signs in with any Google account, types the business code + their 4-digit PIN, and sells: menu, open tables, charge.
- Owner side: owner phone (Conexiones, Equipo). Waiter side: the waiter's Android phone.
- Orders reach the kitchen only through the owner's phone (already established).

## 2. Setup before the demo
- Employee created in Equipo (Mi negocio › Equipo › "+ Nuevo"); the app generates a 4-digit PIN (no manual PIN, see MamaPos note `MamaPosScreen.kt:642-656`). A WhatsApp number on the employee makes the invite one tap.
- Connections: outside US/CA, 2 connections are included per business (owner phone + first device). A waiter phone that is the 2nd device needs a paid connection (Google Play; debug can fake it via long-press on the "Mi cuenta" title, `BillingActions.setDebugPaidConnections`).
- Register OPEN on the owner phone ("Abrir caja"). A closed register blocks the waiter at "Caja cerrada" (`MiniPosHomeScreen.kt:221-235`).
- Factory permissions of a new employee: ALL off (`data/Employee.kt:36-66`): no void, comp, refund, cancel, waive fee, close register, edit customer, stock, reports. So by default a waiter can only sell, save tables and charge.
- Idle lock default: 5 minutes ("Configuración › Seguridad › Bloquear la caja después de", 1/5/15/nunca).
- Waiter phone needs internet for the join (Cloud function `joinBusiness`).

## 3. Country differences
- US and CA: Connections page shows "Todo está incluido" / "En EE. UU. y Canadá no hay cargos por conexiones." (`conn_everything_included`, `conn_usa_body`). Everywhere else: "Conexiones en uso · 1 de 2", circles, monthly price from Play.
- Everything else same everywhere. Money format only on the waiter's sell screen (see R4).
- Words: Spanish "celular"/"teléfono" both used; pt "celular"; en "phone".

## 5. Demo path (two phones, ~10 taps)
1. Owner: Configuración › Conexiones › "+ Conectar" › "El teléfono de un empleado".
2. Tap "Conectar" next to "Ana".
3. Equipo › Ana › "Enviar invitación por WhatsApp" (message with business code + her code).
4. Waiter phone: "Ya uso Platata" › "Trabajo en un negocio" › Google account.
5. Type the business code and PIN → "Unirme" → lands on "Catálogo".
6. Tap dishes → "Cobrar $ 45.000 · 2 artículos" → "Mesas" → "4" → "Guardar".
7. Owner phone: Ventas › Abiertas shows "Mesa 4". Kitchen shows "Mesa 4 · Ana".
8. Leave the waiter phone 5 min (or show "Bloquear la caja después de") → PIN pad.

## 6. UX/UI problems (verified)
1. Tapping "Conectar" on an employee does nothing visible: no next step, no code, and the row keeps saying "Conectar" (connected needs a join or an invite token, `sync/ConnectedPhones.kt`; `persistMiniPosInvite` has no caller). Owner taps again and again. Fix: after Conectar, open a page with the business code, her PIN and "Enviar por WhatsApp", and show "Invitado".
2. The business code is shown on NO screen as plain text; it exists only inside the WhatsApp/copied invite (`StaffInviteSection.kt:52-66`; `phone_reg_shop_code` unused). But the join screen says "Lo ve en Mi negocio › Equipo" (`join_biz_sub`). Fix: show "Código del negocio: XXXX" on the Connect page and the employee page.
3. "Entra con la cuenta que invitó tu jefe" (`welcome_sheet_work_sub`): there is no account invite any more (code + PIN; any Google account works). Fix: "Entra con el código que te dio tu encargado."
4. Invite says "Tu código: 1234" but the join screen asks "TU PIN" (`phone_reg_share_message` vs `join_biz_pin_label`); also "jefe" vs "encargado". Fix: one word, "Tu PIN".
5. "Esto usa 1 de tus conexiones." also shows in US/CA, where nothing is counted or paid (`ConnectEmployeePhoneScreen.kt:110`). Fix: hide the note when `freeCountry`.
6. Waiter must sign in with Google before typing a code (`OnboardingNavGraph.kt:121-146`); a waiter without a Google account on the phone is stuck. Possible friction; no fallback in code.
7. The waiter phone never shows "Enviar a cocina" (see R4 #3), so the waiter does not know the order went to the kitchen.
8. Role line shows "Cajero" for every non-owner without a job title, including waiters (`ConnectEmployeePhoneScreen.kt:33-38` raw role, `MamaPosScreen.kt:88-91`). Fix: a "Mesero" role/title default for restaurants.
9. Locking after 5 min returns to a PIN pad with only the waiter's own face; no "not you?" path except the back arrow to the picker. Minor.

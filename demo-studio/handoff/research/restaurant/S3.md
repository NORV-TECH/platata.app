# S3 — Cobrar en efectivo (owner phone, RESTAURANT)

Source: commit d1b7698, read only. Paths under `app/src/main/java/com/example/`.

## 1. What it does, who, which device
The owner (or a PIN-signed employee) on the OWNER's phone taps dishes, taps "Cobrar $X", takes cash on the pay screen (Exacto, or type what the customer handed over and see the change), and lands on "¡Venta completada!". Sell screens use the plain Material light look (white #FFFFFF, near-black #1C1C1E buttons, grey #6E6E73, light grey #F2F2F7) — NOT the paper look. Counter tablet and waiter phone use the same pay body (`CheckoutSheetBody`) inside their own step flows (not covered here).

## 2. Setup before the demo
- Register OPEN (S2). A tap on a dish with the till closed jumps to "Abrir caja" first.
- A few dishes in the menu (R1/R2). Factory: menu empty.
- Factory payment methods: Efectivo ON, Cortesía ON (so "Más formas (1)" shows), QR off, card off.
- Dining options are all OFF at factory → no "¿Cómo sale este pedido?" question. Turn on Mesas etc. only if you want R5 in the shot.
- Optional: a receipt printer (then the receipt auto-prints and "Imprimir otra vez" appears) and a kitchen screen/order printer (then the left footer button says "Enviar a cocina" instead of "Guardar").

## 3. Country differences
- Money format: CO `$ 13.068`, MX `$130.68`, PE `S/ 18.65`, CL `$18.635`, EC `$13,07`, AR `$ 1.306,80`, PA `USD13.07` (JDK golden; verify), BR `R$ 13,07`, US/CA `$13.07`.
- Pay keypad extra key: "000" for COP/CLP; decimal key for the rest ("." MX/PE/PA/US/CA, "," EC/AR/BR). Digits here are whole units (500 = $500).
- **Cash rounding (cash only)** — `utils/CashRounding.kt`: CL to $10 (1–5 down, 6–9 up), PE always DOWN to S/ 0.10, CA to nearest 5¢. The big "Total" stays exact; "Exacto · X" and the change use the rounded cash amount (`PosViewModelTypes.kt:902 cashDue`). Example CL: Total `$18.635` → "Exacto · $18.630"; PE `S/ 18.67` → `S/ 18.60`; CA `$18.62` → `$18.60`. The receipt prints a "Redondeo en efectivo" line. No rounding in CO, MX, EC, AR, PA, BR, US.
- Pay row besides cash: CO may show "+ Tarjeta / Configurar" (owner, not connected) and the link "Configurar QR o tarjeta · No, gracias"; MX/PE/CL/EC/AR/PA/BR show "Configurar QR · No, gracias"; US/CA show NO QR, and Card as a "+ Card · Set up" button beside cash (then Exact shows no amount). (`PayWaysVisibility.kt`).
- US/CA Sale complete: "Text receipt" + "Email receipt" instead of WhatsApp (`ReceiptSendRoute`).
- Words: es "Cobrar / Venta", pt "Cobrar / Venda", en "Charge / Sale".

## 6. UX problems (verified)
1. Cash rounding is not explained on the phone pay screen: Total shows exact (`CheckoutPayBlock.kt:164`), "Exacto" and change use the rounded amount (`:313`, `:686-689`); the "Redondeo en efectivo %s" line exists only on `CashTenderScreen.kt:52-61`, which the phone flow never reaches. CL/PE/CA cashiers see two different totals. Fix: show "Redondeo en efectivo −$5" under Total when cash.
2. The dining link on the pay screen shows the stored English name ("For Here", "To Go", "Delivery") for every option but Mesas (`CheckoutSheetBody.kt:262-268` uses `opt.name`; seeds are English, `SeedStore.kt:111-114`). The chooser itself is localized (`:1235-1241`). Fix: map by systemKey like the chooser.
3. Two keypad grammars: pay keypad = whole units + decimal key (`CartActions.kt:668`), Open/Close/Cash in-out = cents + "00" (`OpenRegisterScreen.kt:49`). Same cashier, same shift. Fix: one style.
4. US/CA "Text receipt" button is painted WhatsApp green #25D366 (`ReceiptOptionsScreen.kt:354`). Fix: neutral/primary color.
5. "¿Cómo quieres recibir tu recibo?" speaks to the customer while every other line speaks to the cashier ("Recibiste…"). Possible confusion when the owner holds the phone. Fix: "¿Le mandas el recibo?".

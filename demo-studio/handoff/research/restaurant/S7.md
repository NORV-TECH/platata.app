# S7 — Recibo por WhatsApp o impreso (restaurant)

Code: /home/claude/suma-plus-android @ d1b7698d. Paths under `app/src/main/java/com/example/`.

## 1. What it does, who, where
After every sale the "¡Venta completada!" screen offers the receipt: WhatsApp (Latin America + Brazil) or text message + email (US/Canada), print again (if a receipt printer is set up), or view it on screen. Any past sale can also be resent/printed/shared from Ventas > "Venta #N".
Devices: owner phone, counter tablet (inline in its right pane), waiter phone (all use `ReceiptOptionsScreen`). The receipt prints automatically at the moment of sale when a receipt printer is saved (ui/pos/CheckoutActions.kt:558).

## 2. Setup before the demo
- Nothing needed for WhatsApp: WhatsApp must be installed on the phone.
- Faster demo: attach a customer that has a saved WhatsApp number at checkout → WhatsApp opens straight to that chat (no number box).
- Printing: Mi negocio > Ajustes > "Conexiones" > the printers row (UI-1, `Screen.PrintersAndScreens`; exact row wording not checked) > add a Bluetooth or Wi-Fi receipt printer (PRINT-1). Without one, the "Imprimir otra vez" button is hidden and New sale reads "Nueva venta · Sin recibo".
- Receipt look (store name, header/footer messages, phone/address/website switches, receipt number) is in Ajustes > Recibos (not part of this feature).
- Every WhatsApp text ends with "Hecho con Platata · platata.app" / "Feito com Platata · platata.app" / "Made with Platata · platata.app" (WASIG-1). SMS/email/share are not signed.

## 3. Country differences
`sync/ReceiptSendRoute.kt:245`: US and CA → Text + Email; every other country (and blank) → WhatsApp.
| | CO MX PE CL EC AR PA BR | US CA |
|---|---|---|
| First button | green "WhatsApp" | green "Text receipt" + outlined "Email receipt" |
| Number box seed | the shop's dial code, e.g. "+57 " (ui/common/WhatsappReceipt.kt:24) | SMS opens with the customer's number or empty "To" |
| Cash rounding lines on receipt | CL, PE (and CA) print "rounding" + "cash total" | CA yes, US no |
Money on receipt uses `formatCurrency`: CO `$ 45.000`, CL `$45.000`, MX `$1,234.50`, PE `S/ 1,234.50`, BR `R$ 1.234,50`, PA `USD1,234.50`.

## 6. UX/UI problems (verified)
1. US/CA: the receipt viewer's "Enviar" always opens the WhatsApp step (`ReceiptContent`, ShowReceiptDialog.kt:105-117 — no country route), while the Sale complete screen offers Text/Email there. Fix: pass `ReceiptSendRoute` to `ReceiptContent`.
2. US/CA: "Text receipt" on Sale complete is painted WhatsApp green #25D366 (ReceiptOptionsScreen.kt:300-304); the same action on "Sale #N" is dark (SaleActionRow default). Fix: neutral/primary colour for SMS.
3. Inconsistent ending: after WhatsApp the sale ends and the app jumps Home (`onSent → wipeAndGoHome`, ReceiptOptionsScreen.kt:121), so the cashier cannot also print; after Text/Email the screen stays. Fix: stay on Sale complete after WhatsApp too (or end after both).
4. A typed WhatsApp number is used once and never saved to a customer — next visit the box is empty again. Possible improvement.
5. Tipped US/CA sales: no "Tip" line on the receipt; total jumps (see S5 problem 8).
6. pt wording mix: "comprovante" on buttons but the viewer title and the email subject say "Recibo" (`receipt_dlg_title` pt = "Recibo"). Fix: "Comprovante".
7. Question text "¿Cómo quieres recibir tu recibo?" repeats "recibir/recibo" and addresses the customer while the cashier holds the phone. Fix: "¿Enviar el recibo?".

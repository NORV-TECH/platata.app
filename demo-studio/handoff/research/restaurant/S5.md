# S5 — Cobrar con tarjeta (restaurant)

Code: /home/claude/suma-plus-android @ d1b7698d. Paths under `app/src/main/java/com/example/`.

## 1. What it does, who, where
Take a card payment inside Platata. Two different systems by country (sync/CardCountryGate.kt:102-107):
- Colombia: **Wompi**. The cashier TYPES the card (number, expiry, CVC, name) on the phone; Platata sends it to the bank. Card-not-present.
- US and Canada: **Stripe Tap to Pay**. The customer taps/inserts the card on the phone itself (no extra reader is sold). Optional tip screen first.
- Every other country (MX PE CL EC AR PA BR): no card at all (no Card row in Payment methods, no Card button).
Setup: owner only, owner's phone. Charging: owner phone, counter tablet, waiter phone (all use `CheckoutSheetBody`). An employee tapping an unconfigured card gets "Solo el propietario puede configurar los pagos con tarjeta".
**No card photo / camera scan exists.** Only the typed form. A token `CardCamHeight` "scanner accelerator — deferred; token reserved" (ui/theme/DesignTokens.kt:455) is the only trace; no ML-Kit card code.

- Wompi "Preguntar por cuotas" switch exists but does nothing (problem 4).

## 6. UX/UI problems (verified)
1. **Device-can't-tap warning leaks to every country.** `cardDeviceHintRes` and `cardDeviceBar` are computed with no country gate (CheckoutSheetBody.kt:168, 462-477, rendered at :1041). On a phone without NFC or with Android ≤12, ANY country's checkout shows the red bar "Este teléfono no puede cobrar con tarjeta — tu cuenta de Stripe está bien" / "Las tarjetas necesitan Android 13…", even in Mexico/Brazil with no card at all; and in Colombia the Wompi Card button reads "No disponible" though the typed form works (tap still opens it). The Home strip IS gated (HomeScreen.kt:423 `if (stripeTenderAvailable)`). Fix: pass both only when `stripeOn`.
2. Voseo in the Stripe Spanish strings: "Insertá / Acercá / Deslizá / Presentá la tarjeta" (`stripe_present_*`); rest of the app uses tú. Fix: "Inserta / Acerca / Desliza / Presenta".
3. Waiter-phone device bar says "pide efectivo o QR" (`card_device_bar_phone`) but this bar only matters in US/CA, where QR does not exist. Fix: "pide efectivo".
4. "Preguntar por cuotas" (Wompi) switch does nothing: `askInstallments` is never read at checkout; the overlay always uses `defaultInstallments` (=1, no UI sets it) (WompiPaymentOverlay.kt:138). Fix: add a cuotas picker or hide the switch.
5. Wompi expiry field: cashier types "MMAA" blind (masked, no slash, no "MM / AA" hint). Hint strings `card_field_expiry_hint`, `card_field_holder_hint` exist but are unused. Fix: show the hints; consider not masking expiry.
6. Sending pill always reads "TARJETA · 1 cuota": brand/last four are only known after approval, and "TARJETA" is hard-coded Spanish (WompiPaymentOverlay.kt:572). Fix: drop the pill while sending or localize.
7. Stripe tip screen has no visible back/cancel; only the system back gesture closes it (StripePaymentSheet.kt:193-272). The cashier hands the phone to the customer — no way out on screen. Fix: add "Cancel" text button.
8. **Tip is not printed on the receipt.** The order total includes the tip (CheckoutActions.kt:377, 452) but no receipt line says "Tip"; the digital receipt's Total jumps above the item sum (ReceiptFormatter.kt has no tip line), and the thermal print uses the pre-tip `grandTotal` (CheckoutActions.kt:551) — possible mismatch between paper and digital totals. Fix: add a "Propina/Tip" line and print the tipped total.
9. Card reader row is only reachable from the Stripe connect page, through the button "Connect a different account" (PaymentMethodsScreen.kt:1401-1411). Fix: put "Card reader" directly in the Card block.
10. Wording: Wompi consent boxes are pre-ticked and cannot be unticked — fine legally per code comment, but the cashier ticks for the customer; possible concern.

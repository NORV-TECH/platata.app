## 1. What it does, who, where
Besides Cash, QR and Card, a sale can be closed with any payment method the owner names (Nequi, Daviplata, Mercado Pago, Yape, Transferencia, Datáfono, Zelle…) or given away free ("Cortesía" / "On the house"). At checkout they sit behind one link "Más formas (N)" → a bottom sheet; one tap closes the sale.
- Owner creates/turns them on: owner's phone, Ajustes > Métodos de pago.
- Used by: owner phone, counter tablet, waiter phone (same `CheckoutSheetBody`). Cortesía only appears on devices allowed to comp (owner always; employees only with "canComp", default false — data/Employee.kt:46).
- **Fiado (tab) is NOT available to a restaurant.** `RestaurantRules.fiadoEnabled = false` (data/business/RestaurantRules.kt:15); the Fiado row is stripped from every list (`scopeFiadoTender`, data/FiadoScope.kt:12, applied in PosViewModel.kt:2111-2125); the Save button never turns into "Fiar" (CheckoutSheetBody.kt:310-319). Confirmed.

## 2. Setup before the demo
- Factory (all countries): Efectivo ON, Pago QR OFF, Tarjeta OFF (card countries only), Cortesía ON (PaymentConfigStore.kt:461-463). **No custom methods are seeded** — no Nequi, no Mercado Pago. The app has no per-country preset list (only a sort rule that puts a method literally named "Mercado Pago" third, PosViewModel.kt:2123).
- So a fresh restaurant owner phone shows "Más formas (1)" (just Cortesía).
- To demo Nequi: Ajustes > Métodos de pago > "+ Nuevo" → dialog "Agregar método de pago", field "Nombre (ej. Cheques)" → type "Nequi" → "Agregar". It is created ON. Max 10 methods (the "+ Nuevo" disappears at 10; PaymentMethodsScreen.kt:264).
- Each custom method has a switch and a red trash icon (confirm "¿Eliminar método de pago?" / "¿Seguro que quieres eliminar "Nequi"?"). With a cash drawer, each also has "Abrir cajón".
- Cortesía row: switch + note "Solo tú y quien autorices en Equipo > Permisos." / pt "Só você e quem você autorizar em Equipe > Permissões." / en "Only you and people you allow in Team > Permissions."

## 6. UX/UI problems (verified)
1. One accidental tap in the sheet closes the sale with that method — no confirm, no proof photo (CheckoutSheetBody.kt:762-765). The subtitle warns "se cobra al tocar", but for Nequi/transfer (money the cashier must check, like QR) there is no "check your bank" reminder. Fix: optional confirm, or reuse the QR "Pagó · Foto del pago" step for custom wallet methods.
2. Add-method example "Nombre (ej. Cheques)" (`pay_methods_field_name`) — cheques are rare in LatAm restaurants. Fix: "ej. Nequi, Transferencia".
3. No preset wallets per country (Nequi, Yape, Pix, Mercado Pago…): a new restaurant must type them. Possible improvement for onboarding.
4. Possible: comp sale's paper receipt prints the full price as total — the thermal `ReceiptPrintData.total` uses `grandTotal` (not 0) for comp (CheckoutActions.kt:546), while the stored sale is 0. Fix: print 0 / "Cortesía".
5. Possible: "Más formas" is easy to miss on the owner phone — on a new restaurant it shows "(1)" and only holds Cortesía.
6. The link reads "Más formas" but the sheet title says "Otro método de pago" — two names for one thing. Fix: one name.

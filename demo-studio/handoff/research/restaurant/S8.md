# S8 — Devolver una venta (restaurant)

Code: /home/claude/suma-plus-android @ d1b7698d. Paths under `app/src/main/java/com/example/`.

## 1. What it does, who, where
Give money back for a finished sale — all of it, some dishes, or a typed amount. The app tells the cashier WHERE the money goes (cash drawer, the same wallet, the customer's card) and, for card sales that cannot be refunded inside the app, says so instead of offering a button.
- Who: the owner always; an employee only with "canRefund" (default false, data/Employee.kt:36).
- Where: bottom tab "Ventas" > a sale > "Venta #N" > "Devolver". On owner phone, counter tablet and waiter phone (shared `SaleDetailHost`).
- PIN: security is ON by default for every new business (data/repo/SeedStore.kt:135), so after "Sí, devolver" a PIN pad asks for the PIN of the owner or an employee allowed to refund.
- Separate from refund: "¿Venta equivocada? Anularla" (void) — a small red link, only for a sale of the open shift (or today if the register was never used), never for a Stripe card sale.

## 2. Setup before the demo
- At least one finished sale (cash is simplest).
- Know the owner's PIN: it is AUTO-GENERATED at business creation (SeedStore.kt:128 `generateUniqueEmployeePin()`), the owner never chose it — find it in Mi negocio > Equipo (code shown, hides after 45 s).
- For card refunds: CO a Wompi card sale (refund happens in the Wompi dashboard); US/CA a Stripe card sale (refund goes straight to the card, needs internet).

## 3. Country differences — the route (sync/RefundRoute.kt:66, `RefundSaleAction`, `RefundDestinationLine`)
| Sale paid with | Where the money goes / what the app shows |
|---|---|
| Cash (all countries) | "Entrega $X en efectivo"; leaves the drawer. CL rounds the refund to $10 and PE to S/0.10 in the BUYER's favour; CA to 5¢ (utils/CashRounding.kt:13-25) |
| QR / Nequi / any custom method | "Devuelve $X por Nequi" — book-keeping only, the owner sends the money back himself |
| Cortesía | no line (nothing was paid) |
| Card, Colombia (Wompi) | button "Devolver en Wompi" → "Siguiente: devolver $X en Wompi" → PIN → dialog "Devuélvelo en Wompi" → owner refunds in his Wompi panel → "Ya lo devolví en Wompi". Nothing is booked until that tap |
| Card, US/CA (Stripe) | "Goes back to the customer's card"; Stripe refund runs from the app (spinner on the confirm button). While pending: amber "Stripe is still processing this refund. Do not refund again…" and no button |
| Card in US/CA but this phone has no Stripe reference (sale rung on another phone) | no button; grey "Refund this on the register that took the card, or in Stripe." + owner button "Open Stripe" |
| Interac debit (CA) | no button; grey "Interac can't be refunded here. Give cash or store credit." |
Money format: CO `$ 22.000`, MX `$1,234.50`, BR `R$ 1.234,50`, US `$12.50`, PA `USD1,234.50`.

## 6. UX/UI problems (verified)
1. Owner is asked for a PIN he never chose: security is on by default and the owner PIN is auto-generated (SeedStore.kt:128,135); the dialog only says "Ingresa el PIN de un empleado autorizado". Fix: on the owner's phone allow fingerprint/screen lock, or say "Tu PIN está en Equipo".
2. Wompi dialog sends the owner to "tu panel de Wompi" but has no "Abrir Wompi" button and the transaction id is plain, not copyable (TransactionsScreen.kt:2283-2309). Fix: add an open-dashboard button and a copy action.
3. Wompi road skips the confirm box; if the owner leaves the app to Wompi and Android recreates the screen, the pending prompt is dropped on dispose (`dismissWompiDashboardRefund`, :1623) — possible lost step. Fix: keep the prompt until answered.
4. Item rows show the dish's list price, but the refund is a prorated share incl. tax/discount, so the bottom amount can differ from the ticked prices with no explanation (:1447-1489 vs :1833). Fix: show the per-dish refund value or a hint "incluye impuestos/descuentos".
5. English casing is inconsistent: "Select All Items", "Refund Amount", "Confirm Refund", "Yes, Refund" vs sentence case elsewhere. Fix: sentence case.
6. "Interac … Give cash or store credit." — the app has no store-credit feature. Fix: "Give cash."
7. Refund result and pending banner amounts use `formatCurrency(result.amount)` without the shop currency (TransactionsScreen.kt:1261, 1562) — same class of global-currency bug as PHONEFIX-1. Possible wrong symbol right after a business switch.
8. The void link is hidden outside the shift window and never shown disabled — a cashier looking for "Anular" yesterday's sale finds nothing. Possible: add a one-line reason.

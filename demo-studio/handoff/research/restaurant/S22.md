# S22 — Copia en Google Drive (Respaldo) — as seen by a RESTAURANT

Code: /home/claude/suma-plus-android @ d1b7698d (sim/usa-canada). All paths under `app/src/main/java/com/example/`.

## 1. What it does, who, which device
- The owner connects one Google account; the app then copies the WHOLE restaurant database (menu, sales, tabs, clients, team, settings) to that person's Google Drive, folder `Drive/Platata/<Negocio>`. Every business on the phone is copied, not only the open one (`work/DailyBackupWorker.kt:10-14`).
- Restore = bring a copy back (same phone or a new phone).
- **Owner's phone only ("Tu teléfono").** The route bounces every other device (`ui/navigation/SettingsNavGraph.kt:82-95`); the workers and buttons refuse on a tablet / waiter phone / kitchen screen (`ui/pos/BackupActions.kt:133,186`, `DailyBackupWorker.kt:27`).

## 2. Setup before the demo
- Factory default: **nothing is connected**. The sell screen shows the amber bar "Esta tienda no tiene respaldo · Conectar" until Drive is connected.
- Needs a real Google account on the phone, internet, and the Drive permission (Google's own consent sheet appears once).
- To show restore you need at least one copy in Drive: tap "Respaldar ahora" once (copies are not made instantly by the schedule: first automatic run 90 s after connecting, then every 6 h, only if something changed — `BackupActions.kt:217-239`, `WorkspaceBackup.kt:158`).
- Copies older than 30 days are pruned, but the newest one is always kept (`work/DriveBackupHelper.kt:293-322`). Invoice photos go up separately, Wi-Fi only, never pruned.
- If no copy succeeds for 48 h while the shop has changes, or the Google token dies, a notification "Los respaldos de Platata se detuvieron" + the bar "Los respaldos se detuvieron · Solucionar" appear (`work/BackupAlerts.kt`, `WorkspaceBackup.kt:113`).

## 3. Country differences
Same everywhere (no country branch in any backup file). Only language changes (es / pt-BR / en). No money is shown on these screens except in the restore warning (sale count, not amounts).

## 6. UX/UI problems (verified)
1. "Nunca se borran automáticamente" is false: copies older than 30 days ARE deleted (newest kept). `res/values-es/strings.xml` `backup_drive_note` vs `work/DriveBackupHelper.kt:293-322`. Fix: "Guardamos los últimos 30 días."
2. "Se crean automáticamente una vez al día" is wrong: the schedule is every 6 h, only when something changed. `restore_no_points` vs `ui/pos/BackupActions.kt:227-229`. Fix: "Se crean solos cada pocas horas cuando hay cambios."
3. Restore list and confirm dialog show the raw English label "La Fonda — Oct 19, 2026 · 14:00" in Spanish/Portuguese. `BackupSettingsScreen.kt:523,643` (label from `DriveBackupHelper.kt:541-561`). Fix: format through `RestoreLabel.parse` like the new-phone screen does.
4. "Último respaldo" older than 24 h is always written with a Spanish locale and 12-h clock ("19 oct, 2:00 p. m.") even in Portuguese/English. `BackupSettingsScreen.kt:752` (`Locale("es")`). Fix: use the phone's locale.
5. "Respaldar ahora" with no internet spins "Respaldando…" forever (WorkManager waits for a network, nothing says so). `BackupActions.kt:144-152,153-171`. Fix: if offline, say "Se respaldará cuando haya internet" and stop the spinner.
6. The failing-bar date uses a different format again ("MMM d, h:mm a", English order) — `HomeScreen.kt:409-411`. Fix: one shared date formatter.
7. "Datos en Riesgo" body is jargon ("archivos transaccionales") and Title Case. `backup_risk_desc`. Fix: "Tus ventas, menú y clientes aún no tienen copia."
8. Possible: unused frequency strings ("Tiempo real (Recomendado)", "Sincronizar cada 1 hora"…) exist in strings.xml but no screen shows them — old notes describe a frequency picker that does not exist. Don't film/script it.

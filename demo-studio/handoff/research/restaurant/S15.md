# S15 — Pago a empleados (Employee pay tracking), as seen by a RESTAURANT

Source: /home/claude/suma-plus-android @ d1b7698d (read only). Paths under `app/src/main/java/com/example/`.

## 1. What it does, who, where
- The owner records roughly what each person earns per month. Then, from the 5th of every month, the app asks "how much did you pay last month?" with the amounts pre-filled; one tap "Todo pagado" saves it, or "No trabajó este mes" saves 0 for one person. Past months can be opened and corrected. The totals feed "Pagado a empleados" on the accountant sheet (S12). Nothing is paid or calculated; it is a record.
- **Owner's phone only.** Lives inside Team (S14): the switch at the top of the list, amounts on each row, "Meses anteriores" at the bottom, and the monthly ask as a full-screen page over the sell screen.

## 2. Setup before the demo
- At least one non-owner, non-blocked employee (S14). The owner is never paid here.
- Off by default (`employee_pay_enabled` absent).
- Turning on: switch → (first time only) the "before you turn it on" page → type an amount for every person → "Activar". This stamps `employee_pay_since` = the current month (`ui/pos/EmployeePayActions.kt:53-58`).
- The monthly ask only appears on/after the 5th, then the 10th, 20th, 25th, and only for the PREVIOUS month (`ui/screens/team/EmployeePayAskScreen.kt:51-112`). To film it on a given day the phone date must be ≥ 5 and last month unanswered (see also P6: it may show right after turning on).
- For "Meses anteriores" with data: confirm one or two months (or seed `employee_pay_month` rows).

## 3. Country differences
- Same screens and words everywhere. Only money format changes, both in the boxes (drawn with the country's grouping, stored as a plain number — `MoneyVisualTransformation`) and in totals: CO `$ 1.200.000`, CL `$1.200.000`, MX `$1,200,000.00`, PE `S/ 1,200,000.00`, AR `$ 1.200.000,00`, EC `$1.200.000,00`, PA `USD1,200,000.00`, BR `R$ 1.200.000,00`, US/CA `$1,200,000.00`.
- Months are named in the app language ("septiembre / setembro / September").

## 5. Demo path (owner phone, ~10 taps)
1. Flag → "Equipo" → tap the "Rastrear pagos de empleados" switch.
2. Consent page → "Listo, entiendo".
3. Type $ 1.200.000 for Ana, $ 1.100.000 for Luis → "Activar". Rows now show the amounts; "Meses anteriores ›" appears.
4. Back to the sell screen → (on/after the 5th, last month unanswered) the page "Pago de septiembre" appears.
5. Tap "No trabajó este mes" under Luis → tag "No trabajó" + "Deshacer".
6. "Todo pagado".
7. Flag → "Equipo" → "Meses anteriores" → "2026 · $ 1.200.000 pagado hasta ahora", "septiembre · 2 personas · 1 no trabajó".
8. Tap septiembre → change Luis to $ 300.000 → "Guardar cambios".
9. Reports → "Mes pasado" → "Enviar al contador" → "Pagado a empleados $ 1.500.000".

## 6. UX/UI problems (verified in code)
- **P1 — The "it switched itself off" notice is never shown.** The app computes `employeePayAutoOffMonth` (`ui/pos/WorkspaceSwitchActions.kt:291-294`, `EmployeePayActions.kt:178-186`) but no screen displays `employee_pay_auto_off_note` ("Se apagó en %s. Guardamos todo…" — no UI reference found). The owner who missed the 25th is never told. Fix: show it above the switch while off.
- **P2 — "Meses anteriores" disappears when tracking is off.** The bar is drawn only `payEnabled` (`TeamManagementScreen.kt:507-522`), and the accountant link needs tracking on too, so saved history is unreachable while off — contradicting the consent text "Lo que ya nos dijiste se queda". Fix: show the bar whenever any month is saved (`employeePayEverConfirmed`).
- **P3 — No way to cancel turning on.** After the consent, the list is stuck in amount-entry with the red "Cada persona necesita un monto…" line; there is no Cancel and Back leaves Team (`:483-506`, no BackHandler for `turningOn`). Fix: a "Cancelar" next to "Activar" and Back = cancel.
- **P4 — "1 personas"** and "1 no trabajó" are not plurals (`employee_pay_history_people`, `employee_pay_history_people_didnt`). Fix: `<plurals>`.
- **P5 — Consent says "if the month ends", code switches off after the 25th** (`EmployeePayAskScreen.kt:105-108` vs `employee_pay_consent_row3_title`). Fix: "Si el 25 pasa sin respuesta, se apaga solo".
- **P6 — Possible: asked about the month BEFORE you started.** "Desde hoy" says tracking starts today, but the ask targets the previous month with no check of `employee_pay_since` (`EmployeePayActions.kt:80-121`); turn it on on 19 Oct, go back to the sell screen, and "Pago de septiembre" can pop up at once. Fix: skip months before `employee_pay_since`.
- **P7 — Possible: the ask can silently miss on a cold start.** It runs once when the role resolves and reads `employees` (a `WhileSubscribed` flow that starts empty, `ui/PosViewModel.kt:2171-2175`); with an empty list `hasEmployees` is false → no ask and no dot until the next Home visit. Fix: read the roster fresh (`repository.hasPayableEmployees()`, as the accountant sheet does).
- **P8 — Possible privacy leak:** the ask (everyone's salary) is gated on `userRole == OWNER` only (`HomeScreen.kt:253, 1341`), which stays OWNER during a staff PIN session on the owner's phone. Fix: `ReportsAudience.isOwnerView`.
- **P9 — "Rastrear pagos de empleados" is a literal, cold label** ("rastrear" = track/trace); the rest of the feature says "llevar la cuenta de lo que pagas". Fix: "Anotar lo que les pago".
- **P10 — Month editor has no unsaved-changes guard**: Back after typing loses the edits with no warning (`TeamManagementScreen.kt:189-191`, `EmployeePayMonthDetailScreen`). Fix: confirm discard when changed.

# T3 — "Crear un producto en segundos" (scan an unknown barcode → new item)

Checked against code at commit **6736ddfb**. Paths under `app/src/main/java/com/example/`.

## 1. What it does, who, where
- While selling, the owner scans a code the shop does not have yet. A full-screen form "¿Qué es este artículo?" asks name, price, category and quantity; "Agregar al carrito" **saves the product to the catalogue with its barcode AND adds it to the sale** in one tap. From then on the same code goes straight into the sale.
- Code: form `ui/screens/home/ScannerScreen.kt:299-563`; save `ui/screens/home/ScanCartScreen.kt:21-106` → `findOrCreateScannedProduct` (`data/repo/OrderSyncStore.kt:772-800`, one lock + one transaction, so two phones cannot make two rows for one code); the product is also pushed to the shop's other devices (`ScanCartScreen.kt:91-98`, `pushScannedProduct`).
- Owner phone and employee phone. **Counter tablet: never** (it only scans known products, `ui/pos/TabletScanActions.kt`). Retail = Pharmacy.
- There is **no "scan to build the catalogue without selling" mode** (the strings `scan_build_*` "CREANDO CATÁLOGO" exist but no code uses them). The item form (Items › + Nuevo) has **no barcode field** (searched `ui/screens/items/ItemsScreen.kt` and the items folder): a product typed by hand can never be scanned.

## 2. Setup before the demo
- Tienda/Farmacia, barcode list downloaded (T2), camera allowed, **register open**.
- Printed EAN-13 labels for "his own" products (not in the list), e.g. 7709990000016 / …023 / …030 (checked absent from list v1.2). For the "known code" variant use 7708033169352 (list row "GENERICO Limpiador De Vidrios Con Spray").
- Seeded categories exist from creation (24 "ready-made" shop categories, e.g. "Cereales y Harinas", "Aseo del Hogar", "Farmacia", "Otros").

## 3. Country differences
- Same flow everywhere. Price chip "Sugerido $ X" only in Colombia (`data/PriceSuggestions.kt:57-66`).
- Price typed with the keyboard's decimal key; `filterDecimalInput` keeps it as typed (CO/CL whole pesos: "3500"; MX/US "35.50").
- Names from the list are Spanish in every country (see T2).

## 4. Screens in order

### 4a. Camera — see T1 §4b. Tap on an unknown code → beep (the beep means "read", not "known", `ScannerScreen.kt:133-138`) → form.

### 4b. "¿Qué es este artículo?" — `ScannerScreen.kt:378-562`
Full screen over the camera, Material light, surface #FFFFFF, 16dp side margin, 12dp gaps, scrolls, moves above the keyboard.
- Title headlineSmall Bold "**¿Qué es este artículo?**" (pt "O que é este item?", en "What is this item?").
- The scanned number, bodySmall grey #6E6E73: "7709990000016".
- **Nombre** (pt Nome, en Name):
  - unknown code: editable field, keyboard opens here, capital words, Next → price;
  - known code (from the list or the shop): greyed, read-only, 2 lines.
- **Monto** (pt Valor, en Amount) — decimal keyboard, Done.
- Under the price: chip "Sugerido $ 14.890" (CO, known code) or grey text "Sin precio sugerido" (pt "Sem preço sugerido", en "No suggested price").
- **Seleccionar categoría** dropdown (pt "Selecionar categoria", en "Select category") — only when the category is unknown; red outline until picked; lists every shop category.
- Quantity row: [–] **1** [+] (titleMedium bold, centred).
- Button full width, black #1C1C1E, 52dp: "**Agregar al carrito**" (pt "Adicionar ao carrinho", en "Add to Cart"); off until name, price and category are set.
- Text button red #FF3B30 "**Descartar**" (pt/en "Descartar / Discard"). If something was typed, Back/Descartar asks first ("Vas a perder este producto escaneado." / pt "Você vai perder este produto escaneado." / en "You'll lose this scanned item.").
- While the form is open the camera is disarmed (`captureEnabled = false`, `:130`).

### 4c. Back on the camera
Status line = the new name; Cobrar button badge +1. The product appears in Items (shop list) with its barcode, stock "Sin contar" (never counted).

## 5. Demo path (owner phone, Colombia)
1. Escanear › **Agregar productos**.
2. Aim at label 7709990000016 → tap → "¿Qué es este artículo?" with the number under the title.
3. Type **Arroz 500 g** → Next.
4. Type **3500**.
5. Categoría → **Cereales y Harinas**.
6. **Agregar al carrito** → back on camera, status "Arroz 500 g", "Cobrar $ 3.500".
7. Tap the same label again → beep, "Otra más · Arroz 500 g × 2" (no form any more).
8. (Variant) label 7708033169352 → form with greyed name "GENERICO Limpiador De Vidrios Con Spray", no category field, chip "Sugerido $ 14.890" → tap chip → Agregar al carrito.
9. Panel (flag) › **Artículos**: "Arroz 500 g", stock "Sin contar"; search "77099" finds it by barcode.

## 6. UX/UI problems (verified)
1. The price field is labelled "Monto"/"Amount", not "Precio" (`ScannerScreen.kt:444`, `common_amount`); every other product form says "Precio". Fix: `barless_field_price`. — small.
2. The category dropdown has no "+ Nueva categoría" (the item form has one, E-12) and is not sorted (`:512-520`). Fix: same list as `CategoryChoices.forEditor`. — small.
3. No barcode field in the item form; a product typed by hand (or a typo'd code) cannot be fixed or linked to a barcode. Fix: barcode field (with scan icon) in the item form. — small, but a natural demo question.
4. Creating a product means putting it in a sale. To stock 30 new items he has to ring them and then clear the sale. Fix: a "scan to add to catalogue" mode from Items (the unused `scan_build_*` strings suggest it was planned).
5. A known code's name is locked even when it is wrong, Spanish, or cut at 40 chars (`:411-422`). Fix: allow edit. (Also T2.)
6. The quantity buttons are bare `IconButton`s with no label for screen readers (`contentDescription = null`, `:530-541`). Fix: "Menos"/"Más".

## 1. Differences
- **Fiado lines** (shop/pharmacy only):
  - "En fiado (sin cobrar)" | "No fiado (sem receber)" | "On tab (not collected yet)" — "− $ X" just above Total cobrado (`ReportsScreen.kt:333-339`). So Total cobrado = money really in hand.
  - "Fiado perdonado" | "Fiado perdoado" | "Tab forgiven" — red "− $ X" under Total cobrado, a loss line, never inside sales (`ReportsScreen.kt:345-353`).
  - "Cómo te pagaron" lists a "Fiado" row among the methods (REVFIX-5).
- **Where the shop entry is**: owner phone = panel row "Reportes · Hoy · $ X" (same). Waiter phone in a shop = a "Reportes" row inside its Scan card, only with "Ver reportes" (`ui/screens/onboarding/MiniPosScanTab.kt:123`).
- Category list uses the 24 ready-made shop categories ("Bebidas", "Lácteos", …); top items list is by product. "Ventas por tipo de consumo" never shows (shops have no dining options).
- **No stock in Reports**: no inventory value, units on hand, running-low or shrinkage figures (searched "stock" in `ReportsScreen.kt`: none). Stock lives only in Mi negocio › Inventario.
- Tab PAYMENTS collected (abonos) are not a Reports line; they appear only on the register/close screens (S9/S10).

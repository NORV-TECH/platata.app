## 1. Differences
- Two fiado lines can appear on the closed summary and in its WhatsApp/print text:
  - "Abonos de fiado" | "Pagamentos de fiado" | "Tab payments (fiado)" — cash collected on tabs this shift, part of the expected drawer (`ui/screens/cash/RegisterClosedScreen.kt:288-289`, `utils/ShiftSummaryText.kt:91`).
  - "En fiado (sin cobrar)" | "No fiado (sem receber)" | "On tab (not collected yet)" shown as "−$ X" above Total cobrado (`RegisterClosedScreen.kt:262`, `ShiftSummaryText.kt:81`; CLAUDE.md REVFIX-5).
- The "open tables" warning before closing (FIX-1 item 6) uses non-table words for a shop (`CloseRegisterScreen.kt:171-174` `asTables = isRestaurantType`), but a shop's owner phone cannot park sales (Fiar replaces Guardar, S3), so in practice it never fires for a shop.
- Restaurant S10 problems 2, 3, 4, 6, 7 are fixed per CLAUDE.md REGISTER-2 / FIX-3 (one "Debe haber en la caja" word, owner name, business name in the summary, permission "Abrir y cerrar caja", pt "Sobra").

## 2. Demo path (CO, ~10 taps)
After a day with 3 cash sales, one fiado sale and one abono of $ 10.000: Mi negocio → "Caja abierta" → "Cerrar caja" → 1, 5, 0, 000 → "Cuadrado" → "Cerrar caja" → "Cerrar caja" → summary shows "Abonos de fiado $ 10.000" and "En fiado (sin cobrar) −$ 18.500" → "Enviar por WhatsApp".

None new for shops found. (The fiado lines appear only when > 0, which is right.)

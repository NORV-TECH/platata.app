# T8 — "Fiado" (put a sale on the customer's tab)

Checked against code at commit 6736ddfb. Paths under `app/src/main/java/com/example/`.

## 1. What it does, who, where
- At the pay step the cashier attaches a client and taps "Fiar a Juan". The sale is booked as a normal PAID sale with payment method "Fiado" AND, in the same write, a debt in Juan's name. The goods leave the shelf (stock moves, T6). The Clients list then shows who owes how much, coloured by age.
- RETAIL and PHARMACY only (`fiadoEnabled = true` in RetailRules; restaurant/services false).
- Owner's phone: full (sell on tab, see balances, the "TE DEBEN" header).
- Counter tablet / employee phone: selling on tab needs the employee grant `canFiar`, which has **no switch anywhere** (Team form carries it unchanged, `ui/screens/team/TeamManagementScreen.kt:1173-1179`; the permission chips are cancel/close/comp/stock/edit-customer/refund/reports/void only). So in practice ONLY the owner phone can fiar. Balances are shown only on the owner device (`CustomerCrmScreen.kt:119`, `fiadoMoneyOn` needs `DeviceRole.OWNER`).
- Pharmacy = identical.

## 2. Setup before the demo
- Retail/pharmacy shop, products, register open.
- At least one client with a name (WhatsApp optional). A typed "order name" is NOT a client; Fiar needs a real client from the list.
- Nothing to switch on: the "Fiado" payment row is seeded inactive and has no switch (`data/repo/SeedStore.kt:99`); the Fiar button is the only door.
- For the list demo: 3–4 clients, two with older tabs (use "＋ Monto manual", T9) so the age colours show.

## 3. Country differences
- Same in every country (fiado is by type, not country). Words: es "Fiar / Fiado / TE DEBEN", pt "Fiar / Fiado / TE DEVEM", en "Put on tab / Tab / THEY OWE YOU".
- Money: CO "$ 45.000", CL "$45.000", MX "$450.00", PE "S/ 45.00", AR "$ 4.500,00", EC "$45,00", PA/US/CA "$45.00", BR "R$ 45,00".
- The cashier can't put a card or QR sale on tab; the tab is its own button.

## 6. UX/UI problems
1. **BLOCKS A DEMO** — On the owner phone, "Fiar a Juan" books the sale but nothing navigates: `CheckoutSheetBody.kt:339` calls `viewModel.fiarCurrentCart()` (fire-and-forget, `CartActions.kt:684-690`), and the pay sheet in `HomeScreen.kt:1225-1290` only navigates to Sale complete from `onCheckout`. `finalizeOrder` empties the cart, so the cashier is left on the pay sheet of an empty $0 sale and never sees "Fiaste $X / AHORA TE DEBE" (the tablet and employee phone watch `lastCompletedOrderId`; HomeScreen does not). Verified by reading; not run on a phone. Fix: in CheckoutSheetBody await `finalizeOrder()` from the Fiar tap and call the same path as `onCheckout` (close sheet → ReceiptOptions).
2. **BLOCKS A DEMO on tablet / employee phone** — the PAY step's Fiar button ignores the `canFiar` grant: `saveEnabled = !fiadoEnabled || fiadoCustomer != null` (`CheckoutSheetBody.kt:337`), while `finalizeOrder` refuses without `canFiar` (`CheckoutActions.kt:185`) and returns silently. "Fiar a Juan" looks live, the tap does nothing (the same bug the cart chip fixed as BUG-F1, `CartSheetContent.kt:366-379`). Fix: add `uiState.miniPosPermissions?.canFiar != false` to `saveEnabled`.
3. small — There is no switch to let an employee sell on tab (`canFiar` has no chip). If that is intended, the dim "Fiar" should say why. Fix: either add the permission chip or hide Fiar off the owner device.
4. small — A greyed "Fiar" gives no hint when tapped; the hint string "Agrega un cliente para fiar" (`fiado_no_customer_snack`) exists but `fiadoNoCustomerEvent` (`PosViewModel.kt:242`) is collected by no screen. Fix: make the dim button tappable to show that line.
5. small — The pay screen's client field does not show "ya te debe $X" (only the cart sheet does), so on the owner's retail flow (Charge → pay screen) the cashier fiars again without seeing the old balance. Fix: show the same amber line under the attached client in `CheckoutCustomerField`.
6. small — es sort chip "Más plata" is Colombian slang; MX/AR/CL say "dinero". Fix: "Más deuda" (pt already says "Maior dívida").
7. small — "1 llevan más de 30 días" (`fiado_over_30` is a string, not a plural). Fix: `<plurals>` ("1 lleva…").
8. small (possible) — A staff PIN session on the owner phone counts as the OWNER device, so it sees every balance and can Forgive (gate is `DeviceRole.OWNER`, `CustomerCrmScreen.kt:119`, not `ReportsAudience.isOwnerView`). Fix: use `isOwnerView`.
9. small — `fiarCurrentCart` finds the tender by English name `getPaymentMethodByName("Fiado")` (`CartActions.kt:686`); choke point 15 says by `systemKey`; a missing row silently does nothing. Fix: `getPaymentMethodBySystemKey("fiado")`.

# T9 — "Abonos al fiado" (Settle / Forgive / Manual amount on the customer page)

Checked against code at commit 6736ddfb. Paths under `app/src/main/java/com/example/`.

## 1. What it does, who, where
- On a client's page the owner records money the client brings in ("Saldar" — any amount, paid oldest tab first, with the tender: cash, QR, another method), writes off a debt ("Perdonar deuda", asks twice), or types an old debt from the paper notebook ("＋ Monto manual", not a sale).
- Owner's phone only (`fiadoMoneyOn` = retail rules + `DeviceRole.OWNER` + not the picker, `ui/screens/customers/CustomerCrmScreen.kt:119`). Tablet / employee phone see the client page without the money block.
- Pharmacy = identical.

## 2. Setup before the demo
- A retail shop with a client who owes (a Fiar sale, T8, or "＋ Monto manual").
- Register OPEN on the owner phone if the abono is cash and you want it in the drawer: a cash settle is stored with the open shift's id (`ui/pos/CustomerFiadoActions.kt:87-90`); with no open shift it is stored with no shift and counts in no drawer.
- Payment chips shown = active tenders except card, fiado, comp (`FiadoMoneyScreens.kt:142-145`). Cash is preselected.

## 3. Country differences
- Same everywhere. The keypad follows the pay-screen rule (`MoneyKeypadEntry`): whole units typed (1-2-5 = 125); "000" key for COP/CLP, decimal key elsewhere.
- Money: CO "$ 15.000", CL "$15.000", MX "$150.00", PE "S/ 150.00", AR "$ 1.500,00", EC "$15,00", PA/US/CA "$15.00", BR "R$ 15,00".

- Cash abono → the open shift's drawer ("expected cash") and Reports; other tenders show under their own method; a write-off appears in Reports as "Fiado perdonado"; a manual debt is not a sale (`data/repo/FiadoStore.kt:67-105`).

## 6. UX/UI problems
1. small — "Salda 1 fiados completos" / "Settles 1 full tabs": `fiado_saldar_result` is a string with %d, not a plural, and with a partial payment it reads "Salda 0 fiados completos" (`FiadoMoneyScreens.kt:293`). Fix: `<plurals>`, and hide the first half when 0.
2. small (possible) — A CASH abono taken while the register is closed is saved with no shift (`CustomerFiadoActions.kt:88`) and lands in no drawer, with no warning. Fix: warn "La caja está cerrada; este efectivo no entra en ninguna caja" or ask to open it.
3. small — The Forgive first tap changes nothing visible on the button (only a grey line appears under it), easy to miss on a small phone. Fix: change the button label to "Toca otra vez para perdonar" while `confirming`.
4. small — "Más plata" sort and the long "Saldar — Juan Pérez Rodríguez" title (ellipsised by the top bar). Fix: title "Saldar" with the name as subtitle.
5. small — The purchase list on the page has no "Fiado" mark: a tab sale is status PAID, so `PurchaseRow` shows no badge (`CustomerPage.kt:212-214`); the owner can't see which purchases are on the tab. Fix: show the `fiado_badge_*` chip there too.
6. small — The amber block and the page take the currency from the global `CurrencyConfig.current` (`FiadoMoneyScreens.kt:70`, `CustomerPage.kt:74`), the same pattern PHONEFIX-1 fixed on the Register card; safe today because the page is entered after a switch. Fix: pass `uiState.currencyCode`.

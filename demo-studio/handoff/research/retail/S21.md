## 1. Differences
- **Scanning works offline** on every device: known barcodes are read from the phone's own catalog; NEW barcodes are looked up in the downloaded dictionary file (`ui/pos/DictionaryDownload.kt:43`, local file) — no internet needed once it is on the phone. The first download (~30 MB) needs internet, and the shop cannot be created without it (S1).
- **Fiado works offline on the owner's phone** (a local write, `FiadoStore`); a settle in cash lands in the open drawer. Nothing to sync (fiado lives only on the owner's phone, S6).
- Stock moves offline on the device that sold (S19 §1.4): no change.
- No kitchen line for a shop, so the restaurant's "la cocina lo recibe cuando vuelva internet" note (`held_sales_strip_kitchen`, restaurants only) does not show — correct.
- A product created by scan on an employee phone reaches the owner (and other phones) only when that phone is back online (shared `synced_products`).

## 2. Demo path (owner phone, 6 taps)
Airplane mode → grey line "Sin internet" on Home → scan a soda (adds) → scan a NEW barcode (name found in the dictionary, type price) → "Cobrar" → "Efectivo" → "Venta completada"; "Tarjeta" shows "Sin conexión".

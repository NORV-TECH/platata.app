# S3 — Cobrar en efectivo (SHOP / PHARMACY, owner's phone)

Commit read: `6736ddfb`. Paths under `app/src/main/java/com/example/`.
The PAY screen (`CheckoutSheetBody`), cash keypad, Exacto, change, cash rounding and "¡Venta completada!" are the same as restaurant S3. What differs is how products get into the sale, the Save button, and stock.

## 1. What differs
- **No tab row, no dish grid on the owner's phone.** A shop's register is ONE screen, `ScanCheckoutTab` (`ui/screens/home/ScanCheckoutTab.kt:39-99`; `HomeScreen.kt:366` forces tab 1, `:609` hides the TabRow for retail). Products enter by: the camera ("Agregar productos"), "Sin código" (search-and-sell shelf for loose goods), or "Teclado" (typed amount). There is no tap-a-tile library on the owner phone (the Catálogo grid exists only on the counter tablet and the waiter phone).
- **"Guardar" becomes "Fiar".** On the pay screen the Save button reads "Fiar" (dim, inert) or "Fiar a Ana" once a client is attached (`CheckoutSheetBody.kt:322-341`). So a shop cannot park a sale as an open ticket from the owner phone. See S6.
- **Stock moves.** A finished sale takes each counted product off the shelf (`data/repo/StockStore.kt:76-87`, called once from PlaceOrderUseCase). Weighed lines move by the weight. Uncounted / "don't count" items never move.
- No dining options, no kitchen ("Enviar a cocina" never shows: the fiado label wins, `CheckoutSheetBody.kt:328-334`). Card tip defaults OFF for shops (`RetailRules.kt:32`).

## 2. Setup
Register open (S2). Dictionary downloaded (S1). Ideally 5–10 products already scanned once with prices (first scan of an unknown code asks name/price/category and SAVES the product — `ScannerScreen.kt:207-215`, `ScanCartScreen.kt:62-82`).

## 3. Country differences
Same as restaurant S3 (money formats; cash rounding CL/PE/CA). At this commit Panama prints "$" (CLAUDE.md FIX-3). Scan price SUGGESTION ("Sugerido $ 2.400") only in Colombia (`data/PriceSuggestions.kt`, `ScannerScreen.kt:204`); everywhere else "Sin precio sugerido".

## 6. UX problems
1. **A shop cannot save/park a sale on the owner phone**: the Save slot always becomes "Fiar" for retail (`CheckoutSheetBody.kt:328-341`; `CartSheetContent.kt:363-396` same on tablet/waiter). A customer who "goes to fetch money" has no hold. Possible product gap; ask Monti.
2. **Owner phone has no tappable product grid** for regular non-barcode items (bread, eggs by unit); each sale must go through the "Sin código" search (`ScanCheckoutTab.kt:267-279`). Slow for a barrio shop's top-10 items. Possible: offer favourites.
3. Dictionary product names include brand names (`ProductLookupRepository` column `brands`; overlay locks the name) — real brands will appear on demo screens. Demo note, not a bug.
4. "Add to Cart" / "CARRITO" (Title Case, "cart") vs the app's "venta / sale" elsewhere (`scan_focused_add`, `scan_cart_label`). Small.
5. Cash-rounding line and keypad grammar from restaurant S3 are fixed at this commit (CLAUDE.md FIX-3 items 4, 7).

# T10 — "Recordatorio de fiado por WhatsApp" (Remind button)

Checked against code at commit 6736ddfb. Paths under `app/src/main/java/com/example/`.

## 1. What it does, who, where
- On a debtor's page the owner taps "Recordarle". WhatsApp opens on the client's chat with a ready text: hello, how much they owe, the shop's name, thanks, and the Platata signature line. The owner presses Send in WhatsApp himself (nothing is sent automatically, no clipboard).
- Owner's phone only (it lives in the amber block, `ui/screens/customers/FiadoMoneyScreens.kt:104-109`, shown only on the owner device for retail/pharmacy).
- Only when the client has a real WhatsApp number (`hasRealWhatsapp`; a `NONUM_` placeholder hides the button).
- Pharmacy = identical.

## 2. Setup before the demo
- A client who owes (T8/T9) and has a WhatsApp number saved on the client form.
- WhatsApp on the phone (the link is `https://api.whatsapp.com/send?phone=<digits>&text=…`, `utils/WhatsappOpen.kt:19-22`; without WhatsApp it opens the browser page).
- The shop's language = the language chosen in Settings › Language (changing the app language also sets `business_language`, `ui/navigation/SettingsNavGraph.kt:124-128`).

## 3. Country differences
- The TEXT follows the shop's language, not the phone's (`utils/FiadoRemindMessage.kt:12-16`):
  - es: "Hola Juan Pérez, te recuerdo que tienes pendiente $ 37.900 en Tienda Doña Rosa. ¡Gracias!"
  - pt: "Oi Juan Pérez, passando para lembrar que você tem R$ 37,90 pendente em Mercadinho da Rosa. Obrigado!"
  - en: "Hi Juan Pérez, a reminder that you owe $37.90 at Rosa's Corner Store. Thanks!"
  - then a blank line and "Hecho con Platata · platata.app" | "Feito com Platata · platata.app" | "Made with Platata · platata.app" (same language, `utils/WhatsappSignatureText.kt:24-25`).
- The amount uses the shop currency's format (CO "$ 37.900", CL "$37.900", MX "$379.00", PE "S/ 379.00", AR "$ 3.790,00", EC "$37,90", PA/US/CA "$37.90", BR "R$ 37,90").
- The number dialled is the saved digits with country code (`WhatsappReceipt.directNumber`); the client form already turns local numbers into international ones by the shop's country (WA-FIX-1: Argentina adds 9, Mexico 52, trunk 0 removed, etc.).
- The name is the client's full saved name; the shop name is the business name (`activeWorkspaceName`), not the receipt's store name.

## 5. Demo path (owner phone, 4 taps)
1. Flag → "Clientes" → Juan Pérez (green/red amount).
2. "Recordarle".
3. WhatsApp opens on Juan with "Hola Juan Pérez, te recuerdo que tienes pendiente $ 37.900 en Tienda Doña Rosa. ¡Gracias!" + "Hecho con Platata · platata.app".
4. Tap Send in WhatsApp. (For a Brazil video: set the app language to Português and a BRL shop → "Oi Juan Pérez, passando para lembrar…".)

## 6. UX/UI problems
1. small — No record that a reminder was sent (no "Recordado hace 3 días" on the page or the list), so the owner can nag the same client twice in a day. Fix: store the last-reminded date per client and show it under the button.
2. small — The greeting uses the FULL name ("Hola Juan Pérez") while every other fiado screen uses the first name ("Fiar a Juan", "Juan quedó al día"). Fix: first name, like `fiado_quedo_al_dia`.
3. small — The text names the shop by its business name in the app (`activeWorkspaceName`), which owners often type as a short internal label ("Tienda 2"). Possible. Fix: use the receipt store name when set, else the business name.
4. small — Language: the reminder can only be in the language the owner uses the app in (one setting drives both). A US shop working in English with Spanish-speaking customers cannot send Spanish reminders. Fix (later): a "language of messages to customers" setting.
5. small — No reminder from the Clients list itself (one tap per debtor via the page). Fine for now.

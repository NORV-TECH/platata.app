**Same as restaurant S17** (Settings › "Impuestos", "+ Nuevo", "¿Este impuesto ya está en tus precios?", per-item switch "Cobrar impuesto sobre este artículo"), except below. Restaurant problems 1, 2 (receipt wears the tax's own name, FIX-1 item 5), 3, 4 (comma and 100 cap, BACKOFFICE-1), 5 (edit a tax), 6 ("incluido" on the cart line), 7 (presets) are fixed at this commit (BACKOFFICE-2 A).

## 1. Differences
- The tax screen has no business-type rule (no `rules.` in `ui/screens/settings/TaxesManagementScreen.kt` or `sync/TaxPresets.kt`): a tienda in Colombia gets the same two chips **"IVA 19 %"** and **"Impoconsumo 8 %"** (`sync/TaxPresets.kt:17`), with the "one or the other" note.
- A product has ONE switch `isTaxable` (default true, `data/Product.kt:39`), no choice of WHICH rate. Every product the scanner or the dictionary adds is taxable by default.

## 2. Demo path (owner phone, CO, 7 taps)
Ajustes → "Impuestos" → "+ Nuevo" → chip "IVA 19 %" → "Sí, ya está incluido" → "Guardar" (toast "Impuesto activo. Se aplica a los artículos marcados con impuesto.") → scan a soda → "Cobrar": the pay screen line reads "Impuesto (IVA) incluido $ 380" (`home_tax_named_included`) and the receipt "IVA incluido". (Then open a fruit item → switch "Cobrar impuesto sobre este artículo" off, to show an exempt product.)

## 3. UX problems
1. **Impoconsumo 8 % is offered to a tienda / pharmacy** (`TaxPresets.kt:17`) — it is the restaurant/bar consumption tax in Colombia; a tendero who taps it charges a tax he does not owe. Small (wrong demo if tapped). Fix: preset per type (`rules`).
2. **No per-product rate**: Colombian shops mix 19 %, 5 % and excluded goods; Mexico mixes 16 % and 0 %; with one switch per product the owner can only say "all at the shop rate" or "none". Possible product gap, not a bug. Pharmacy: most medicines are excluded from IVA in Colombia, so every medicine must be switched off by hand.

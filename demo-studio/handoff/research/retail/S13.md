## 5. UX problems
1. **Debts of 0–14 days are painted GREEN** (`CustomerCrmScreen.kt:654`) — green elsewhere means paid ("FIADO SALDADO", "a favor"). A tendero can read a fresh debt as settled. Fix: neutral/amber for fresh, red for old.
2. Possible: a staff PIN session on the owner's phone still sees every debt and can Saldar / Perdonar (gate is device role only, `CustomerCrmScreen.kt:119`; `ui/pos/CustomerFiadoActions.kt:93` no check). Fix: `ReportsAudience.isOwnerView` + `canReceivePayment` for Saldar.
3. "Perdonar deuda" has no fingerprint even with Security on, while deleting a client does (`CustomerCrmScreen.kt:1056`). Fix: same phone-lock prompt. Small.
4. Not plurals: "Salda 1 fiados completos", "1 fiados · … hace 1 días", "1 llevan más de 30 días" (`fiado_saldar_result`, `fiado_page_debts_age`, `fiado_over_30` are plain strings). Small.
5. List header and debtor amounts format with the global `CurrencyConfig.current` (`CustomerCrmScreen.kt:303, 668-689`) — same bug class PHONEFIX-1 fixed on the register card; possible wrong symbol right after a business switch. Small.
6. Clients list on tablet / waiter phone shows no money at all (by design) — a cashier cannot tell a customer what he owes. Possible gap.

## 1. Stock on a refund / void (`data/repo/StockStore.kt:89-116`, `ui/screens/transactions/TransactionsScreen.kt:1394-1416, 1600-1612`)
- **Full refund or void** puts every counted item back on the shelf — but ONLY on the device that rang the sale (`returnSaleToStock` checks `order.originDeviceId == thisDeviceId`). A sale rung on the counter tablet and refunded from the owner's phone returns nothing to the owner's Inventario.
- **Partial refund puts NOTHING back**, even if the cashier ticked items on the "Artículos" tab (the app stores only an amount). When the sale touches counted items the confirm box adds: "El inventario no cambia. Una devolución parcial no regresa productos al estante: la app solo guarda el valor devuelto, no cuáles productos volvieron. Corrige el conteo a mano en Inventario." | pt "…Corrija a contagem à mão em Estoque." | en "Stock stays as it is. … Fix the count by hand in Stock." (`tx_refund_stock_partial`).
- On the Wompi road (CO card) the confirm box is skipped, so this note is never seen (CLAUDE.md S7-B7, accepted).

## 2. Fiado sale refund
- Destination line for a sale whose tab is still open: "Se descuenta de la cuenta de Ana" | "Sai da conta de Ana" | "Comes off Ana's tab" (`sync/RefundDestinationLine.kt:37-40`, `refund_goes_tab`).
- What really happens (`data/repo/OrderStore.kt:394-398`): a FULL refund closes the sale's debt (badge "FIADO ANULADO · Ana"); a **PARTIAL refund leaves the debt exactly as it was** (Monti's round-274 decision).
- Sales list rows of fiado sales carry a badge (`TransactionsScreen.kt:199-232`): amber "FIADO · Ana" | "FIADO · Ana" | "TAB · Ana" (open, bg #FFF6E5, text #B07500); green "FIADO SALDADO · 12 oct" | "FIADO QUITADO · …" | "TAB SETTLED · …" (#1A7F3C); grey "FIADO PERDONADO · …"; "FIADO ANULADO · Ana".

## 5. UX problems
1. **BLOCKS A DEMO (money words): partial refund of an open-tab fiado sale says "Se descuenta de la cuenta de Ana" but the debt does not change** (`RefundDestinationLine.kt:37-40` ignores partial vs full; `OrderStore.kt:396-397`). 2. Ticking items on the "Artículos" tab looks like returning those products to the shelf, yet a partial refund moves no stock (the note sits only in the confirm box) (`TransactionsScreen.kt:1395-1401`). Fix: show the note on the refund screen itself, next to the item list.
3. A refund done on the owner phone of a sale rung on a tablet/waiter phone returns no stock anywhere (`StockStore.kt:108`); nothing says so. Small, but owners will notice wrong counts.
4. English note says "Fix the count by hand in Stock" while the panel row is "Inventory" (`tx_refund_stock_partial` en vs `scan_row_inventory`). Small.

# S7 — Recibo por WhatsApp o impreso (SHOP / PHARMACY)

Commit read: `6736ddfb`. Paths under `app/src/main/java/com/example/`.
**Same as restaurant S7** (Sale complete stack, WhatsApp/SMS route by country, print, "Venta #N" resend), except:

## 1. Differences
- **Weighed lines** print their working instead of "@ $x c/u": e.g. "1,5 kg × $ 7.000" (`utils/ReceiptFormatter.kt:119-135, 234-235`). Typical for fruit / meat sold through "Sin código" or a by-weight product.
- **Fiado sale**: the Sale complete hero is "Fiaste $X / a Ana / AHORA TE DEBE $Y" (S6). The receipt itself prints the payment-method line "Fiado" (localized, `ui/pos/ReceiptBuilder.kt:120,228`) but NOT the client's new balance.
- Printers: a shop's printer page offers "Recibos" and "Comandas" for every type since PRINT-2/PRINT-5 (`printers_v2` list); a shop normally uses only receipts. The cash-drawer pulse applies to Cash only by default (MKT2-1).
- At this commit WhatsApp no longer ends the sale (PAY-1 item 2), and the viewer offers SMS + WhatsApp in US/CA — restaurant S7 problems 1 and 3 are closed.

## 2. Demo path (CO, 6 taps)
Sell 1 kg tomate + bread in cash → "¡Venta completada!" → "WhatsApp" → type 300 123 4567 → "Enviar" → WhatsApp shows "1 kg × $ 3.000 … Hecho con Platata · platata.app" → back → "Nueva venta".

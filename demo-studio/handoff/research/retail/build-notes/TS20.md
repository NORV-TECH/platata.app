# TS20 — Vender en línea (shop / pharmacy)

## Built
- Owner phone: panel → "Vender en línea" → (outside US/CA) the locked-store dialog "Agregar conexión" — the store opens when a tablet or an
  employee phone uses the third connection (conn_dlg_store words) → switch on → address → hours → "Encender la tienda" (the page then shows
  "Fuera de tu tienda online" with the weighed items) → Compartir → the customer's web page (non-weighed products only) → add → name and
  phone → "Hacer pedido" → owner Home with the order notification. 9 beats. Devices: owner phone + customer's phone browser.
- Countries: US/CA have no lock step (FREE): beat 2 is explain-only and `TS20-US.json` carries the es voice for US. Pharmacy: no weighed
  items, so no "Fuera de tu tienda online" card (beat 3 text adapts). Checked at 6736ddfb.

## Left out and why
- Paying online: a shop's online orders are pay at pickup (`onlinePayOptions`). Sold-out items still orderable / weighed items absent
  (#33, already sent): only in-stock, per-item products are tapped.
- R1–R7: nothing in this page changes.

## New UX/UI problems
- The Sell-online page lists EVERY category of the shop, so a new shop shows about 20 rows "0 de 0 en línea" (the 24 ready-made categories)
  before the "Fuera de tu tienda online" card, which ends up far down (`sync/OnlineStoreSellable.kt:100-111` maps all categories;
  `ui/screens/online/SellOnlineScreen.kt` draws them). Fix: skip categories with no products. Small.

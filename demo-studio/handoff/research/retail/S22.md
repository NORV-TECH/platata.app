Shop differences:

## 1. Differences
- What a shop's backup carries: the whole shop `.db` (products with barcodes and stock counts, fiado debts and payments, customers, sales) plus the asset folders `ProductImages/`, `Invoices/` (supplier invoice photos), `Receipts/`, `QrPayment/` (`work/DriveBackupHelper.kt:280-284`). Fiado is therefore safe only through this backup (it lives on the owner's phone alone, S6).
- **Not in the backup: the barcode dictionary** (~30 MB, a device file, `data/ProductLookupRepository.kt:179-182`). After "Restaurar un negocio" on a new phone the shop's own products scan at once, but a NEW barcode has no name until the dictionary is downloaded again (the scanner's download door). The restore flow does not offer it (no dictionary step in `ui/screens/settings/RestoreWelcomeStep.kt`).

## 2. Demo path (owner phone, ~8 taps)
Flag → amber "Tus datos no tienen respaldo" row → "Conectar Google Drive" → pick account → allow → "Respaldar ahora" → "Último respaldo: hoy 14:05". Say aloud: "tu cuaderno de fiados queda guardado en tu Drive".

# T5 — "Vender por peso" (weight entry)

Checked against code at commit **6736ddfb**. Paths under `app/src/main/java/com/example/`.

## 1. What it does, who, where
- A product whose unit is weight, volume, length or time (`data/UnitOfSale.kt:44-66`: groups "Weight & Mass", "Volume & Liquid", "Length & Size", "Time & Services") opens a **full-screen weight keypad** when tapped: he types 0,5 (or taps a quick weight) and sees the price × weight before adding. Counted units (Unidad, Docena, Manojo, Paquete…) just add one.
- Screen: `ui/screens/home/WeightEntryDialog.kt:59-241` (full-screen `Dialog`, G-06). Line added by `CartActions.addItemToCartWithWeight` (`ui/pos/CartActions.kt:222-260`): one cart line, price = rate × weight, the order row remembers rate + weight; the receipt prints "0,5 Libra × $ 3.000" (`utils/ReceiptFormatter.kt:125-130`).
- Who / where (no scale integration, typed only):
  - **Owner phone**: retail has no product grid (tabs Teclado | Escanear), so weighed items are reached from **Sin código** (his items grid, `BarlessShelfScreen.kt:159-163, 319-335`) or Favoritos. A catalogue pick on Sin código asks the weight inside its price sheet instead (T4).
  - **Counter tablet**: tab "Catálogo" → tap a weighed product (`MamaPosScreen.kt:855-858` → `ProductSheetHost.kt:256-278`).
  - **Employee phone**: its library tab (`MiniPosSellScreen.kt:258-260`).
  - A **barcoded** weighed product scanned with the camera does NOT open the keypad (added as 1) — see §6.
- Retail = Pharmacy.

## 2. Setup before the demo
- Tienda, register open, one product sold by weight: either sold once from Sin código with "Por: Libra" (T4), or created in Artículos › + Nuevo with **Unidad de venta = Libra (lb)** / **Kilogramo (kg)** and a price per unit (e.g. Tomate, $ 3.000).

## 3. Country differences
- Decimal key and numbers use the country's mark: "," in CO, CL, EC, AR, BR; "." in MX, PE, PA, US, CA (`utils/decimalSeparatorFor`; display only, `WeightEntryDialog.kt:49-54`).
- Unit: Colombia defaults to **Libra (lb, ~500 g, no kg conversion)**; other Spanish countries and BR Kilogramo; US/CA list Pound first (T11).
- Money: CO "$ 1.500", CL "$1.500", MX/PE/PA/US/CA "$1.50" (PE "S/ 1.50"), EC "$1,50", AR "$ 1,50", BR "R$ 1,50".

## 5. Demo path (counter tablet, then owner phone)
1. Tablet › **Catálogo** → tap **Tomate** → keypad "Tomate · $ 3.000 por lb".
2. Tap **0,5 lb** → big "0,50", "Total: $ 1.500".
3. Clear with ⌫, type **1 , 2 5** → "1,25", "Total: $ 3.750".
4. **Agregar a la venta · $ 3.750** → line in the ticket on the right.
5. Owner phone › Escanear › **Sin código** → tap his **Tomate** card → same keypad → **0,5 lb** → add.
6. **Cobrar · $ 1.500** → cash → receipt shows "0,5 Libra × $ 3.000".

## 6. UX/UI problems (verified)
1. The unit under the big number is the **raw stored name** (`WeightEntryDialog.kt:119-124`): a Spanish shop outside CO reads "Kilogram", a US shop with Libra reads "Libra", in English. Fix: `localizedUnitName`. — small, visible in a demo outside CO.
2. The weight is invisible in the cart and pay screen (row shows only name + price, qty 1). The cashier cannot check "was it half a libra?" before charging. Fix: show "0,5 lb × $ 3.000" under the name (the data is on the line: `soldWeight`, `unitRate`). — small.
3. The receipt line uses the raw unit name too ("0,5 Kilogram × …", `ReceiptFormatter.kt:125-130` gets `product.unitOfSale`). Fix: localized name or abbreviation.
4. The hidden variation name is built with `String.format("%.2f")` in the phone locale + the raw unit ("Regular (0.50 Kilogram)", `CartActions.kt:241`). Not on the main screens I checked; possible on any screen that prints `variation.name` (e.g. a reopened ticket). Fix: build it from the abbreviation and the country's decimal mark.
5. Quick weights are the same four numbers for every unit (0,25/0,5/1/2,5) — fine for lb/kg, odd for grams, litres or hours (`:140`). Fix: per-unit presets (e.g. 100/250/500 g).
6. A barcoded product sold by weight, when scanned, goes in as 1 unit with no keypad (`ScanCartScreen.kt:103`, no measure check). Fix: open this keypad on scan too.
7. The button says "Agregar a la venta" here but "Agregar al carrito" on the Sin código price sheet and the scan form. Fix: one wording.
8. Raw `16.dp`/`12.dp`/`8.dp` instead of `DesignTokens` (`:102-163`) — code style only.

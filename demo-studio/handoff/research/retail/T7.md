# T7 — "Proveedores y facturas" (supplier invoices)

Checked against code at commit 6736ddfb. Paths under `app/src/main/java/com/example/`.

## 1. What it does, who, where
- The owner photographs a supplier's paper invoice. The photo is kept; the app reads the supplier name, the date and the total (QR on the paper if any, then on-phone text recognition, then a background AI read over the internet) and files it under that supplier. A list shows every invoice by month (with a month total) or by supplier (with "This year" total). The totals go to the accountant sheet.
- **Does stock go up from an invoice? NO.** The invoice record has no product lines: `SupplierInvoice` = supplier, amount, date, photo, QR text, OCR text, sources (`data/SupplierInvoice.kt:120-147`). No file under `ui/screens/suppliers/`, `data/repo/SupplierStore.kt` or `work/SupplierInvoiceReadWorker.kt` touches stock (grep "stock" = 0 hits). Stock goes up only by hand: Inventario › tap the number › "Compré +N" (see T6).
- Owner's phone only: the panel row and the list are refused for a Mini POS phone and for a staff PIN session (`data/SupplierInvoiceList.kt:69-70`, `mayBrowseInvoices`). Counter tablet: no door. Invoices are not synced to other devices (CLAUDE.md, D-1).
- Pharmacy = identical (`hasSupplierInvoiceRow = true` in RetailRules, no override).

## 2. Setup before the demo
- Nothing to switch on. A retail shop has the panel row "Facturas".
- Camera permission (asked on the first Scan).
- Internet for the background read (name/total). Without internet the photo and any QR date/total are kept; name and total stay "Toca para agregar" until the read runs later (WorkManager, CONNECTED constraint).
- For a smooth demo, pre-load two suppliers' invoices (e.g. "Lácteos del Valle", "Panadería San José") so the list has months and totals; then scan one live.

## 3. Country differences
- Same flow everywhere. The QR reader is deliberately generic, no per-country fiscal format (`utils/SupplierQrParser.kt:16-21`): it lifts an ISO date or a value labelled total/valor/monto/name… if present. A Colombian DIAN QR gives the date and the duplicate check (CUFE) but no name or total (`work/SupplierInvoiceReadWorker.kt` header).
- Money format: CO "$ 1.250.000", CL "$1.250.000", MX "$1,250,000.00", PE "S/ 1,250,000.00", AR "$ 1.250.000,00", EC "$1.250.000,00", PA/US/CA "$1,250,000.00", BR "R$ 1.250.000,00".
- Words: es "Factura(s) / Proveedor", pt "Nota(s) / Nota fiscal / Fornecedor", en "Invoice(s) / Supplier".

## 6. UX/UI problems
1. small — The app can read an invoice but never offers to add its goods to stock; a tendero will expect "I scanned the milk invoice, the milk went up". Today the two are unrelated (no link anywhere). Fix (later): after Done, a "¿Sumar al inventario?" step, or at least a line saying stock is counted separately.
2. small — The search field in the "Todas" tab says "Por nombre o teléfono…" but searches the supplier NAME only (`data/SupplierInvoiceList.kt:53-60`). Fix: a tab-specific hint "Por proveedor" or also match phone.
3. small — Camera ✕ is announced to screen readers as "Atrás/Back" (`SupplierInvoiceCameraScreen.kt:227`) while it shows a ✕. Fix: `common_close`.
4. small — The name and total read needs internet and runs silently in the background; offline the screen shows "Leyéndola…" only if a read was queued, then a dash/"Toca para agregar" later with no "will read when online" message. Possible. Fix: say "Lo leemos cuando haya internet" when offline.
5. small — Month header color is `colorScheme.primary` (#1C1C1E, near-black) — reads fine, but the "+1 sin total" orange #E65100 sits next to it on a pale band; ok. No fix needed.

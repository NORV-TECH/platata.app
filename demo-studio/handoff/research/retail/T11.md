# T11 — "Unidades de medida" (unit of sale, Libra vs Pound, new unit)

Checked against code at commit **6736ddfb**. Paths under `app/src/main/java/com/example/`.

## 1. What it does, who, where
- Every product has a **unit of sale**. "Por unidad" = sold by the piece; a weight/volume/length/time unit = sold by amount (opens the weight keypad, T5); counted units (Docena, Paquete, Caja, Manojo…) add one at a time. The rule lives in ONE place: `data/UnitOfSale.kt:44-66`.
- The item form shows a **short list of 7 units for the shop's country**, then "Más unidades…", "+ Nueva unidad" and "Administrar unidades" (`ui/screens/items/UnitOfSalePicker.kt:62-189`, rules `sync/UnitPicker.kt`).
- Owner phone only (item form, Artículos). Employees with stock permission see units only as labels. Retail = Pharmacy (services products have no unit chip).

## 2. Setup before the demo
- Nothing: 40 units are seeded in every new shop (`data/PosDatabase.kt:3216-3255`), grouped Weight & Mass, Volume & Liquid, Count & Quantity, Food & Portions, Time & Services, Length & Size. Two of them abbreviate "lb": **Pound** and **Libra**; each country sees only one.

## 3. Country differences — `sync/UnitPicker.kt:17-55`
| Country | Short list (order) | Hidden duplicate |
|---|---|---|
| US, CA (`POUND_COUNTRIES`) | Per Item · Pound (lb) · Ounce (oz) · Kilogram (kg) · Gram (g) · Liter (l) · Fluid Ounce (fl oz) | Libra |
| CO, MX, PE, CL, EC, AR, PA, BR | Por unidad · Kilogramo (kg) · Gramo (g) · **Libra (lb)** · Litro (l) · Mililitro (ml) · Docena (dz) | Pound |
- **Libra** (Colombian ~500 g) has **no kg/g conversion** in the stock keypad on purpose (`StockScreen.shortUnit`, pin `LibraHasNoMassChipsE19Test`); Pound converts at 453.59 g.
- The No-barcode page picks Libra for weighed items only when the currency is COP, Kilogram everywhere else (`data/NoBarcodeCatalog.kt:259-274`).
- Words: es "Libra", pt "Libra", en "Pound" (the Libra row is shown as "Pound" in English, `unit_name_libra`); in a Spanish US shop the Pound row reads "Libra" (`unit_name_pound`).

## 6. UX/UI problems (verified)
1. The duplicate check compares the typed name with the STORED English names (`sync/UnitPicker.kt:94-98`), so a Spanish owner can create "Docena" (abbr "doc") next to the seeded Dozen, which already shows as "Docena (dz)" — two identical-looking rows. And when a name does collide the message still talks about the abbreviation ("Ya existe una unidad con esta abreviatura."). Fix: compare against `localizedUnitName` too; two messages. — small.
2. The No-barcode price sheet ignores this whole design: full unit table, red English headings, two "Libra" rows (`BarlessShelfScreen.kt:494-547`); and its default weight unit is Kilogram in the US/CA (`NoBarcodeCatalog.kt:262`). Fix: reuse `UnitPicker` and `usesPounds`. — small (see T4).
3. "Libra" is in the short list of Brazil, Mexico, Chile, Peru, Argentina, Ecuador, Panama, where shops weigh in kilos (`UnitPicker.kt:20-22`, rule "everywhere except US/CA"). Fix: Libra only for CO (Kilogram/Gram/Litro…/Docena elsewhere). — small.
4. The Units page's own "+" dialog still saves a blank category = "measured" (documented known gap), so a "Caja" made there opens the weight keypad at the till, while the same unit made from the item form is counted. Fix: same switch as 4b. — small.
5. Raw unit names leak in other screens: weight keypad, Sin código grid "$ 3,00 / Kilogram", receipt "0,5 Kilogram ×" (see T4/T5). Fix: `localizedUnitName` everywhere.
6. "Unidad de Venta" / "Unidades de Medida" use title case, unusual in Spanish/Portuguese. Fix: "Unidad de venta".

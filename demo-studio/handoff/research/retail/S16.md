## 3. UX problems
1. A tienda finds a paused "Cargo por servicio" in its list it never asked for (`SeedStore.kt:205` seeds every type). Small. Fix: seed only where `tipDefaultOn` / restaurants.
2. Item picker has **no search** (`DiscountsManagementScreen.kt:560-700`, grouped by category only) — a shop with 300 scanned products must scroll a long list. Small (BLOCKS nothing, but slow in a demo with a real catalog).
3. Idea texts: Title Case Spanish ("Descuento por Pago en Efectivo", "Recompensa VIP de Lealtad") and "modelos de piso" (furniture/electronics words) do not fit a barrio tienda. Small. Fix: "Descuento por pagar en efectivo", "Producto por vencer o dañado".

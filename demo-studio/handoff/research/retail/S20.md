## 1. Differences
- What a shop loses from the page (`sync/OnlineStoreSellable.kt:60-70`, mirror of the server rule): every **weighed** product ("Se vende por peso o medida": fruit, cheese, rice by the kilo) and every product with **sizes built with options** ("Tiene opciones que la página aún no muestra"). They are listed read-only under the categories with that reason.
- **No stock gate**: a product with 0 on the shelf is still offered online (`functions/storefront-projection.js:20` "No stock gate (the till has none)"); an online order does not move stock until it is charged at the till.
- No delivery choice on the page (pickup only, as for restaurants).

# T4 — "Productos sin código" (the No-barcode page: bread, fruit, ready icons)

Checked against code at commit **6736ddfb**. Paths under `app/src/main/java/com/example/`.

## 1. What it does, who, where
- One page to sell things that have no barcode. He types a few letters ("tomate"); the page shows **his own** no-barcode items first (with his price: one tap = into the sale) and then items from the **bundled catalogue of 3,523 items with drawn icons** (tap = a small price sheet). If nothing fits, the last row is **Vender «lo que escribió»**.
- A catalogue pick is **not saved until the sale is charged**; then it becomes his product (with icon) and next time it shows with his price (`ui/pos/GuideActions.kt:719-802`). Abandoning the sale saves nothing.
- Screen: `ui/screens/items/BarlessShelfScreen.kt:78-357`; data `data/NoBarcodeCatalog.kt:41-256`; icons `assets/no_barcode_icons/*.svg` (3,519) via `ui/components/NoBarcodeItemIcon.kt` (fallback: category emoji).
- Owner phone and employee phone: from the Scan tab card row "🏷 Sin código" (`ScanCheckoutTab.kt:267-272`, `MiniPosScanTab.kt:137-142`) or the camera's grey pill. Counter tablet: no such page; his saved no-barcode items appear in "Catálogo". Retail = Pharmacy (catalogue has no medicines).

## 2. Setup before the demo
- Tienda, register open. Nothing else: the catalogue ships inside the app (no download).
- For the "his items" grid, ring and charge 2–3 items once beforehand (e.g. tomate, pan francés, cilantro).

## 3. Country differences
- Catalogue language = app language (es for all Spanish countries, pt-br for BR, en for US/CA); unknown → Spanish (`NoBarcodeCatalog.kt:118-124`).
- Default unit of a catalogue item sold by weight: **Libra in Colombia (COP)**, **Kilogram everywhere else — including the US and Canada** (`NoBarcodeCatalog.kt:259-274`).
- Money: CO "$ 3.000", CL "$3.000", MX/PA/US/CA "$3.00", PE "S/ 3.00", EC "$3,00", AR "$ 3,00", BR "R$ 3,00".

## 5. Demo path (owner phone, Colombia)
1. Escanear tab › **Sin código**.
2. Type **tomate** → first row "🍅 tomate" (catalogue, no price) → tap.
3. Sheet: Precio **3000**, Por shows **Libra**, Peso **0.5** → "Agregar al carrito · $ 1.500" → tap.
4. Type **pan fr** → "pan francés" → Precio **500**, Por Unidad → "Agregar al carrito · $ 500".
5. Type **arepa de choclo** (not in the catalogue) → **Vender «arepa de choclo»** → Precio 1000, Categoría "Panadería" → add.
6. **Cobrar · $ 2.500** → pay cash → done.
7. Open Sin código again: grid shows tomate / pan francés / arepa de choclo with icons and his prices; tap tomate → weight keypad (T5).

## 6. UX/UI problems (verified)
1. **Picked catalogue items are filed under ENGLISH categories.** The catalogue's category column is English in every language file ("Fruits & Vegetables", "Bread", "Tofu & Soy"…) and `persistSoldNoBarcodeItems` creates a shop category with that name (`GuideActions.kt:770`, `CatalogDaoStore.kt:239-244`). A Spanish tienda ends up with "Fruits & Vegetables" beside its seeded "Frutas y Verduras", and the page's pills show the English names right in the demo (step 7). Fix: map catalogue categories to the seeded `retail_cat_*` (or translate). — **BLOCKS A DEMO**.
2. Typing "**pan**" shows pana (fabric), panko, paneer, panela, pandoro, pancetta — no bread; the placeholder itself suggests "pan" (`NoBarcodeCatalog.kt:150-195`, ranking by name length among starts-with). Fix: rank whole-word "pan …" above longer single words, or add item "pan". — **BLOCKS A DEMO** (if the presenter types "pan").
3. The empty-focus suggestions show "labneh (لبنة)" in Arabic script in es/pt/en (`NoBarcodeCatalog.kt:218-255`, first Cheese/Dairy/Eggs row by id). Fix: pick a local first item (queso campesino / queijo minas). — small, visible.
4. Eggs ("huevo A/AA/AAA", "ovo", "AA egg") sit in category "Tofu & Soy" (catalogue data); with problem 1 a shop gets a "Tofu & Soy" category for eggs. Fix in data.
5. The sheet's "Por" list shows the whole unit table: English group headings in **red** ("Weight & Mass", error colour), both "Libra" rows (Pound and Libra both read "Libra" in Spanish), Milligram, Yard… (`BarlessShelfScreen.kt:494-547`). The item form uses `UnitPicker` (short list, grey translated headings). Fix: reuse `UnitPicker.shortList/more` + `unitCategoryTitle`. — small.
6. The sheet asks a **weight** for any unit that is not Unidad (`isQtyMode = unitOfSale != PER_ITEM`, `:488`): cilantro (catalogue unit "bunch" = Manojo, a counted unit) asks "Peso (Manojo)" with decimals; afterwards the grid tap adds it as 1 (uses `isSoldByMeasure`, `:160`). Fix: `UnitOfSale.isSoldByMeasure` in the sheet too. — small.
7. Grid price label prints the raw stored unit name: "$ 3,00 / Kilogram" in a Spanish shop outside CO (`:305-307`). Fix: `localizedUnitName` + abbreviation ("$ 3,00 / kg").
8. US/CA default weight unit is Kilogram, not Pound (`NoBarcodeCatalog.kt:262`, COP only), while the item form's list puts Pound first for US/CA (`sync/UnitPicker.kt:18`). Fix: use `UnitPicker.usesPounds(country)`.
9. Button says "Agregar al carrito" here but "Agregar a la venta" on the weight keypad (`:579` vs `WeightEntryDialog.kt:224`). Fix: one wording.
10. The price step is a form (name, price, unit, category, weight) in a bottom sheet — against the app's own rule "a form is a full page" (CLAUDE.md UI rules). Possible keyboard overlap on small phones. Fix: full page or keep minimal fields.
11. "plátano" appears twice (fruit and "Flowers & Plants") — catalogue data.

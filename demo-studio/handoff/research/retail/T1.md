# T1 — "Escanear el código de barras" (scan a product into the sale)

Checked against code at commit **6736ddfb**. Paths under `app/src/main/java/com/example/`.

## 1. What it does, who, where
- The owner points the phone camera at a barcode, **taps the screen once** (it is tap-to-capture, not a live scanner), hears a beep, and the product goes into the open sale. Scanning the same code again adds one more.
- **Owner's phone** (`ui/screens/home/ScannerScreen.kt`): known product → straight into the sale; unknown / unpriced → the "¿Qué es este artículo?" form (T3).
- **Employee phone (Mini POS)**: same camera screen, same behaviour (it can also create products, see §7).
- **Counter tablet (MamaPos)**: its own "Escanear" tab (`ui/screens/mamapos/MamaScanPane.kt`) scans **known products only**; an unknown code or a product with no price is a red message, never a new item (`ui/pos/TabletScanActions.kt:28-47`). It also accepts a plug-in USB/Bluetooth barcode reader that types digits + Enter (`TabletScanActions.kt:58-72`, `MamaScanPane.kt:98-117`).
- Retail and Pharmacy: identical (`PharmacyRules` is an empty subclass of `RetailRules`; `hasScanTab = true`, `defaultHomeTab = 1`).

## 2. Setup before the demo
- A Tienda (or Farmacia) business. The barcode list (T2) must be on the phone, otherwise "Agregar productos" opens the download page instead of the camera (`ScanCheckoutTab.kt:170-172`).
- Camera permission allowed (first tap asks; a refusal shows a dialog with "Abrir ajustes", `ScanCheckoutTab.kt:141-168`).
- **Open the register first** ("Abrir caja"). On a known-closed register the add is held and the app jumps to Open register (`ui/pos/CartActions.kt:172-185`).
- A few products already in the catalogue with a barcode and a price (create them with T3 first). Brief says no real brands: print your own EAN-13 labels, e.g. **7709990000016, 7709990000023, 7709990000030** (checked: not in the downloaded list v1.2, so they behave as "your own" products).
- Tablet demo: tablet paired as counter register, products synced from the owner phone.

## 3. Country differences
- Same everywhere. Only EAN-13, EAN-8, UPC-A, UPC-E are read (`BarcodeCameraBox.kt:115-119`); UPC-E is expanded to UPC-A (`data/BarcodeFormats.kt:39-58`). Codes must be 8, 12 or 13 digits (`ScannerScreen.kt:132`, `TabletScanActions.kt:50`).
- Money on the Charge button: CO `$ 12.500`, CL `$12.500`, MX/PA/US/CA `$12.50`, PE `S/ 12.50`, EC `$12,50`, AR `$ 12,50`, BR `R$ 12,50` (golden file `functions/test-vectors/money-format.json`).

## 5. Demo path (owner phone, then tablet)
1. Home opens on "Escanear" → tap **Agregar productos**.
2. Aim the box at label 7709990000016 (shop product "Arroz 500 g"), tap screen → beep, "Arroz 500 g".
3. Tap screen again on same label → "Otra más · Arroz 500 g × 2".
4. Aim at 7709990000023 → beep, "Panela" (or another own product).
5. Point at a blank wall, tap → buzz + red flash + "No se detectó código…".
6. Tap **Cobrar $ X** (badge 3) → pay screen.
7. Tablet: tab **Escanear** → tap "Escanear" (permission) → tap screen on the arroz label → name in white, line appears in the ticket on the right.
8. Tablet: scan a label the shop never created → red "No está en tu catálogo" (nothing is created).

## 6. UX/UI problems (verified)
1. **Scanning after removing the line adds nothing but says it did.** `removeItemFromCart` (`ui/pos/CartActions.kt:263-283`, used by the breakdown screen's minus `CheckoutBreakdownScreen.kt:339`) never clears the scan memory (`ScanCartActions.kt:45, 320`), so a re-scan takes the "already scanned" path (`ScannerScreen.kt:139-152`), shows "Otra más · X × 2" and `updateScanCartItemQty` finds no cart line to change (`ScanCartActions.kt:103-121`). Also a "+" in the cart is overwritten by the next scan's count. Fix: decide "already scanned" from `uiState.cart`, or keep scanCart in sync in removeItemFromCart. — small (can bite a live demo).
2. Same desync when the register is closed: `commitScanItem` puts the code in the scan memory (`ScanCartScreen.kt:102`) before `addItemToCart` refuses; if Open register is abandoned the next scan "adds another" to nothing. Fix: add to scanCart only after the cart add ran.
3. A barcoded product sold by weight/measure is added as qty 1, no weight asked (`ScanCartScreen.kt:103`, no `isSoldByMeasure` check). Fix: open `WeightEntryDialog` like the Library tap does. — small, possible for deli items.
4. The tablet's error text is red #FF3B30 on a 75% black band — low contrast, and only the band shows it (`MamaScanPane.kt:139-163`). Fix: white text with a red icon/chip.
5. Owner Home shows "Teclado" twice: as a tab and as a card row (`HomeTabLabels.kt:24`, `ScanCheckoutTab.kt:274-279`). Fix: drop one.
6. Tablet: a plug-in reader only works while the Scan pane holds focus (`MamaScanPane.kt:99-117`); on Catálogo/Ventas a reader's digits go nowhere and nothing says so. Fix: listen on the whole tablet screen or say "reader works on Escanear".

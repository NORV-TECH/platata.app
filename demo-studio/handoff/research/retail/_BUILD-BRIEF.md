## The voice script `features/<ID>.json`
Same shape as `features/R4.json`, but with three languages per step and calm settings:
`{"id":"<ID>","name":"…","business":"tienda","voice_settings":{"es":{"stability":0.5,"similarity_boost":0.75},"pt":{"stability":0.5,"similarity_boost":0.75},"en":{"stability":0.5,"similarity_boost":0.75}},"steps":[{"id":"1-…","voice":{"es":"…","pt":"…","en":"…"}}, …]}`
- Number of steps in the json == number of beats in the page, same order, step ids "1-xxx", "2-xxx"… (they sort alphabetically: use "1-" … "9-" then "91-", "92-" if more than 9 — better: keep ≤ 9 beats).
- es spoken to the owner with **tú** (Colombian neutral, the app's words), pt-BR with **você**, en American. The voice only explains the feature: no small talk, no jokes, no "¡Hola!", no sound words. One or two short sentences per line; the whole script ≤ ~550 characters per language. The last line: "Así … con Platata." / "Assim … com o Platata." / "That's how … with Platata."
- Voice lines must describe exactly what is on screen at that beat and use the app's own words for buttons. Money spoken in words is fine but avoid numbers that differ by country unless the line is country-specific.

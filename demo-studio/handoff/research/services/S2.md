Commit read: `17d519ac` (branch sim/usa-canada). Paths under `app/src/main/java/com/example/`.
Base: restaurant `S2.md` (d1b7698) and retail `S2.md` (6736ddfb). Only what differs for services, plus what changed in the code since.

## 1. What it does, who, which device
Same mechanism as restaurant: one shared till per business, opened with the starting cash; `Platata_sync/{bizKey}/registers/{token}/shift/current` existing = open (choke point 5).
- **Owner's phone:** opens it (amber bar, panel card, or the first tap on a service).
- **Employee phone (Mini POS) / counter tablet:** cannot open it from their screens; locked "Caja cerrada" page / snackbar "Abre la caja primero" (`register_gate_message`). Same as restaurant.
- **Who may open (changed since restaurant S2):** REGISTER-2 — the permission "Abrir y cerrar caja" (`perm_can_close_shift`) now gates opening too (`ShiftController.openShift`, `OpenRegisterScreen.kt:52-53`). A PIN session without it sees `register_open_not_allowed` instead of the button. Restaurant S2 problem 4 is closed.
- **Trades:** no difference between beauty / pets / vehicles / repairs / home. The register is the same for all 41 trades.

What is different for services is WHEN the register is needed:
- **Booking needs an open register.** "Agendar" (or "Crear orden" / "Con fecha", word by trade, `sync/ServiceActionLabel.kt`) is enabled only when the sale has items (`HomeScreen.kt:787-799`, `isButtonEnabled = totalCharge > 0`), and the only way to put a service in the sale is a tile tap → `addItemToCart`, which refuses on a known-closed register (`CartActions.kt:176-187`). So a salon owner who wants to book tomorrow's client after closing must open the till first.
- **Charging a booked appointment does NOT check the register.** Agenda › appointment › "Cobrar" (`AppointmentDetailScreen.kt:178-182`) calls `loadSavedOrderIntoCart` + opens the pay screen (`ui/navigation/ReportsNavGraph.kt:116-124`); neither `loadSavedOrderIntoCart` (`SavedTicketActions.kt:618`) nor `finalizeOrder` / `PlaceOrderUseCase` checks the shift (grep: `registerBlocksSale` is only read in `CartActions`, `HomeScreen.kt:701`, `ManualCalculatorScreen.kt:65`). A cash sale can be finished with the till closed.

## 6. UX/UI problems (verified in code)
1. **Booking an appointment needs an open register.** `Agendar` is off with an empty sale (`HomeScreen.kt:787-799`) and a service can only enter the sale through `addItemToCart`, which sends a closed register to "Abrir caja" (`CartActions.kt:176-187`). Booking tomorrow's client at night forces opening the till (and then closing it again). Fix: let a booking add services without the register gate (only `finalizeOrder` needs it).
2. **Charging a booked appointment skips the register check.** Agenda › "Cobrar" (`ReportsNavGraph.kt:116-124` → `SavedTicketActions.kt:618`) reaches the pay screen and `finalizeOrder` with the till closed; nothing in the pay path checks the shift. A cash sale then belongs to no shift, so no drawer counts it. Fix: on the pay screen, when the register is known closed, send the owner to "Abrir caja" first (same gate as a tile tap).
3. Small: the amber "Caja cerrada · Abrir" bar shows above the setup picker of a business that has not finished setup (`HomeScreen.kt:446-451`). Fix: hide it until `servicesSetupDone` (or skip).
4. Small, possible: `OpenRegisterScreen` formats with the global `CurrencyConfig.current` (`OpenRegisterScreen.kt:39`) — the bug class PHONEFIX-1 fixed on the register card; right after switching between two of the demo businesses in different countries the amount could show the old currency. Fix: read `uiState.currencyCode`.
5. Related, outside S2 (for the close-register researcher): closing the register lists every SAVED order as unpaid (`OpenTablesAtClose.kt`, `CloseRegisterScreen.kt:76`), and for services every future booking is a SAVED order, so a salon closing at night with 10 bookings for tomorrow reads "Tienes 10 pedidos abiertos sin cobrar ($ …). ¿Cerrar igual?" (`register_close_open_tickets`). Fix: leave out orders with `scheduledFor`/`readyFor` in the future.

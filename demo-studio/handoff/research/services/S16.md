## 6. UX/UI problems (services only)
1. **BLOCKS A DEMO if demoed — "Promo nuevo cliente" gives 10 % to EVERY client, not new ones.** The only condition is "a client is attached" (`ui/pos/AutoRules.kt:66`, `DiscountIdeas.kt:24`). In a salon every booking has a client, so it becomes a permanent 10 % on the linked services. Fix: a first-visit check (client with no earlier paid sale), or rename the idea "Descuento con cliente".
2. small — After saving a new extra charge the host jumps to tab 0 = Descuentos, so the owner doesn't see what he just saved (`DiscountsManagementScreen.kt:198-201`, `selectedTabIndex = 0`). Fix: return to the tab the form was opened from.
3. small — "Cargo nocturno" opens with "Preguntar al cobrar" OFF, so once items are picked it adds 15 % to those services at ALL hours (no time condition exists; `DiscountDraft.fromIdea` `sync/DiscountDraft.kt:84-91`). Fix: open that idea with ask-at-checkout ON (whole bill), or rename "Cargo de urgencia".
4. small — Words for services: "Automático en N artículos", "Elegir artículos", "Selecciona al menos un artículo", "Artículos para …" (`disc_auto_on_items`, `disc_choose_items`, `disc_scope_required`, `disc_link_dialog_title`). Fix: "servicios" for services.
5. small — Title "Descuentos y combos" leaves out the third tab "Cargos" (`disc_title_with_combos`). Fix: "Descuentos, combos y cargos" or "Precios especiales".
6. small (possible, legal) — "Cargo por tarjeta de crédito" is offered in Colombia, where surcharging card payments is widely reported as not allowed; in the US some states restrict it, and debit surcharges are banned. Fix: offer it only where legal (Monti / lawyer to confirm).
7. small — Idea names still Title Case ("Descuento por Pago en Efectivo", "Recompensa VIP de Lealtad"; pt "Sobretaxa de Cartão de Crédito") — already flagged for retail (item 32), also hits services.
8. small — Item picker has no search and lists services and products together (also flagged for retail).

## 1. What a services backup carries, who, device
- The whole shop `.db`: services and their option prices, products, clients, sales, **appointments / work orders** (open orders with `scheduledFor` / `readyFor`, `data/Order.kt:132-147`), the chosen trades (`app_metadata` `trade_slugs`, `BusinessProfileStore.kt:121-124`) and "Dónde atiendes" with its surcharge (`app_metadata` `where_modes`, `ShopSettingsStore.kt:96-99`). Plus `ProductImages/`, `Invoices/`, `Receipts/`, `QrPayment/` (`work/DriveBackupHelper.kt:280-283`).
- The researched catalogue (`assets/catalogo_investigado/*.json`, service minutes on tiles) is inside the app, so it never needs a backup.
- Services have the invoices row (`ServicesRules.hasSupplierInvoiceRow = true`), so the "Fotos de facturas" card shows too: good for the Taller de motos (parts invoices).
- Owner phone only. Staff phones / counter tablet: no backup screen.

## 2. Setup before the demo
Same as restaurant: Drive not connected by default, the bar on Home "Este negocio no tiene respaldo · Conectar" (`backup_off_banner_title` / `_action`). On a services home the bar sits above the Servicios / Productos tabs.

## 5. Demo path (Veterinaria, owner phone, ~9 taps)
1. Home shows amber "Este negocio no tiene respaldo · Conectar" → tap.
2. "Conectar Google Drive" → pick account → allow Drive.
3. "Respaldar ahora" → "Respaldando…" → "Último respaldo: Hace menos de 1 hora".
4. Second phone, same Google account: "Ya uso Platata" → "Restaurar un negocio" → "¡Qué bueno verte otra vez!" → "Recuperar" → "Sí, restaurar" → lands on Servicios with "Consulta general", "Vacunación" and tomorrow's appointments in the agenda.
Say aloud: "tus citas y tus clientes quedan guardados en tu Drive".

## 6. UX/UI problems
1. **The restore warning does not count appointments.** `RestoreWarning.rungSince` counts only PAID / COMPLETED / PARTIALLY_REFUNDED (`BackupSettingsScreen.kt:806-813`), but a booked appointment or work order is an open order. Restoring a copy older than this morning's bookings moves them aside with no word about them (body says "no se puede deshacer" but mentions only sales, or uses the no-sales body). Small (data is kept in the set-aside file). Fix: also count open orders with `scheduledFor`/`readyFor` created since, e.g. "…y 3 citas agendadas…".
2. Risk card text names "categorías, opciones y productos" (`backup_risk_desc`) — a salon thinks in services, clients and appointments, and services cannot manage categories. Small. Fix: per-type text or "Tus ventas, servicios, clientes y citas…".

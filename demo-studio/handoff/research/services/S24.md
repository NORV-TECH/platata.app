## 1. What it does for a services owner, devices
- An owner with two shops (e.g. a Peluquería and a separate Uñas) holds two workspaces, each with its own trades, services, prices, clients, agenda, team, country. Switching = tap the row; lands on that shop's Home (Servicios tab). Owner phone only; counter tablet / staff phone have no switcher.
- A branch = a new shop that starts with a copy of the parent's catalogue: services (with `servicePriceMode` and per-choice prices in `product_variations`), products, option lists, add-ons, combos, discounts/charges, taxes, payment methods (`BranchSnapshotStore.extractBranchSnapshot`, lines 90-142). Optional: team, clients, receipt + security settings. Never: sales, open tickets, **appointments / work orders** (`purgeTransactions`, `WorkspaceSwitchActions.kt:485`).
- **Not copied to a branch (services-only loss):** the shop's trades (`app_metadata` `trade_slugs`) and "Dónde atiendes" (`where_modes`). `applyBranchSnapshot` writes only `business_type`, `currency_code`, `business_language` (+ country) (`BranchSnapshotStore.kt:400-428`). See problem 1.

## 6. UX/UI problems
1. **A services branch loses its trades and "Dónde atiendes".** `trade_slugs` and `where_modes` live in `app_metadata` and the branch copy writes only three keys (`BranchSnapshotStore.kt:400-428`). In the branch: the till's second button falls back to "Agendar" (`tradeActionFor(empty)`, `data/TradeFamily.kt:114`) — a Taller de motos branch shows "Agendar" instead of "Crear orden"; the trade card in Mis servicios disappears (`ServicesMenuScreen.kt:165`); service tiles lose their minutes (`GuideActions.loadTradeSlugs` loads nothing, `:152`); domicilio / recogida and their charge are OFF. Small for a salon demo, BLOCKS A DEMO for a Taller de motos branch. Fix: copy `trade_slugs` and `where_modes` in `createBranchFromWorkspace` (same place the country is copied).
2. "Copia este menú" / "su propio menú y equipo" / "Menú y precios" are restaurant words for a services owner (`ContextSwitcher.kt:355, 387`, `BranchSetupScreen.kt:302`). Already flagged for shops (R23, retail 34); also hits services, not fixed. Small. Fix: "servicios y precios" by `rules`.
3. Switch warning says "Tu pantalla de cocina dejará de recibir pedidos" (`host_switch_warn_kitchen`, `HostSwitchWarningDialog.kt:21`) — for services the screen is the "Pantalla de trastienda" (`ServicesRules.prepScreenName = BACK_ROOM`). Small. Fix: pick the word from `rules.prepScreenName`.
4. Branch note mentions only "ventas y cuentas abiertas"; booked appointments also stay behind (`branch_setup_no_carry`). Small. Fix: services wording "…ni las citas…".

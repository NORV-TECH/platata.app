## 1. What differs for services, who, where
- Owner phone: flag → "Caja abierta" → Register screen → "Cerrar caja". Employee phone with "Abrir y cerrar caja": More panel row
  "Cerrar caja · Contar el efectivo y cerrar la caja" (`ui/screens/more/MoreScreen.kt:146-153`), blind count. Counter tablet: no close door (unchanged).
- **The "open tickets" warning fires on appointments.** Since FIX-1 the Close button first asks `OpenTablesAtClose.of(openOrders)`
  (`CloseRegisterScreen.kt:75-76, 158`). For services, every booking in the Agenda is an open ticket in `openOrders` (the Agenda list is
  built from that same list, `ServicesTransactionsScreen.kt:110-114`), and `OpenTablesAtClose` counts every non-online open order
  (`sync/OpenTablesAtClose.kt:14-17`). So a salon with tomorrow's citas sees, every evening:
  "Antes de cerrar · Tienes 6 pedidos abiertos sin cobrar ($ 420.000). ¿Cerrar igual?" (pt "Você tem 6 pedidos abertos sem cobrar…",
  en "You have 6 open tickets not paid yet…") — `asTables = rules.isRestaurantType` is false, so it uses `register_close_open_tickets`.
  "Cerrar igual" continues normally; nothing is blocked.
- The summary can carry two lines a restaurant rarely has: "Cargos por servicio +$ 20.000" (the "A domicilio" trip charge rides
  `serviceCharges`, `ui/pos/AutoRules.kt:68-90`) and, in US/CA, "Propinas" (services ask for a tip by default, `tipDefaultOn = true`).
- No fiado lines (services have no tab).

## 6. UX/UI problems (verified)
1. **BLOCKS A DEMO — every evening a salon / vet / workshop with future bookings is told it has "pedidos abiertos sin cobrar".**
   Appointments for tomorrow are not unpaid tickets of today's till (`sync/OpenTablesAtClose.kt:14-17` counts every open order;
   `CloseRegisterScreen.kt:75-76, 158`). Fix: for services, leave out orders with `scheduledFor` / `readyFor` after the end of today
   (count only today's undone, unpaid jobs), and say "citas de hoy sin cobrar".
2. Small: the warning names them "pedidos" (`register_close_open_tickets`) — a salon says "citas", a workshop "trabajos". Fix: a services wording.

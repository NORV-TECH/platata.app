## 1. What it does, who, where
- Owner's phone: Settings › "Tu negocio" › "Conexiones" — paper theme in the **services accent**: main/button #7C55E0 (white text), soft #EEEAF5, accent ink #6A45CC (dark: soft #2D2647, ink #AE96EC) (`ui/theme/PaperColor.kt:57-59`); page #FBFAF7, surface #FFFFFF, ink #17181B as in restaurant S19.
- Chooser "¿Qué quieres conectar?": "Tablet de mostrador — Una tablet que vende en tu mostrador." / "El teléfono de un empleado — Tu empleado vende desde su propio teléfono, como una caja." (same words for a salon; "caja" wording).
- "Impresoras y pantallas" page: services print **receipts only** (`ServicesRules.kt:83` `PrinterKinds.RECEIPT_ONLY`), and the prep screen is called "Pantalla de trastienda" / back-room screen (`ServicesRules.kt:80`, button "Conectar una pantalla de trastienda"). Neither uses a connection.
- **Who uses which device in a services shop:**
  - Owner phone: everything (setup, Mis servicios, Agenda, Reports, Vender en línea).
  - Counter tablet (MamaPos, landscape, light): reception desk of a salon / vet / taller — sell, book, charge, run the Agenda.
  - Employee phone (Mini POS): a stylist / groomer / mechanic with her own phone — sell, book, charge, see the Agenda.
- Trades: no device difference by trade except the booking button word: beauty/vet/home trades "Agendar" / "Agendar" / "Book"; taller/repair trades "Crear orden" / "Criar ordem" / "Create order"; carpintería/herrería/vidriería "Con fecha" (`sync/ServiceActionLabel.kt`, `data/TradeFamily.kt`; taller-motos = ORDEN, `:59`).
- **Who performs the service: I don't see this in the code.** Searched `assignedEmployee`, `performedBy`, `stylist`, `employeeId` on orders/bookings: a booking has no "done by" field. The order only keeps `employeeName` = the person logged in when it was saved, and it is OVERWRITTEN by whoever charges it (`ui/pos/SavedTicketActions.kt:258`, `ui/pos/CheckoutActions.kt:395`). So "Ventas por empleado" credits the person who charged, not the stylist who did the work.

## 2. Setup / defaults
- Same as restaurant S19: a new business has 0 devices, hero "1 de 2", nudge "Conecta tu segunda caja" (services too). A services shop needs no device for any feature except: **online booking outside US/CA unlocks only with a 3rd connection in use** (see V9/S20).
- Before pairing: the owner finishes the services setup (trades + services), and for the employee phone creates the employee (Team) with a PIN and switches "Conectar" on.
- Staff permissions that matter for services: "Cancelar recibos" (`perm_can_cancel_receipt`) also gates "No asistió" and "Cancelar cita" on a booking (`ui/pos/AppointmentActions.kt:86`). No stock permission for services (`offersStockPermission = false`). Staff cannot create or price services (no "+" on their library, `MiniPosSellScreen.kt:80-82`).

## 6. UX/UI problems
1. **No "who does it" on a booking or sale** (no field; `employeeName` = whoever charged, `CheckoutActions.kt:395`). A salon with three stylists cannot see or report per-stylist work, and "Ventas por empleado" credits the cashier. Possible product gap; ask Monti. (Not a bug in code.)
2. Staff without "Cancelar recibos" can open "No asistió" / "Cancelar cita", confirm, and the page closes as if done, but nothing happens (`AppointmentDetailScreen.kt:365-381` always shows the buttons; `AppointmentActions.kt:86` returns silently). Fix: hide the two buttons when the person lacks the permission, or show "No tienes permiso". Small.
3. Tablet "Ventas" uses the generic sales grid while the employee phone and owner use the services sales list (`MamaPosScreen.kt:1206` vs `MiniPosSellScreen.kt:682`): different rows for the same sales. Small.
4. Already flagged, still present, also hits services: the counter tablet code page says "Conecta este celular y la tablet al mismo WiFi." (`counter_reg_sync_status_waiting`, `ui/screens/onboarding/CounterRegisterSyncInfoScreen.kt:85-88`).
5. Chooser and Connections still call the device "caja" ("Conecta tu segunda caja", section "Cajas", "la otra es para tu primera caja") — odd for a salon or a vet; a services word ("recepción" / "equipo") would fit better. Small.
6. Online bookings ring on every device (owner, tablet, each employee phone) up to 11 times and only the device that opens the alert stops (`ui/pos/OnlineOrderAlerts.kt:63-78`). Noisy in a salon with several phones. Small (see V9 §6.5).
7. Stale code comment says the tablet has no Agendar branch (`MamaPosScreen.kt:1423-1428`) although it is wired (`:1373`). Not user-visible.

# V1 — Pick your trade, services come already written (trade picker → pick services → prices → "Lo que trae" → done → till)

Commit read: `17d519ac` (/home/claude/app-head, branch sim/usa-canada). Paths under `app/src/main/java/com/example/`. Nothing run on a phone.

## 1. What it does, who, which device
Right after a SERVICES business is created (S1), the owner picks his trade(s) from 41, ticks the services he does from a researched list (names, minutes, options, and in Colombia only, prices), sets prices, optionally adjusts what each service includes / charges extra, and lands on a till full of service tiles. **Owner's phone only.** Counter tablet and employee phone never run it (`PlatataApp.kt:416` `inServicesSetup = !isMiniPosClient && …`); they receive the finished catalogue by sync.

Where it lives: Home tab 0 IS the setup until it is finished (`HomeScreen.kt:1044-1072`, `ServicesFrontDoor.kt:48`). The flag `services_setup_done` (app_metadata) turns on in ONE place, the end of `GuideActions.finishBridge` (`ui/pos/GuideActions.kt:482`, flag ~612). While it is off: no tab row, no bottom bar, no Book/Charge footer (`HomeScreen.kt:609`, `PlatataApp.kt:412-418`).

Trade differences in the code: none in the flow itself. Differences come from the catalogue (how many services carry a price, how many have "choices") and from `TradeFamily` (`data/TradeFamily.kt`): peluquería, uñas, veterinaria = CITA → left footer button "Agendar"; taller-motos = ORDEN → "Crear orden" | "Criar ordem" | "Create order" (`sync/ServiceActionLabel.kt`). Family colours: Belleza #D9468A (peluquería, uñas), Mascotas #8BAA2E (veterinaria), Vehículos #3D63DD (taller-motos) (`ui/theme/PaperColor.kt`).

Names, minutes, options travel everywhere.

| | CO | MX PE CL EC AR PA BR US CA |
|---|---|---|
| Pick list subtitle | "40 min · $ 45.000" / "desde $ 18.000" | "40 min" only |
| Price step after "Usar estos N servicios" | one card per service (bridge, pills −20% / common / +20%) | ONE list "Tus precios", empty boxes (`setupPriceStartsAsList`, `data/catalog/GuideLogic.kt:48`; route `OnlineNavGraph.kt:100`) |

| Untouched service | saved at the suggested price + "sugerido" badge | saved with NO price → till asks ("Sin precio" tile) |
| Trade products seeded on Productos tab | with CO prices | with no prices |

Money format in boxes/pills = the business currency (`formatCurrency`, golden `functions/test-vectors/money-format.json`): CO `$ 45.000`, MX `$450.00`, PE `S/ 45.00`, CL `$45.000`, EC `$45,00`, AR `$ 4.500,00`, PA `$45.00`, BR `R$ 45,00`, US/CA `$45.00`. The price box keyboard is Number in 0-decimal currencies (CO, CL) and Decimal elsewhere (`ServicesPriceListScreen.kt:334`).

Language: service, option and category names come from the catalogue in es / en / pt (fr exists but dropped); `textoEnIdioma` falls back to Spanish (`CatalogoInvestigadoATienda.kt:202`). The language used is `app.resources.configuration.locales[0]` (`GuideActions.kt:174-180`), not the shop's `business_language` (see problem 8).

## 6. UX / UI problems (verified in code at 17d519ac)
1. **Outside Colombia the setup promises prices it never shows.** Lede "Escoge tu oficio. Te traemos sus servicios con precios." (`trade_picker_lede`, `TradePickerScreen.kt:180`) and the type pitch "41 oficios con sus servicios y precios" (S1) are shown in every country; outside CO every price is stripped (`CatalogoInvestigadoAssets.kt:73`). Fix: country-aware lede ("Te traemos sus servicios; tú pones los precios.") when `!PriceSuggestions.mayShowResearchedPrices`.
2. **Real brand names land on the Productos tab with no choice.** `finishBridge` seeds every researched product of the trade (Olaplex, L'Oréal, Schwarzkopf, Bravecto, NexGard, Royal Canin, Hill's, Masglo, Mia Secret, Utopick…) whenever ≥1 service is ticked (`GuideActions.kt` finishBridge "Round 268" loop). Owner never ticked them; brand footage is a no-go for the demos (and a trademark question). Fix: let the owner tick products too, or seed generic names ("Tinte profesional", "Antipulgas en tableta").
3. **"Según la opción" looks selected but is not saved.** On a choice page the segment shows "Según la opción" for a null (FIXED) mode (`BridgeScreen.kt:391`); unless tapped, the service is saved FIXED, so the till tile shows the FIRST choice's price as a flat price ("Vacunación $ 32.000", "Peluquería y baño $ 47.000"; `ProductLibraryView.kt:331` uses the first variation, `ProductCard.kt` shows "Desde" only for FROM), while "Mis servicios" says "Desde" for the same service. A first choice with no price (Retoque de raíz) shows "Sin precio". Fix: store FROM for a choices service by default, or show "Desde $min" on any multi-choice tile.
4. **"En la caja" service still shows a price on the tile.** Choosing "En la caja" without tapping a pill leaves the suggested price stored (`BridgeScreen.kt` commitCurrentPrice saves only on a touch); the tile shows "$ 45.000 · sugerido" (`ProductCard.kt` priceLabel ignores ASK) but tapping it asks for the price (`CartActions.kt:473`). Fix: tile label "En la caja" when `ServicePriceMode.asksAtTill`.
5. **Back from "Tus precios" (or from a price card) loses the ticks.** `PickServicesScreen` keeps `index` and `ticked` in plain `remember` (`PickServicesScreen.kt:124,140`), so returning to it shows trade 1 with nothing ticked though the walk still holds the selection. Fix: `rememberSaveable`, seeded from `_guideSelections`.
6. **Leaving a price card lands on a bare Material page.** "¿Salir sin guardar?" → `cancelGuide` empties the slugs, then `popBackStack` to the Guide route, which draws the fallback "¿Qué haces?" + "Volver" button (`PickServicesScreen.kt:103-121`, `OnlineNavGraph.kt:130-133`). Fix: pop back to Home (the picker) directly.
7. **Outside Colombia "Lo que trae" is unreachable for plain services**, and every researched extra becomes "se pregunta en la caja" silently (`ServicesPriceListScreen.kt:170-183` has no chevron for plain rows). Owners never learn their services carry ask-at-till extras until the till asks. Fix: a chevron/row on each list line opening the same sheet.
8. Service and category names are written into the shop's data in the app/phone language, not the shop's `business_language` (choke 12 rule) (`GuideActions.kt:174-180`, finishBridge `app.getString(tradeNameRes)`). Small; possible on API < 33 where per-app locale may not reach Application resources.

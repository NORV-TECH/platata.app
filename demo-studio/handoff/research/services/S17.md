## 1. What it does, who, which device
- Same screen as every type: owner's phone, Configuración › Cobros › "Impuestos" (`SettingsHubScreen.kt:95, 222`, route `taxes_workspace`, `SettingsNavGraph.kt:150`). Rates ride the reference to the counter tablet / employee phones.
- **What is different for services: a SERVICE has no tax switch at all.** Editing a service opens `ServiceEditScreen` (`CatalogNavGraph.kt:150-163`), which has name, price, options and delete — no "Cobrar impuesto sobre este artículo" row (no `isTaxable` anywhere in `ui/screens/services/ServiceEditScreen.kt`). Services are created by the trade walk (`GuideActions.finishBridge`, never sets `isTaxable`) or by "+ Nuevo" in Mis servicios, which goes to the trade picker (`CatalogNavGraph.kt:424`). So every service is `isTaxable = true` (`data/Product.kt:39`) and is taxed by every active rate (`PosViewModelTypes.kt:844` filters only on `isTaxable`).
- A PRODUCT in the "Productos" tab (shampoo, nail polish, oil filter) uses the normal item form (`ItemsScreen.kt:1428-1450`): the tax checkbox shows once a tax exists. So for services: products can be exempted, services cannot.
- All four trades behave the same (no trade branch in tax code).

## 2. Setup / factory defaults
- No tax seeded (no `TaxRate(` seed). No service charge seeded for services (`SeedStore.seedServiceFee` returns unless `rules.isRestaurantType`, `SeedStore.kt:201-205`).
- Presets for services: Impoconsumo is hidden (`TaxPresets.forCountry(..., isRestaurant = false)`, `TaxesManagementScreen.kt:151`). So a Colombian salon sees only the chip "IVA 19 %".
- New tax saved ON. Default answer to "¿Este impuesto ya está en tus precios?": included everywhere except US/CA (`sync/TaxPriceDefault.kt`).

## 6. UX/UI problems
1. **A service cannot be made tax-exempt** — `ServiceEditScreen.kt` has no tax row and every walk-made service is `isTaxable = true`. A US/CA salon that adds "Sales tax 8.875 % added on top" taxes every haircut, manicure and vet consult; it can only exempt products. BLOCKS A US/CA DEMO of taxes. Fix: draw the same `ShopSwitches.showTaxCheckbox` row in `ServiceEditScreen` and pass `isTaxable` through `saveService*`.
2. **CO services see the restaurant note** "En Colombia los restaurantes cobran impoconsumo 8% en vez de IVA" after tapping IVA (`TaxesManagementScreen.kt:172-180`; `TaxPresets.rivalOf` ignores the type). Already flagged as M1 for shops; it also hits services and is not fixed. Fix: show only when `rules.isRestaurantType`.
3. Snackbar "Se aplica a los artículos marcados con impuesto" (`tax_saved_on`) — for services it applies to every service whether marked or not, and services have no mark. Small. Fix: services wording "Se aplica a tus servicios y a los productos marcados".
4. Possible: no way to see which services are taxed (no column/badge anywhere). Small; goes with fix 1.

## 1. What it does, who, where
- Same owner-only switch (panel row "Vender en línea", `ui/screens/home/HomeScreen.kt:1582-1621`, gate `sync/SellOnline.kt:26` `showRow`). Owner's phone only.
- For a services shop the page has TWO halves (`functions/storefront-projection.js:225-247`, `:338-342`):
  - **Servicios** (type SERVICE) → booked for a day and time, paid at the appointment (V9).
  - **Productos** (type ITEM: shampoo, esmalte, aceite…) → ordered for pickup, paid at pickup, exactly like a restaurant/shop order (lands as an "Entrega" row in the Agenda with the "EN LÍNEA" pill, `ServicesTransactionsScreen.kt:390-395`).
  - The tabs only appear when both halves have something (`public/store/app.js:317-320`).
- Wording is switched to services by `data/business/SellOnlineWords.kt` (SERVICES): no "Cómo lo reciben / Para recoger" block, no kitchen "half an hour" tip, prep time renamed to appointment length.
- Trade differences: none in the code (the same screen for beauty, pets, vehicles, repairs, home).

## 2. Setup / defaults
- Unlock: same as restaurant (US/CA free; elsewhere a tablet or employee phone must USE a paid connection; `sync/StoreLockRule.kt`, dialog "Tu tienda online aún no está abierta").
- Defaults (`functions/online-store-hours.js:40-41`): fulfilment ["to_go"] (the seeded "To Go" dining option, `data/repo/SeedStore.kt:116`, makes the Products half orderable), "Duración de la cita" 20 min, card switch irrelevant (hidden for services).
- What shows up: see V9 §2.3. Walk-made services and products are left off (option sets / "Precio sugerido"); the main page lists them under "Fuera de tu tienda online" with the reason. Products seeded by the walk carry "Precio sugerido" whenever the catalogue had a Colombian price (`data/investigado/CatalogoInvestigadoATienda.kt:288`), so they are also left off until the owner edits and saves them.

## 3. Country differences
- Payment row for SERVICES in every country = locked "Paga en el negocio — No se cobra nada al reservar" (`SellOnlineScreen.kt:495-498`). No card row at all, also in US/CA and CO (`SellOnlineGate.cardOnlineRow` → HIDDEN when `cardOnlineAllowed=false`, `sync/SellOnline.kt:41-44`). Products of a services shop are also pay-at-pickup only (server `functions/online-store-rules.js:51`).
- Unlock text: US/CA "La tienda online está incluida en tu país."; others need the 3rd connection.
- Money format: see V9 §3.

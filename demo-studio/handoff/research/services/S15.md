# S15 — Pago a empleados (SERVICES); commission per service

Code read: `/home/claude/app-head` @ `17d519ac`. Paths under `app/src/main/java/com/example/`.
**Same as restaurant S15 and retail S15.** The feature reads no business-type rule (no `rules.` in `ui/screens/team/EmployeePay*.kt` or
`ui/pos/EmployeePayActions.kt`, except the accountant's fiado row). It is a monthly record of a FIXED amount per person, owner phone only.

## 1. What differs for services
- Nothing in the screens.
- **Commission per service: not found in code.** Searched `commission|comisión|comision|porcentaje` over `app/src/main/java` and the strings:
  the only hits are card-processor fees (`accountant_card_fees`, `sync/CardFees.kt`, the US/CA pitch "Solo pagas una comisión en pagos con tarjeta").
  There is no per-service or per-stylist percentage, no "% of what she did" field on `Employee` (`data/Employee.kt`), and no link between a
  sale and the person who performed it (S11/S14). A salon that pays its stylists 40-50 % of their services cannot get that figure from the app;
  it would type each month's total by hand in the monthly ask (boxes are free amounts).
- Changed since the restaurant file (BACKOFFICE-1 / BACKOFFICE-2 C2 / Mercadeo 3.1): the switch reads "Llevar la cuenta de los pagos a
  empleados" / "Acompanhar os pagamentos dos funcionários" / "Track employee payments" (no more "Rastrear"); the consent says "Si pasa el 25 sin
  respuesta, se apaga solo"; the "Se apagó en septiembre. Guardamos todo lo que ya nos dijiste." note shows on Team and once on the owner's Home;
  "Meses anteriores" stays reachable while off; turning on has a Cancel; "1 persona" plurals; the monthly ask never asks about a month before
  tracking began and is shown only in the owner's own view (not a staff PIN session); the month editor asks before discarding.
  Restaurant P1-P10 are closed.

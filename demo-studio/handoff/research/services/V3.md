# V3 — Book an appointment ("Agendar" / "Crear orden" / "Con fecha")

Checked against code at commit **17d519ac** (branch sim/usa-canada). Paths under `app/src/main/java/com/example/`.
No screenshot of this screen exists in `app/src/test/screenshots/` (searched book / agenda / appoint / cuando).

## 1. What it does, who, where
- At the till the person taps one or more services (they go into the cart), then taps the LEFT footer button. A full page "¿Cuándo?" asks: when the client comes and/or when the job is ready, where it happens (only if the shop works in 2+ places), for whom (a client from the list, required) and a note. "Guardar" saves the cart as an UNPAID open ticket (status PENDING, lifecycle SAVED) carrying `scheduledFor` (arrival) and/or `readyFor` (ready date). Nothing is charged. The booking then lives in the Agenda (V4).
- The kind is derived, never chosen (`data/ScheduleKind.kt:15-24`): arrival only = cita, ready only = orden, both = both.
- The button word follows the shop's trades (`sync/ServiceActionLabel.kt`, table `data/TradeFamily.kt:23-75`):
  - Peluquería, Uñas, Veterinaria = CITA → "Agendar" | "Agendar" | "Book".
  - Taller de motos = ORDEN → "Crear orden" | "Criar ordem" | "Create order".
  - A shop with both kinds (or carpintería/herrería/vidriería) → "Con fecha" | "Com data" | "With date".
  - **The screen behind the button is the same for every trade**: both date questions are always shown (see problem 3).
- Devices (one copy of the screen on all three):
  - Owner phone: footer `FooterButtonPair` (`ui/screens/home/HomeScreen.kt:787-801`) → route `booking` (`ui/navigation/OnlineNavGraph.kt:64-70`).
  - Counter tablet (MamaPos): pill button above "Cobrar" in the cart step (`ui/screens/home/CartSheetContent.kt:851-867`, tag `mama_book_btn`) → step `BOOK` (`ui/screens/mamapos/MamaPosScreen.kt:1429-1449`, own top bar shown). If the shop's receipt mode is MANUAL the tablet first asks the ticket NAME step.
  - Employee phone (Mini POS): `MiniPosActionDuo` footer (`ui/screens/onboarding/MiniPosSellScreen.kt:64-104`, tag `minipos_book_btn`) → step `BOOK` with no top bar of its own (`:563-578`).
- No employee permission is needed to book.
- **Who performs it**: I don't see this in the code. `Order` has no stylist / vet / mechanic field (searched `stylist`, `assignedEmployee`, `performer`, `profesional`, `atiende`; `Order.employeeName` is only who saved the ticket). There is no per-person calendar.
- **Duration**: not stored. The researched `duration_min` of a catalogue service is only DISPLAYED later on the appointment detail (V4) and on the till tile (CU-21); the booking screen ignores it, slots are 15 min, overlap is checked per clock HOUR only.
- **Notice to the client by WhatsApp**: none when booking. The only WhatsApp notice is "Guardar y avisar por WhatsApp" after RESCHEDULING (V4, problem 4 there).

## 2. Setup before the demo
- Services business, trade picked, setup finished (`services_setup_done`), at least one service with a price.
- **The register must be open**: tapping a service on a known-closed register (owner / staff phone) sends the owner to "Open register" instead of adding it (`ui/pos/CartActions.kt:173-184`). So booking next week's appointment needs the cash drawer opened first.
- "Agendar" is grey until the cart total is > 0 (`HomeScreen.kt:691`, `MiniPosSellScreen` `enabled = cart not empty`; tablet also needs no typed keypad amount).
- Clients: optional beforehand; "¿Para quién?" opens the client list in picker mode and a client created there is selected at once (`ui/screens/customers/CustomerCrmScreen.kt:61-80`).
- "¿Dónde se hace?" appears only when 2–3 work places are switched on (Settings › "Where you work"); factory = only "En el local", so the block is not drawn (`ui/screens/where/WhereGate.kt:73`).
- Optional, side effect: if an order-slip printer is set up, saving a booking prints an order slip at once (problem 9).

## 3. Country differences
- Same screen and rules in every country. Only formats change, by APP LANGUAGE (not country) (`ui/screens/services/WhenPicker.kt:73-116`):
  - Time: es "3:00 p. m.", en "3:00 PM", pt "15:00".
  - Day line: es "vie 23 de oct", en "Fri 23 Oct", pt "sex 23/10"; today/tomorrow = "Hoy/Mañana" | "Hoje/Amanhã" | "Today/Tomorrow".
  - Month grid: weeks start Monday everywhere, US/CA included; day-initials row is the Spanish "L M M J V S D" in every language (problem 7).
- Opening hours are hard-coded 8:00–20:00 for every shop and country (`WhenPicker.kt:47-49`).
- Money appears only later in the Agenda row (V4): CO "$ 45.000", MX "$450.00", PE "S/ 45.00", CL "$45.000", EC "$45,00", AR "$ 45.000,00", PA/US "$45.00", CA "$45.00", BR "R$ 45,00" (golden `functions/test-vectors/money-format.json`).
- Client WhatsApp field starts with the shop country's dial code (CO +57, MX +52, BR +55 …).
- No card, Pix, tip, fiado or tax step here — booking charges nothing ("No se cobra nada ahora."). Charging happens later (V4 → normal pay screen; tips only US/CA, `ServicesRules.tipDefaultOn = true`).

## 6. UX/UI problems
1. **BLOCKS A DEMO** — A ready date saved with "Sin hora" is stored as MIDNIGHT: `readyMillis = dateStart + (readyMins ?: 0)` (`BookingScreen.kt:92`). The Agenda row and the detail then read `minsOf()` = 0 and print "12:00 a. m." instead of "Sin hora" (`ServicesTransactionsScreen.kt:343,367-373`, `AppointmentDetailScreen.kt:99`). Fix: store a "no time" marker (e.g. keep the date at midday + a flag, or a separate no-time rule) and show "Sin hora" when the stored time is the no-time value.
2. **BLOCKS A DEMO** — No WhatsApp confirmation to the client at booking time; the only WhatsApp is after a reschedule (V4). A narrated "the client gets a confirmation" cannot be shown. Fix: after Save offer "Avisar por WhatsApp" opening the client's own chat (`WhatsappOpen` + `directNumber`) with "Hola Ana, tu cita en <negocio> quedó para vie 23 de oct, 3:00 p. m. (Corte de dama)." in the shop's language.
3. **BLOCKS A DEMO** (looks wrong on video) — Every trade sees BOTH date questions, and the "¿Cuándo está listo?" strip starts open: a hair or nail salon is asked when the haircut "is ready"; a taller is asked first "¿Cuándo viene?" (`BookingScreen.kt:136-165`). Fix: show only the trade family's question (CITA → arrival, ORDEN → ready, AMBOS → both) from `TradeFamily`, the other behind a small "+ fecha de entrega" link.
4. small — The booking screen never shows WHAT is being booked (service names, price, duration). Fix: one summary line at the top "Corte de dama · 40 min · $ 45.000".
5. small — Past time slots of today and past days in the month grid can be picked (`WhenPicker.kt:420-429`, `SlotGrid` has no "now" check). Fix: disable slots before now and days before today.
6. small — Counter tablet: no confirmation after Save (MamaPos collects neither `bookingSavedEvent` nor shows a saved notice for a booking; `MamaPosScreen.kt:1440-1446`). Fix: collect `bookingSavedEvent` on the tablet like `MiniPosSellScreen.kt:246`.
7. small — Month grid initials "L M M J V S D" are hard-coded Spanish in every language (`WhenPicker.kt:380`); en "Open 8:00 a. m. – 8:00 p. m." uses the Spanish a. m. style while the slots say "3:00 PM" (`values/strings.xml dp_hours`); week starts Monday in US/CA. Fix: take initials and first day of week from the locale; en "Open 8:00 AM – 8:00 PM".
8. small — With one work place the where-block is hidden but both its dividers remain: two lines 24 dp apart (`BookingScreen.kt:166,171`). Fix: draw the second divider only when the block shows.
9. small (possible) — Saving a booking prints an order slip at once on any order printer and puts the ticket on a back-room screen today, with no date on it (`ui/pos/SavedTicketActions.kt:475-497`; KDS reads unready PENDING tickets). Fix: skip the slip/KDS for a ticket with a future `scheduledFor`/`readyFor`, or print the date on it.
10. small — Owner phone after Save: the C-42 snackbar ("Agendado … · Ver agenda", back to the till) and the old `showOrderSavedDialog` jump to the Agenda (`HomeScreen.kt:1331-1350`) both fire; the snackbar is cut off by the navigation. Pick one (the test `BookingSavedReturnsToAgendaTest` says "returns to the sell screen").
11. small — Opening hours are fixed 8:00–20:00 and the text says "Abres …" as if it were the shop's own hours (`WhenPicker.kt:47-49`). A vet opening at 7:00 or nails until 21:00 cannot book those hours. Fix: a "Horario" setting, or a time box for out-of-hours.
12. small — Overlap is checked per clock hour and ignores duration: a 150-min "Uñas acrílicas" at 3:00 does not warn a 4:00 booking. Fix: use the researched `duration_min` when known.
13. small — Booking needs the register OPEN even for next week (`CartActions.kt:173-184`). Fix: let the services "Agendar" path add to the cart without the register gate (charging still gated).
14. small — Amber hint text #FF9500 on white is about 2.2:1 contrast (`BookingScreen.kt:203-215`). Fix: a darker amber text token.
15. small — MANUAL receipt mode on the tablet asks a ticket NAME (free text) before the booking, then the booking still requires a real client (`MamaPosScreen.kt:1373-1376`). Fix: skip the NAME step when `booking = true`.

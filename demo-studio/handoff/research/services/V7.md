# V7 — Services and products on one ticket (shampoo, dog food, motor oil next to a service)

Commit read: `17d519ac` (app-head, sim/usa-canada). Paths under `app/src/main/java/com/example/`.

## 1. What it does, who, which device
- A services Register has two tabs: **"Servicios · N"** and **"Productos · N"** (`ui/screens/home/HomeTabLabels.kt:28-31`, rule `homeTabs = SERVICES_PRODUCTOS`). Both feed ONE cart, so a haircut and a shampoo, or a brake service and brake pads, go on the same ticket, same receipt, same payment.
- Products = `CatalogType.ITEM`; services = `SERVICE` (`sync/ServicesLibrarySplit`). Products have stock; services never do (stock queries filter `p.type = 'ITEM'`, `data/PosDao.kt:1593-1603, 1663-1674`).
- **Owner phone:** sells both, creates/edits products, sees the stock row "📦 Inventario" on the Productos tab, counts stock.
- **Counter tablet (MamaPos) and employee phone (Mini POS):** tabs "Servicios · Productos · Ventas · Agenda" (`MamaPosScreen.kt:819-830`, `MiniPosSellScreen.kt:601-607`), view-only library: they sell products but cannot create them or count stock (services never offer the stock permission: `offersStockPermission = false`, `ServicesRules.kt:62`).
- Trade differences: none in code; what differs is the catalogue each trade seeds (see 2).

## 2. Setup before the demo
- **The setup walk seeds the trade's researched PRODUCTS automatically** at "Terminar" (`ui/pos/GuideActions.kt:582-602`), filed under the same trade category as the services. Counts: Peluquería 13, Veterinaria 11, Taller de motos 12, Uñas 12.
  - Colombia: products with a researched price keep it and the "sugerido" badge (e.g. Uñas "Polvo efecto espejo" $ 9.900).
  - Every other country: all researched prices are stripped (`data/investigado/CatalogoInvestigadoAssets.kt:76-91`), so every seeded product is unpriced and the till asks the price on a keypad.
  - Names are in the phone's language at walk time (es/pt/en).
- **Brand warning for filming:** many seeded products carry real brands (Peluquería: Olaplex, L'Oréal, Schwarzkopf…; Veterinaria: Bravecto, NexGard, Royal Canin, Seresto…; Uñas: Masglo, Organic Nails, Mia Secret…). Taller de motos product NAMES are generic ("Filtro de aceite", "Bujía", "Pastillas de freno") but their axis values list brands (Motul, NGK…). Rename or delete the branded rows before a demo.
- Creating a product by hand (owner phone): Register → Productos tab → "Editar" → "+ Agregar" tile (or long-press a product to edit). With zero products the Productos tab IS the new-product form (`HomeScreen.kt:1085-1105`).
- A hand-made product is filed in the hidden system category "Otros servicios" (never the trade's chip; `ItemsScreen.kt:586-596`).

## 3. Country differences
- Prices: researched prices only in CO; abroad products ask at the till until the owner types a price.
- Tax: the "Cobrar impuesto sobre este artículo" checkbox shows only when the shop has an active tax; US/CA say "Charge sales tax on this item" (`ItemsScreen.kt:1428-1453`).
- Money: CO "$ 45.000", MX "$450.00", PE "S/ 45.00", CL "$45.000", AR "$ 4.500,00", EC/PA/US/CA "$45.00", BR "R$ 45,00".
- No fiado for services (`fiadoEnabled = false`), so no "Fiar" button.

## 4. Screens in order

### 4a. Register tabs (owner phone, Material light) — `HomeScreen.kt:343-362, 1047-1117`
- Tab row: es "Servicios · 12" / "Productos · 12" | pt "Serviços" / "Produtos" | en "Services" / "Products". Opens on Servicios.
- Productos tab with products: on top the quiet row `TappableMilestoneRow` 📦 "Inventario" | "Estoque" | "Inventory" with the right side two lines (red "3 quedan pocos" / grey "12 sin contar" | pt "3 acabando" / "12 não contados" | en "3 running low" / "12 not counted"), each hidden at 0 (`ServicesStockEntry.kt:24-38`). Then category chips ("Todos", trade name, …) and the product grid (plain product tiles; the service tile with trade icon/minutes is for services only).
- Above the grid, owner only: right-aligned "Editar" text button; editing = amber bar #FEF3C7, text #856404 "Editando · Mis servicios › · Listo" (`HomeScreen.kt:1983-2066`) and a "+ Agregar" tile (primary colour) at the grid end.
- Footer (both tabs, unless Productos is empty): "Teclado" link, current-sale strip, then the pair **left "Agendar" (word follows the trade) / right "Cobrar $ X"** (`HomeScreen.kt:760-800`). The cart mixes both tabs.

### 4b. New product form — `ui/screens/items/ItemsScreen.kt` (ItemEditorScreen), Material light
- Top bar: X, title "Nuevo **Producto**" (Producto in #007AFF) | pt "Novo Produto" | en "New Product".
- Photo box + "Nombre" field; "Precio" field (optional for services: blank = till asks; money drawn in shop format via `MoneyVisualTransformation`). No category field, no unit of sale, no description, no add-ons for services (`ItemsScreen.kt:1377-1420`).
- Switch "Contar inventario" | "Contar estoque" | "Count stock" (on by default) → block "¿Cuántos tienes?" / "¿Cuándo queda poco?" (tap a number = shared keypad sheet; no unit chip for services, `ItemsScreen.kt:1236-1264`).
- Row "¿Vendes otros tamaños?" / "Cada uno con su precio." | pt "Você vende outros tamanhos?" | en "Do you sell other sizes?" (tune icon) → options page.
- Tax checkbox (only with taxes). Button "Crear producto" | "Criar produto" | "Create product" (pill).

### 4c. Stock screen — `ui/screens/stock/StockScreen.kt:150-260`
- No title; X on the left of a tab row: "Sin contar" (red badge with count) | "Todo" (pt "Não contados" / "Tudo"; en "Not counted" / "All"). Opens on "Todo" when something is low, else on "Sin contar".
- Sin contar lead: "Nos diste el precio. No cuántos tienes." Rows: name · size, grey "?" and an amber pill "Sin contar" (#FF9500 text on cream). Screenshot `ScreenshotTests.stock_missing_full_light.png` (current).
- Tap a row → sheet "¿Cuántos tienes?" with keypad, Comprado/Contado toggle, "Cuenta lo que hay en la tienda ahora.", Guardar, "No contar este producto".

### 4d. Ticket, receipt, stock move
- Selling a counted product moves stock on the device that sold (`PosDao.kt:1643-1647`); a service never moves stock. Receipt lists service and product lines the same way.

## 6. UX/UI problems
1. **BLOCKS A DEMO (brands on screen)** — setup seeds real-brand products into every shop of these trades (`GuideActions.kt:592-602` from `assets/catalogo_investigado/*.json` `products`). Fix for demos: rename/delete; product question for Monti (see 7).
2. Abroad, every seeded product is unpriced, so the first tap on any product opens the price keypad (`productoATienda`, `CatalogoInvestigadoATienda.kt:272-304`). Not wrong, but the US/BR demo must set prices first. Small.
3. Hand-made products go to the hidden "Otros servicios" bucket (`ItemsScreen.kt:586-596`): on the owner phone they appear only under "Todos" (the trade chip doesn't show them); possible on the tablet/staff phone the chip list is unfiltered (`MamaPosScreen.kt:159`, `MiniPosSellScreen.kt:128`) and shows a chip "Otros servicios" that holds products. Fix: a "Productos" bucket, or file new products under the trade category like the seeded ones. Small (possible).
4. Stock sheet hint says "Cuenta lo que hay en la tienda ahora." / "in the shop" for a salon or vet (`stock_sheet_count_hint`). Small.
5. Staff can never count stock in a services shop (`offersStockPermission = false`), so a receptionist on the counter tablet can't receive a delivery. By design? (see 7). Small.
6. Rule name `editorHidesStockAndAdvanced = true` (`ServicesRules.kt:26`) but the product form DOES show stock; only "advanced" is hidden. Code-reading trap, no user impact.

# S7 — Recibo por WhatsApp o impreso (SERVICES): qué muestra un recibo de servicios

Commit read: `17d519ac`. Paths under `app/src/main/java/com/example/`.
Base: restaurant `S7.md` (d1b7698) and retail `S7.md`. Sale complete, the WhatsApp / text / email route, "Ver recibo" and "Venta #N" are the same code for every type. Below: what a SERVICES receipt actually contains, and what changed since.

## 1. What it does, who, which device
After the sale, "¡Venta completada!" offers WhatsApp (Latin America, Brazil) or "Recibo por mensaje" + "Recibo por correo" (US/CA), "Imprimir otra vez" (receipt printer saved), "Ver recibo". Any past sale: Ventas › Ventas tab › the sale › "Venta #N" (for services the bottom tab "Ventas" opens `ServicesTransactionsScreen` with tabs "Ventas" | "Agenda", `:126-160`; the sale opens in the shared `SaleDetailHost`, `:322`).
Devices: owner phone, staff phone, counter tablet. Services print RECEIPTS only by default (`ServicesRules.kt:85`), but since PRINT-2/PRINT-5 a services printer may also be ticked "Order slips".
**Services advantage:** a charged appointment always has a client (booking needs "¿Para quién?"), so if that client has a WhatsApp number, "WhatsApp" opens straight to their chat with the receipt (no number box).

### What a services receipt shows (verified in `utils/ReceiptFormatter.kt:313-430`, the copy sent by WhatsApp / shown / reprinted)

| Included items and add-ons | YES, as "+ name" lines under the service ("+ Lavado", "+ Cepillado / secado con secador", "+ Decoración adicional, por uña ×3"); included items (switched on at 0) print exactly like paid add-ons | `ReceiptFormatter.kt:362-364`, `OptionSelectionSheet.buildSelectedOptions` |

## 2. Setup before the demo
- WhatsApp installed; the client of the appointment saved with a WhatsApp number (faster demo).
- Printer (optional): Mi negocio › Ajustes › Conexiones › "Impresoras y pantallas" › "+ Agregar impresora" (PRINT-5 four pages; tick "Recibos"). Without a printer: no "Imprimir otra vez", New sale reads "Nueva venta · Sin recibo".
- Receipt look: Ajustes › Recibos (store name, header, footer, phone/address/website switches).

## 3. Country differences
`sync/ReceiptSendRoute`: US, CA → text + email; everyone else → WhatsApp. Money on the receipt: CO `$ 70.000`, CL `$70.000`, MX `$1,234.50`, PE `S/ 1,234.50`, EC `$1.234,50`, AR `$ 1.234,50`, PA `$1,234.50`, BR `R$ 1.234,50`, US/CA `$1,234.50`. Cash rounding lines on CL/PE/CA cash sales. Date dd/MM/yyyy except US. WhatsApp text ends with "Hecho con Platata · platata.app" | "Feito com Platata · platata.app" | "Made with Platata · platata.app". Outside Colombia the researched choices do not exist at all (S3 problem 7), so the receipt can only ever say "Vacunación".

## 6. UX/UI problems (verified in code)
1. **The chosen option is missing from every stored receipt.** "Vacunación" without "Antirrábica", "Cepillado" without "Largo", "Cambio de aceite" without "Sintético": the order line stores only `cartItem.product.name` (`CheckoutActions.kt:276`), `OrderItem` has no variation name, and the past renderers print `item.productName` (`ReceiptFormatter.kt:345-357`, reprint `ReceiptBuilder.kt:150-151`). Only the live paper print shows it (`ReceiptActions.kt:161-163`), so the paper and the WhatsApp copy of the same sale differ. Same gap for restaurant sizes. Fix: store the line name as "Vacunación (Antirrábica)" when the variation has a real name (no Room change), or add the variation name to the order line.
2. **Printed receipt shows empty brackets: "1x Corte de dama ()".** The live print appends `(${variation.name})` whenever the name is not "Regular" (`ReceiptActions.kt:161`), but researched services and keypad-priced services have a variation named "" (`CatalogoInvestigadoATienda.kt:109`, `CartActions.kt` ask-price variation `name = ""`). Every printed services receipt for a one-price or typed-price service shows "()". Fix: `if (item.variation.name.isNotBlank() && item.variation.name != "Regular")`.
3. Included items print like add-ons: "+ Kit desechable", "+ Limado y forma de la uña" sit beside "+ Decoración adicional, por uña ×3" with no hint which ones cost extra; a manicure prints five "+" lines for things that were included (`OptionSelectionSheet.kt:417-447` turns every switched-on 0-price item into a line). Small. Fix: leave 0-price included items off the receipt, or group them as "Incluye: …".
4. No "who did it" and no "where" (unless priced) on the receipt or on "Venta #N" (see table). For a salon, "Atendió: Carla" is expected on the receipt. Product question (§7). The owner-phone value "Tienda Local" (ROUND3 #20) is still open and appears on "Venta #N" for services too.
5. Small: the default footer "¡Gracias por tu compra!" (`receipt_footer_default`, seeded for every type by `ReceiptFooterSeed.kt`) reads odd for a vet or a haircut. Fix: per type, "¡Gracias por tu visita!" | "Obrigado pela visita!" | "Thanks for your visit!" for services.
6. Small, possible: on the paper roll, add-on lines are cut at the paper width (`"   + $mod".take(w)`, `PrintManager.kt:353-357`), e.g. "   + Exfoliación e hidratación d" on 58 mm; the kitchen slip already wraps with `TicketWrap`. Fix: wrap like the slip.
7. Small, possible: a services printer ticked "Order slips" prints a "Comanda" with "Mesero: …" (`receipt_label_order_ticket`, `receipt_label_waiter`, `CheckoutActions.kt:595-610`) — restaurant words on a salon's slip. Fix: hide the order-slip choice for services (their rules say RECEIPT_ONLY) or use neutral words.

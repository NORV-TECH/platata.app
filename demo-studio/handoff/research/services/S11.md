## 1. What it shows for a services shop, who, where
- Owner phone: panel row "Reportes" with subtitle "Ventas netas hoy · $ 412.000" outside US/CA, "Today · $412.00" in US/CA
  (`HomeScreen.kt:1779-1795`, `NetSalesWords.outsideUsCa`). Black bottom button "Resumen para el contador" (S12).
- Employee phone: More panel row "Reportes · Mira cómo va el negocio" only with "Ver reportes" (`MoreScreen.kt:137-145`); no
  "Ventas por empleado", grey "Solo ves las ventas de esta caja." Counter tablet: no Reports entry (unchanged).
- **By service** = "Artículos con más ingresos / Itens com maior receita / Top items by revenue" (up to 10 rows, each "N vendidos"),
  one row per service name as rung (`productName` of the line: "Corte de dama", "Vacunación"…). There is no services-specific report
  (no minutes worked, no bookings kept / no-shows, no repeat clients) — not found in code (searched `ReportsScreen.kt`, `ReportsStore`).
- **By category** = "Ventas de artículos por categoría": services are filed under the TRADE name chosen at setup ("Peluquería",
  `GuideActions.finishBridge` → `getOrCreateCategoryByName(tradeNameRes(slug))`, `ui/pos/GuideActions.kt:488-491`); products and hand-made
  items land in the seeded system category **"Otros servicios" / "Outros serviços" / "Other services"** (`SeedStore.kt:184-191`,
  `ItemsScreen.kt:587-593`). A one-trade salon therefore sees two bars: "Peluquería" and "Otros servicios" (= the shampoo it sold).
- **By employee** = "Ventas por empleado", owner only, "N ventas" under each. The name is `Order.employeeName = currentUser` at the moment
  of CHARGING (`ui/pos/CheckoutActions.kt:395`; a saved booking is restamped when charged, `SavedTicketActions.kt:258`). There is **no
  "who did the service" field** on the order or on a line (`data/Order.kt:80-313`, `data/OrderItem.kt:27-72`; searched performedBy /
  stylist / assigned: not found). A salon where the owner charges every client shows everything under the owner.
- **By where** = "Ventas por tipo de consumo" (shown only with 2+ values in the period): for services the order's `diningOptionName`
  holds the where-mode ("En el local" / "A domicilio" / "Recogen y entregan", `CartActions.resolvedWhereModeName`, :709-722). With only
  "En el local" switched on (the default) it never shows.
- **Trip charge**: the "A domicilio" price rides the fee path, so it appears as "Cargos por servicio +$ 20.000"; its page lists it by name
  "A domicilio". Tips (US/CA): services ask for a tip by default (`ServicesRules.tipDefaultOn = true`), so "Propinas" is a normal line there.
- No fiado lines, no stock figures.

## 6. UX/UI problems (verified)
1. "Ventas por empleado" credits whoever **charged**, not who did the service (`CheckoutActions.kt:395`; no performer field). In a salon
   this reads as stylist sales and is wrong whenever the owner charges at the counter. Small for the screen, big for the promise — see
   open question. Fix later: a "who did it" on the booking / line.
2. Product sales of a services shop are filed and reported under **"Otros servicios"** (`ItemsScreen.kt:587-593`, `SeedStore.kt:184-191`), so
   the category list calls shampoo a service. Fix: a separate "Productos" system category for `CatalogType.ITEM`.
3. Restaurant words on a services report: "Ventas por tipo de consumo" for the where-modes (`reports_dining_title`), "Artículos con más
   ingresos" for services (`reports_top_items`), and the home-visit price under "Cargos por servicio". Fix: services titles ("Ventas por
   lugar", "Servicios con más ingresos", "Cargos por ir a domicilio"). Small.
4. Possible: the where-mode name is stored in the app language at sale time (`app.getString(where_mode_*)`, `CartActions.kt:713-720`), and
   `localizedDiningOption` only translates built-in dining names, so a shop whose language changes shows "En el local" and "In the shop" as two
   rows. Small.
5. Same as restaurant (still open): `"Tienda Local"` is still the Spanish fallback seller name in every language (`ui/pos/PosViewModelTypes.kt:534, 545`,
   `AuthActions.kt:334`; already flagged ROUND3 #20) — it appears as a row in "Ventas por empleado".

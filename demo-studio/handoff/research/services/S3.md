# S3 — Cobrar en efectivo (SERVICES, owner phone mainly)

Commit read: `17d519ac`. Paths under `app/src/main/java/com/example/`.
Base: restaurant `S3.md` (pay screen, keypad, Exacto, change, Sale complete) and retail `S3.md`. The pay screen (`CheckoutSheetBody` → `CheckoutPayBlock`) and "¡Venta completada!" are the same code. Below: what is different for services, and what changed since d1b7698.

## 1. What it does, who, which device
The owner (or a PIN-signed employee) taps services on the Servicios tab (or products on Productos), taps "Cobrar $X", takes cash on the pay screen, lands on "¡Venta completada!".
Services differences:
- **The sell screen is a grid of SERVICE tiles** (`ProductCard.kt:250-335`, `ServiceTileBody`): trade icon in a 40 dp tinted box (services accent #7C55E0, soft #EEEAF5), name (bodyLarge 500, 2 lines), price bold left and "40 min" right (minutes only for a researched service, CU-21). Price label: "Sin precio" | "Sem preço" | "No price" when the service has none, "Desde $ X" | "A partir de" | "From" for FROM mode (`ProductCard.kt:101-107`), and a small "sugerido" | "sugerido" | "suggested" under a researched price not yet reviewed.
- **A tap can open three different things** (`CartActions.handleProductClick`, `:454-497`):
  a) service with a priced choice (e.g. Vacunación, Cepillado, Cambio de aceite) → "Elige uno" sheet listing each choice with its price (`VariationSelectionSheet.kt`);
  b) service with no price, or set to "En la caja" (ASK) → the keypad titled with the service's name, "Agregar" (`HomeScreen.kt:983-1043`);
  c) service with options (included items, paid add-ons) → the option sheet (`OptionSelectionSheet.kt`): included items as switches already ON ("Lavado"), paid add-ons OFF with "+$ 25.000", per-unit add-ons with "−/+" ("Decoración adicional, por uña · $ 2.000 por uña"), unknown-price add-ons "· se pregunta en la caja" (asks the amount when switched on), button "Agregar a la venta · $ X" (`opt_add_to_sale`; pt "Adicionar à venda · %1$s", en "Add to sale · %1$s") — but for a researched service X is only the add-ons, see problem 1; with nothing extra on it reads just "Agregar" | "Adicionar" | "Add" (`common_add`).
- **No Save button and no Fiar** on the pay screen (`hasSaveTicketButton = false`, `ServicesRules.kt:47`; `CheckoutSheetBody.kt:137, 1065, 1151`). The "save for later" is the footer's "Agendar" (S1/booking), not part of cash.
- **"¿Dónde se hace?" gate** above the customer field when the shop has 2 or 3 ways of working on (`CheckoutSheetBody.kt:302-308, 409-418`, `where/WhereGate.kt`): chips "En el local" / "A domicilio" / "Recogen y entregan" (pt "No local" / "A domicílio" / "Retirada e entrega"; en "In the shop" / "At their place" / "Pickup and delivery"), hint "Escoge uno para seguir." Pay buttons are off until one is picked. With only "En el local" (factory) nothing is drawn and the sale is saved "En el local". A mode with a price adds its own line to the sale ("A domicilio: $ 10.000") through the fee path (`AutoRules.kt:68-90`).
- **No fiado** for services (`ServicesRules.kt:15`, Monti 26 Sep). **No service charge** seeded for a salon any more (MKT3-3 R5, `SeedStore.kt:202-205`), so no "¿Cargo por servicio?" Sí/No card at checkout.
- Devices: staff phone's "Cobrar" opens the cart step first, then the same pay body (`MiniPosSellScreen.kt:720-756`); the counter tablet uses the pay body inline. Same cash behaviour.
- Trades: beauty / pets / vehicles / repairs / home behave the same; only the left footer word changes ("Agendar" for appointment trades, "Crear orden" for workshops that keep the job, "Con fecha" for mixed; `data/TradeFamily`).

## 6. UX/UI problems (verified in code)
1. **MONEY DISPLAY — the option sheet's button shows the wrong amount for a researched service.** For a SERVICE the button total is `pickedServicePrice(...) ?: 0.0` + add-ons (`OptionSelectionSheet.kt:283-286`), and `pickedServicePrice` only reads a PER_OPTION set (`:470-482`). A researched service's price lives on its VARIATION (the priced pick-one became variations, `CatalogoInvestigadoATienda.kt:90-115`), not on a PER_OPTION set, so the base is 0: "Corte de dama" ($ 45.000) + "Cepillado / secado" (+$ 25.000) reads "Agregar a la venta · $ 25.000" but rings $ 70.000; Manicure + 3 decorated nails reads "$ 6.000", rings $ 34.000; with no add-on it reads only "Agregar". This breaks the S8-B18 rule "printed == rung"; `OptionSheetCtaTotalE24Test` only covers a service priced by a PER_OPTION set. Fix: for a service use `pickedServicePrice ?: basePrice` (the variation the host hands in, already passed by `ProductSheetHost.kt:160`).
2. A choice with no price shows "$ 0" in the "Elige uno" sheet (`VariationSelectionSheet.kt:116` prints `formatCurrency(variation.price)`, and an unpriced choice is stored as `UNSET_PRICE = 0.0`, `data/Product.kt:62`). E.g. Taller de motos › Cambio de aceite: "Semisintético $ 0" (the corpus has no price for it); the tap then asks the price. Fix: show "Sin precio" (`items_price_unset`) when `Product.hasNoPrice`.
3. The words "artículo / artículos" count services: strip "Venta actual · 1 artículo" (`home_current_sale_items`) and pay link "Artículos (1)" (`checkout_items_link`) for a haircut. Small. Fix: "servicio"/"concepto" for services, or a neutral word.
4. Possible: a service set to "En la caja" (ASK) that still carries a stored price shows that price on its tile (`ProductCard.kt:101-107` only special-cases no-price and FROM) but the tap asks for a price anyway (`CartActions.kt:470-474`). Fix: show "En la caja" on the tile, like "Mis servicios" does.
5. Small: the Elige-uno rows carry a shopping-cart icon (`VariationSelectionSheet.kt:100-104`) — reads "product" for a vaccine or a haircut choice. Fix: no icon, or the trade icon.
6. Small: per-unit stepper buttons in the option sheet are bare `TextButton("−")` / `("+")` (`OptionSelectionSheet.kt:236-251`), smaller than the 48 dp the restaurant add-on sheet uses (MENU-2 item 6). Fix: reuse the 48 dp round buttons.
7. **Outside Colombia the researched choices disappear with the prices** (`CatalogoInvestigadoAssets.kt:76-90` sets every `precioCo` to null; `servicioATienda` then makes no choices from an unpriced pick-one, `CatalogoInvestigadoATienda.kt:89-91`). A vet in Mexico or the US cannot pick the vaccine at the till and the receipt only says "Vacunación". Fix: keep the choice names as variations with UNSET prices (the CU-19 price list already asks a box per choice). Possible product decision: see §7.
8. Still open from ROUND3 #20 and also hits services: every sale rung on the owner's phone with no PIN session is stored with `employeeName = "Tienda Local"` (`PosViewModelTypes.kt:545`, `CheckoutActions.kt:395`), in every language; Sale #N reads "Empleado: Tienda Local".

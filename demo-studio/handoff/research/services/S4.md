## 1. What it does, who, which device
The client pays by scanning the shop's own bank QR; the cashier checks the bank and taps "Pagó". In Brazil the QR carries the sale total (Pix).
- Setup: owner, owner phone, Ajustes › "Métodos de pago".
- Charging: owner phone, staff phone (services staff phone opens the cart step, then the same pay body), counter tablet. The QR picture reaches staff devices through the synced reference.
- Services fit: a salon or vet at the counter where the client pays with Nequi/Daviplata QR (CO), Yape/Plin QR (PE), Mercado Pago QR (AR/MX), Pix (BR). Same for all 41 trades.

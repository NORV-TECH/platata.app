# V9 — Clients book online from a link (SERVICES)

Code read: commit `17d519ac` (branch sim/usa-canada). App paths under `app/src/main/java/com/example/`. Web page: `public/store/*` (app.js, logic.js, strings.js, store.css). Server: `functions/storefront-projection.js`, `functions/online-store-hours.js`, `functions-public/index.js` (submitOnlineOrder).
Shared parts (unlock rule, link, address step, owner settings page) are in restaurant `S20.md`; services owner-side differences are in `services/S20.md`. This file is the customer's booking and how it lands.

## 1. What it does, who, where
- The customer opens the shop's link `platata-app.web.app/<address>` on any phone browser, picks a SERVICE, picks a day (next 21 days) and a time, types name + WhatsApp number, taps "Reservar". Nothing is charged online: "Pagas en el negocio". The page then says "Reserva solicitada" + a 6-letter code; "El negocio te confirmará la hora."
- The booking lands on the owner's phone, the counter tablet and every employee phone as an open ticket with a date (`scheduledFor`) = a row in the **Agenda** tagged "EN LÍNEA". The owner confirms by WhatsApp (by hand) and charges it at the appointment with "Cobrar".
- Bookable = a SERVICE-type item on a SERVICES shop (`functions/storefront-projection.js:184-186` `isBookable`; app twin `sync/OnlineStoreSellable.kt:56`). PRODUCTS of the same shop are sold on a second tab for pickup (see S20).
- No collision check: two clients can book the same 10:00 (`functions-public/index.js:34` "No collision check — the owner resolves clashes on the till"). The page never says "free/taken" (`public/store/logic.js:434-437`).
- One slot length for every service: the grid step = the store's "Duración de la cita" (`storefront-projection.js:340` `slotMinutes = prepMinutes`), chips 10/15/20/30/45/60 min (`sync/SellOnline.kt:121`). The per-service researched minutes are NOT used online.
- Trades: no per-trade difference in the booking code. Taller-type trades (ORDEN family, e.g. taller-motos, `data/TradeFamily.kt:59`) still get "Reservar / ¿Cuándo?" online, while their owner button says "Crear orden".

## 2. Setup needed before the demo
1. Store unlocked: US/CA always; elsewhere a tablet or employee phone must USE a paid (3rd) connection (`sync/StoreLockRule.kt`, restaurant S20 §2). For a one-chair Uñas this means two extra devices.
2. Settings › panel row "Vender en línea" → switch on → step 1 address → step 2 "Tu horario" (open/close, "Duración de la cita") → "Encender la tienda".
3. **Services the page can actually book (critical).** A service is left off the page when (`OnlineStoreSellable.kt:58-69`): no price, price 0, "Precio sugerido", or "Tiene opciones que la página aún no muestra" (any item_option_set link).
   - Every service created by the setup walk gets its researched options ("Lo que trae" / "Cobra más") written as option sets linked to the service (`ui/pos/GuideActions.kt:689-703`, `insertItemOptionSetCrossRef`). Every starter service of peluqueria, unas, veterinaria and taller-motos has at least one such option (checked in `assets/catalogo_investigado/*.json`: 0 of 21 / 0 of 18 / 0 of 16 / 1 of 19 services have none). **So walk-made services never appear online.**
   - Also: in Colombia a price left on the suggested pill keeps "Precio sugerido" (`GuideActions.kt:550`, `BridgeScreen.kt:185-199`), another reason to be left off.
   - Workaround for the demo: make the services by hand: till amber bar "Editando" › "Mis servicios" › "+ Nuevo" › trade picker link "Mi oficio no está" › new-service form (name + price) (`ui/navigation/OnlineNavGraph.kt:83-87`). These have no options and a set price → bookable.
4. Android notification permission (asked once when the store is switched on). Keep the owner phone app OPEN: alerts are local (`ui/pos/OnlineOrderAlerts.kt:25-27`; server push not deployed).

## 3. Country differences
- Eligible everywhere in CO MX PE CL EC AR PA BR US CA. Pay option for SERVICES is ONLY "pay at the shop" in every country (`ServicesRules.kt:18-19` `onlinePayOptions = PAY_AT_PICKUP`, `cardOnlineAllowed = false`; server refuses card on a booking, `functions-public/index.js:212`). No card online, no Pix online, in any country, US/CA included.
- Unlock: US/CA free; others need the 3rd connection in use.
- Page language = the shop's language (es / pt / en), not the browser's. Times always in the shop's time zone. Clock: es/pt "10:00", en "10:00 AM" (`logic.js:393-397`).
- Money on the page (`public/store/money.js`): CO "$ 28.000", MX "$350.00", PE "S/ 45.00", CL "$15.000", EC "$15,00", AR "$ 15.000,00", PA "$25.00", BR "R$ 45,00", US "$35.00", CA "$35.00". The booking price is the till's maths (automatic discounts/fees; US/CA additive tax inside the total) (`app.js:675-678`, `logic.js:602-616`).

## 6. UX/UI problems
1. **BLOCKS A DEMO — services made by the setup walk never reach the booking page.** Their researched options are saved as option sets (`GuideActions.kt:689-703`), and any option-set link means "Tiene opciones que la página aún no muestra" (`OnlineStoreSellable.kt:67`, `storefront-projection.js:196`). All four demo trades are hit. The customer then sees an empty page (or only the Products tab). Fix: publish INCLUIDO options as text and COBRA MÁS options as add-ons for services, or let the page show option sets.
2. **BLOCKS A DEMO (CO) — an untouched suggested price keeps the service off the page** ("Precio sugerido", `GuideActions.kt:550`; `BridgeScreen.kt:185-199`; services with choices are never marked reviewed, `BridgeScreen.kt:185`). Fix: treat "Siguiente" on the bridge as accepting the shown price, or publish suggested prices.
3. One slot length for all services, max 60 min (`SellOnline.kt:121`, `storefront-projection.js:340`): a 150-min "Uñas acrílicas" books into a 60-min grid and overlaps the next client. Fix: use each service's minutes, or allow 90/120/180.
4. Online "Desde" and "En la caja" prices are shown as a fixed price (projection ignores `servicePriceMode`, `storefront-projection.js:225-236`). Fix: "Desde $ X" on the card; hide or label ASK services. Small.
5. Opening the booking from the Agenda list does not silence its alert; only tapping the notification, charging or cancelling does (`AppointmentDetailScreen.kt` never calls `onlineOrderSeen`; `PosViewModel.kt:2388`). A booking stays PENDING for days, so every device rings 11 times over 10 minutes. Fix: call `onlineOrderSeen` when the appointment page opens. Small.
6. Notification body says "1 producto" for a service (`OnlineOrderAlerts.kt:156`, plural `online_alert_items`). Fix: "1 servicio" for a booking. Small.
7. Confirming is fully manual: the WhatsApp button opens an empty chat (`AppointmentDetailScreen.kt:264`) and there is no "confirmed" mark, while the customer was told "El negocio te confirmará la hora." Fix: prefill "Hola Laura, tu cita de Manicure tradicional queda el mié 22 a las 10:00." Small.
8. Cancelling or no-show on an online booking tells the customer nothing (no WhatsApp offer on cancel, `AppointmentDetailScreen.kt:372-381`). Small.
9. Clashes only show inside the appointment page, not on the Agenda row (`ServicesTransactionsScreen.kt:392`). Possible; small.
10. Browser tab title says "<shop> · Pedir en línea" on a booking page (`app.js:962`, `title_suffix`). Small.
11. Customer page shop name is iOS blue #007AFF, not the services purple #7C55E0 (already noted for restaurant). Small.

# V8 — At the shop / at the client's place / pickup ("¿Dónde se hace?")

Commit read: `17d519ac` (app-head, sim/usa-canada). Paths under `app/src/main/java/com/example/`.

## 1. What it does, who, which device
- The owner turns on up to two extra ways of working besides "En el local": **"A domicilio"** (you go to the client) and **"Recogen y entregan"** (you collect the thing and bring it back). Each can carry ONE fixed charge ("¿Cobras por ir?"). When 2 or 3 ways are on, every sale and every booking must say which one; the charge rides the ticket as its own line.
- SERVICES only: `ServicesRules.asksWhere = true` (`data/business/ServicesRules.kt:46`); every other type answers false.
- **Owner phone only, in practice.** The setting is `app_metadata["where_modes"]` (`data/repo/ShopSettingsStore.kt:95-100`) and is NOT in the reference snapshot (the adopt block `data/repo/BranchSnapshotStore.kt:401-427` copies business_type, currency, country, online_store, owner settings, language — no where_modes). So a counter tablet / employee phone always reads the default (only "En el local"): the chooser never shows and no home-visit charge is ever added there (see problem 1).
- Same for all 41 trades — there is no per-trade default. Best fits: Uñas (one-person manicurist who goes to homes), Peluquería (bridal hair at home), Taller de motos ("Recogen y entregan": pick up the bike, return it), Veterinaria (home visit).

## 2. Setup before the demo
- Factory default: only "En el local" (domicilio and recogida OFF, amount 0) — `data/WhereModes.kt:71-74`. With one mode nothing is asked anywhere (`showsWhereBlock()` needs 2+, `WhereModes.kt:88`).
- Owner phone: Panel → Configuración → **"Dónde atiendes"** (row in "Tu negocio", subtitle "Local, domicilio, recogida") → switch "A domicilio" ON → type the charge (e.g. 15000) → "Listo". The setup walk (trade picker / prices) never asks this; Settings is the only door (`ui/screens/settings/SettingsHubScreen.kt:99`, route `where_modes` in `ui/navigation/OnlineNavGraph.kt:187`).
- A client in the list helps the booking demo ("¿Para quién?").

## 3. Country differences
- Same everywhere (services in all countries). Only the money changes. The fee box placeholder is `currencySymbol() + " —"` (`WhereScreen.kt:236`).
- Charge examples on the ticket line: CO "A domicilio: $ 15.000", MX "$150.00", PE "S/ 20.00", CL "$5.000", AR "$ 4.500,00", EC/PA/US/CA "$25.00", BR "A domicílio: R$ 30,00".
- The fee is a plain FIXED amount; no %/per-km (the enum `WhereSurcharge` still exists but the screen no longer offers kinds, `WhereScreen.kt:216-219`).
- US/CA: the fee line is a fee, so sales tax/tip behave as for any whole-ticket fee (no special code).

### 4e. How the mode shows afterwards
- **Sale:** stored in `Order.diningOptionName` as the word in the app's language at that moment ("A domicilio"), `ui/pos/CartActions.kt:709-722`, `CheckoutActions.kt:397`.
- **Receipt:** only the FEE line prints ("A domicilio: $ 15.000", `utils/ReceiptFormatter.kt:374-378`). With no charge (amount empty) or "En el local", the receipt says nothing about where.
- **Reports:** the fee appears under "Cargos por servicio" by name; with 2+ modes used, a list "Ventas por tipo de consumo" | "Vendas por tipo de consumo" | "Sales by dining option" splits sales by mode (`ui/screens/reports/ReportsScreen.kt:424-436`).
- **Agenda / appointment detail:** the mode is NOT shown (`AppointmentDetailScreen.kt`, `ServicesTransactionsScreen.kt` never read `diningOptionName`).

## 6. UX/UI problems
1. **BLOCKS A DEMO (multi-device)** — where_modes never reach the counter tablet / employee phone: not in the reference (`BranchSnapshotStore.kt:401-427`), read only from the device's own DB (`ShopSettingsStore.kt:95-96`). A staff member can never mark a home visit, and the home-visit charge is never added there; their bookings save "En el local". Fix: carry `where_modes` in `OwnerSettingsWire` (like `ask_for_tip`) and push the reference on save.
2. The mode picked at booking is invisible later: Agenda and appointment detail do not show "A domicilio" (no reader of `diningOptionName` in `AppointmentDetailScreen.kt` / `ServicesTransactionsScreen.kt`). A home-visit business can't see which jobs are outside. Fix: show a chip "A domicilio" on the agenda card and the detail page (and the client's address if any). Small→medium.
3. Reopening a booking to charge asks "¿Dónde se hace?" again: the reopen restores the frozen fee but not the pick (`SavedTicketActions.kt:758-790` never sets `selectedWhereMode`; only the draft restore does, `DraftCartActions.kt:207`). Picking "En el local" then stores "En el local" while the ticket still carries the "A domicilio" fee line. Fix: restore the pick from `diningOptionName` on reopen. Small (possible wrong record).
4. Possible: the fee box round-trips through a Double on every keystroke (`WhereScreen.kt:232-235`, value = `seedMoneyField(amt)`): in 2-decimal currencies (US/BR/PE/MX) "5" becomes "5.00" and backspacing from the end gives "5.0" → re-seeded "5.00", so the amount can't be cleared by backspace; Brazil also shows "5.00" with a dot. Fix: keep the typed text in local state, use `MoneyVisualTransformation`, save on Listo. Small.
5. Reports call the split "Ventas por tipo de consumo" ("Sales by dining option") for a salon (`ReportsScreen.kt:430`). Fix: a services wording "Ventas por dónde se hizo". Small.
6. Stored mode name is in the app language of the moment (`CartActions.kt:712-720`), not a key: changing the app language splits Reports into two rows ("A domicilio" / "At their place"). Small.
7. Words: page title "¿Dónde se hace?" vs Settings row "Dónde atiendes" vs report title "Dónde trabajas" (`rp_page_where`); pt "Retirada e entrega" vs subtitle "coleta". Pick one name. Small.
8. With no charge typed, nothing on the receipt or sale says it was a home visit (only the fee line carries the word). Small.

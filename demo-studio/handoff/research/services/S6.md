# S6 — Otras formas de pago (SERVICES)

Commit read: `17d519ac`. Paths under `app/src/main/java/com/example/`.
Base: restaurant `S6.md` (d1b7698) and retail `S6.md`. Custom methods (Nequi, Yape, Mercado Pago, Zelle…) and "Cortesía" work the same for every type. Below: services differences and code changes since.

## 1. What it does, who, which device
Besides Efectivo, QR and Tarjeta, a service can be paid with any method the owner names, or given free ("Cortesía" / "On the house").
- **No fiado for services.** `ServicesRules.kt:15` `fiadoEnabled = false` (Monti, 26 Sep): the Fiado row is stripped from every list (`data/FiadoScope.kt:12-13`, so Payment methods shows no Fiado row and no "Siempre activo… toca Fiar" note), the pay screen has NO Save/Fiar button for services at all (`hasSaveTicketButton = false`, `CheckoutSheetBody.kt:137, 1151`). Paying later is done by booking ("Agendar") and charging the appointment afterwards (S1/T-booking), not by a tab.
- Devices: owner creates methods (owner phone, Ajustes › Métodos de pago). Owner phone, staff phone and counter tablet use them (synced). "Cortesía" shows only where comp is allowed (owner always; staff only with the "canComp" grant).
- Trades: no difference.

## 2. Setup before the demo
- Factory (all countries): Efectivo ON, Pago QR OFF, Tarjeta OFF (card countries), Cortesía ON; no custom method seeded. So a new salon's pay screen shows "Otro método de pago (1)" (only Cortesía).
- Add one: Ajustes › "Métodos de pago" › "+ Nuevo" → "Agregar método de pago" | "Adicionar forma de pagamento" | "Add Payment Method" → field "Nombre (ej. Nequi)" — **the example now follows the country** (`sync/PaymentMethodExample.kt`, `PaymentMethodsScreen.kt:792`): CO Nequi, PE Yape, AR/MX Mercado Pago, US Zelle, CA Interac e-Transfer; CL/EC/PA/BR "Transferencia" | "Transferência" (restaurant S6 problem 2 closed). Up to 10 methods.

## 3. Country differences
Only the example name and money format. CO `$ 45.000`, MX `$1,234.50`, PE `S/ 1,234.50`, CL `$45.000`, EC `$1.234,50`, AR `$ 1.234,50`, PA `$1,234.50`, BR `R$ 1.234,50`, US/CA `$1,234.50`. No rounding (cash only).

## 1. What differs for services, who, where
- Same Team page on the owner's phone (panel row "Equipo · 2 empleados · 1 en su celular"). The people are stylists, nail artists, vets,
  mechanics; their trade shows only through the free "Cargo" text, which is also the row badge ("Estilista", "Mecánico").
- **Permission chips for services: 8** (`TeamManagementScreen.kt:1332-1340`, sheet :1458-1520): "Devolver" · "Abrir y cerrar caja" ·
  "Quitar cargos" · "Anular ítems" · "Puede dar cortesía" · "Cancelar recibos" · "Editar clientes" · "Ver reportes"
  (pt "Devolver · Abrir e fechar caixa · Remover taxas · Anular itens · Pode cortesia · Cancelar recibos · Editar clientes · Ver relatórios";
  en "Can refund · Open and close register · Remove fees · Void items · Can comp · Cancel receipts · Edit clients · See reports").
  "Controlar el stock" is NOT offered (`ServicesRules.offersStockPermission = false`), although services keep a Stock screen for their products.
- **Bookings ride an existing chip.** Cancelling a booking AND "No asistió" both call `cancelAppointment`, which needs "Cancelar recibos"
  (`ui/pos/AppointmentActions.kt:83-84`, `actorAllows` = the Mini POS grant, else the PIN-session person, else the owner's phone). Booking,
  moving ("Reprogramar") and charging need no grant. There is no booking-specific permission (`data/Employee.kt:36-66`: no canBook/canCancelAppointment).
- **Who performs a service: not found in code.** A booking has no staff member (`BookingScreen.kt`: "¿Para quién?" is the CLIENT); the time
  picker is one calendar for the whole shop and a slot shows only the first booked client name (`ui/screens/services/WhenPicker.kt:522-556`);
  an order stores only the person who charged (`Order.employeeName`), lines have no person (`data/OrderItem.kt`). So a 3-chair salon cannot
  see "Laura's 10:00" vs "Camila's 10:00".
- Devices: employee phone (Mini POS) for a stylist (tabs Servicios · Productos · Ventas · Agenda, footer "Agendar" + "Cobrar"); counter tablet
  (reception) signs in with "Elige tu perfil" + PIN. Both get the person's grants.

## 6. UX/UI problems (verified)
1. **BLOCKS A DEMO — a stylist without "Cancelar recibos" taps "No asistió" or "Cancelar cita", confirms, the screen closes as if done, and
   nothing happened.** The buttons are always drawn (`AppointmentDetailScreen.kt:186-212`), the dialog calls `cancelAppointment` then `onDone()`
   (:363-368, :376-380), and the action returns silently on a refused grant (`AppointmentActions.kt:84`). Every new employee starts without the grant.
   Fix: hide or dim the two buttons without the grant and say "Pide a … que la cancele", or give bookings their own grant.
2. The grant that controls bookings is called "Cancelar recibos / Cancel receipts" — nothing on the chip says it covers citas and no-shows. Fix:
   for services, label it "Cancelar ventas y citas".
3. Small/possible: no "Controlar el stock" chip for services (`offersStockPermission = false`) while services count product stock — only the owner can count.
4. Still open (already flagged ROUND3 #26, also hits services): `team_new_pin_notice` says the PIN "no se vuelve a mostrar completo" / "will not
   be shown in full again", but the form shows it again with "Mostrar" (`TeamManagementScreen.kt:345-349`).
5. `team_code_sub` still says the PIN is for "una tablet, o … una devolución" — not that it is also how the employee joins a phone (already flagged ROUND3 #23).

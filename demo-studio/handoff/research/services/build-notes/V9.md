## 3. New UX/UI problems (verified at 37db6d6c)
1. BLOCKS real bookings (not this demo) — the server checks the slot against the shop's `slotMinutes` (default 20) while the page
   builds the grid on the service's `durationMin`: `functions-public/index.js:231-238` vs `public/store/app.js:400-405`. A 30-min
   service with the default 20-min "Duración de la cita": 9:30, 10:30… are shown and refused "Esa hora ya no se puede reservar".
   Fix: `slotFits` with the booked service's `durationMin` (fallback `slotMinutes`), same as the page.
2. money — a FROM service is priced by its FIRST stored choice (`functions/storefront-projection.js:145-149,291`), the till by the
   cheapest (`sync/ServicesMenuRule.kt:37-42`): CO "Retoque de raíz" page "Desde $ 70.000" vs app "Desde $ 40.000"; the page's
   sum "Pagas en el negocio · $ X" (`app.js:481-484`) then reads as a fixed price and the booking carries choice 1's price.
   Same root as VS20 #1. Fix: publish the lowest priced choice and say "Desde" in the sum.
3. small — `.tnote` has no side padding (`public/store/store.css:109`, used at `app.js:470-475`): the "¿Cuándo?" notes touch the
   left edge while the sections are inset. Fix: `padding: 0 16px` like `.sect`.
4. small — pt/en button reads "Reservar sex., 23 14:00" (`app.js:409-412,488`: Intl short weekday keeps "sex.," + comma). Fix:
   `formatToParts`, drop the comma / trailing dot.
5. small — still open: tab title "… · Tienda en línea" on a booking page (`app.js:990`, `title_suffix`); shop name in #007AFF, not
   the shop accent (`store.css:23`); the Agenda row gives no sign when an online booking lands on a taken time; Sell online's
   "…los pedidos nuevos" (`SellOnlineScreen.kt:313`, also VS20 #4).

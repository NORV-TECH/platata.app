# V4 — The agenda (where today's appointments are seen, and what can be done with one)

Checked against code at commit **17d519ac** (branch sim/usa-canada). Paths under `app/src/main/java/com/example/`.
No screenshot of the Agenda or the appointment detail exists in `app/src/test/screenshots/`.

## 1. What it does, who, where
- One list of every UNPAID booking (any open ticket that has an arrival or ready date), oldest first, overdue ones on top. A tap opens the booking with four exits: Cobrar (charge), No asistió (no-show), Reprogramar (move), Cancelar cita. Paid bookings leave the list and show in "Ventas"; cancelled / no-show ones show in "Ventas" with the "Anulada" badge.
- There is **no calendar or day view**: no week grid, no per-day header, no "today" filter, no per-person column. I don't see one in the code (searched `calendar`, `day view`, `agenda` in `ui/screens`; the only calendars are the date picker inside booking and the buildings' Pendientes).
- **Where the owner finds it**: bottom bar "Ventas" | "Vendas" | "Sales" (`ui/navigation/NavLabels.kt:24`) → services open `ServicesTransactionsScreen` (`ui/navigation/ReportsNavGraph.kt:55-63`, rule `ServicesRules.transactionsDestination = AGENDA`) with two tabs "Ventas | Agenda N", **Agenda first**. The till, the business panel and Home show nothing about today's appointments (no panel row: `workspaceHasServicesRows` only feeds Settings › "Where you work").
- Devices:
  - Owner phone: the screen above with its own tab row and an X.
  - Counter tablet: 4th tab "Agenda" (`ui/screens/mamapos/MamaPosScreen.kt:819-831`, `:1226-1238`); a tap opens the same detail over the pane (`:1505-1520`).
  - Employee phone: 4th tab "Agenda" (`ui/screens/onboarding/MiniPosSellScreen.kt:603-608`, `:651-668`); a tap navigates to the same detail route.
- Same for every trade; the only trade difference is the row label (arrival = "Viene", ready-only = "Domicilio", see problem 1).
- **Appointment notifications / reminders**: the `BOOKING` alert ("Citas nuevas" | "Novos agendamentos" | "New bookings", line "Cuando un cliente agenda en línea") rings ONLY for a booking made on the ONLINE store (`ui/pos/OnlineOrderAlerts.kt:66-80`, `work/AlertNotifier.kt:86`). There is **no reminder before an appointment**, to the owner or to the client, and nothing on the day (searched `remind`, `recordatorio`, `scheduledFor` in `work/` and `ui/pos/`; the only reminders are the register-still-open job and the buildings' agenda).

## 2. Setup before the demo
- A services shop with 3–5 bookings made through V3: one today, one tomorrow, one next week, one yesterday (shows "Vencida"), and for the Taller one ready-date order.
- A client per booking (required by V3). For the WhatsApp move notice the client needs a real WhatsApp number.
- To demo "No asistió" the booking must be today or earlier (button hidden for future days).
- Staff phone / tablet cancel needs the employee grant "Cancelar recibos" (`canCancelReceipt`), else the cancel silently does nothing (problem 7).

## 3. Country differences
- Same screens everywhere. Time and day words follow the app language (es "3:00 p. m." / "vie 23 de oct", en "3:00 PM" / "Fri 23 Oct", pt "15:00" / "sex 23/10").
- Row amount via `formatCurrency`: CO "$ 45.000", MX "$450.00", PE "S/ 45.00", CL "$45.000", EC "$45,00", AR "$ 45.000,00", PA/US/CA "$45.00", BR "R$ 45,00".
- Phone under the name: raw "+573001234567" (WhatsApp digits with a "+"), not the readable form used on the client page.
- Charging an appointment uses the normal pay screen: card only CO (Wompi) / US-CA (Stripe), Pix amount only BR, tip screen US/CA (services default ON).
- Online bookings (from the services online store) are tagged "EN LÍNEA" and say "Reservada en línea: confirma la hora con el cliente".

## 6. UX/UI problems
1. **BLOCKS A DEMO** (Taller de motos) — A ready-date order is labelled "Domicilio" in Spanish (= home delivery) and "Delivery" in English (`ServicesTransactionsScreen.kt:357`, `values-es sx_delivery`). The motorbike is not being delivered; it is ready for pickup. pt "Entrega" is fine. Fix: es "Listo" / "Entrega", en "Ready".
2. **BLOCKS A DEMO** (Taller) — A ready date saved with "Sin hora" shows "12:00 a. m." in the row and the detail (see V3 problem 1; `ServicesTransactionsScreen.kt:343,367`).
3. **BLOCKS A DEMO** (if the video shows "today at a glance") — No day view: the Agenda is one flat list of every future booking, no day headers, no "Hoy" section, no count per day, and it hides under a bottom-bar tab called "Ventas". Fix: group rows by day with headers ("Hoy · 4 citas", "Mañana", "vie 23 de oct") like `SalesDayGroups`, and name the bottom tab "Agenda" for services.
4. **BLOCKS A DEMO** (the WhatsApp part) — The reschedule notice is a share-sheet text "Ana (+573001234567) — sáb 24 de oct · 10:00 a. m." (`AppointmentDetailScreen.kt:407-414`): no greeting, no shop name, no service, and the owner must find Ana's chat himself. Fix: open Ana's own chat (`WhatsappOpen` with `WhatsappReceipt.directNumber`) with "Hola Ana, movimos tu cita en <negocio> (Pedicure spa) para el sáb 24 de oct a las 10:00 a. m.", signed, in the shop's language.
5. small — The booking note and the where-mode are never shown on the Agenda row or the detail (no `cartNote` / `diningOptionName` in either file). A vet's pet name ("Firulais") or a home visit's address is invisible until the ticket is charged. Fix: a note line in the row and a "Nota" + "Dónde" line on the detail; for "A domicilio" show the client's address.
6. small — Agenda search "por teléfono" never matches a client made in the app: it searches `phoneNumber`, which the client form always saves as null (`ServicesTransactionsScreen.kt:208`, `:282`; `CustomerCrmScreen.kt:860`). Fix: also search `whatsappNumber`.
7. small — On the staff phone / tablet without "Cancelar recibos", "Cancelar cita" and "No asistió" are shown, the dialog closes, and nothing happens (`AppointmentActions.kt:97` returns silently; `AppointmentDetailScreen.kt:365-381`). Fix: hide the two buttons without the grant, or say why.
8. small — "No asistió" shows all day for a booking later today (before its hour) (`AppointmentDetailScreen.kt:186`); the comment says "once the hour is here". Fix: compare the time, not only the day.
9. small — The detail shows only the FIRST service and its duration (`AppointmentDetailScreen.kt:95-96`); the row too. A booking of "Manicure + Pedicure" reads as one service with a wrong end time. Fix: list all services, sum the known durations.
10. small — A "both dates" booking (arrival + ready) can only move its arrival; the ready date is not editable, and the arrival picker offers "Sin hora" (`AppointmentDetailScreen.kt:216-228`, `AppointmentActions.kt:52-55`). Fix: two pickers for BOTH.
11. small — Owner screen top bar uses an X (Close) on a bottom-bar destination and a hand-made Row instead of `SumaTopBar` (`ServicesTransactionsScreen.kt:119-125`); the detail uses Material `TopAppBar` / raw `Button`s, not `SumaButton` (`AppointmentDetailScreen.kt:141-153,180-213`). Fix: `SumaTopBar` with back arrow, `SumaButton` variants.
12. small — The Cancel dialog's red button says "Cancelar cita" also for a taller "orden" (`ax_cancel`); pt mixes "reserva" and "agendamento" on the same flow. Fix: word by kind; pick one pt word.
13. small — Phone under the client name is raw digits "+573001234567" (`ServicesTransactionsScreen.kt:236-238`). Fix: `formatWhatsappForReading`.
14. small — No reminder of any kind: no notification to the owner before an appointment and no reminder text to the client (only online bookings ring). Fix (if wanted): a day-before / morning-of local notification under the BOOKING switch, and a "Recordar por WhatsApp" button on today's rows.
15. small (possible) — The hour-overlap count buckets by UTC hour (`ui/pos/GuideActions.kt:117`, `AppointmentActions.kt:68`); right for every target country except Newfoundland (UTC−3:30).

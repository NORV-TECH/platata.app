## 1. What is protected in a services business (owner phone)
| Action | Gate | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Open the business panel (flag) | phone lock, 10 min session | `ui/app/PlatataApp.kt:254-255, 575-580` — "¿Abrir tu negocio?" / "Confirma tu identidad para abrir tu negocio" |
| Delete a PRODUCT (Productos tab) | phone lock when "Eliminar datos" on | `ItemsScreen.kt:395-410, 1537-1544` — "¿Eliminar artículo?" / "Confirma para eliminar %1$s" |
| **Delete a SERVICE** | **none** — plain "¿Eliminar?" box only | `ServiceEditScreen.kt:268-293` (see problem 1) |
| Delete an add-on / option list | phone lock | `CategoryHubScreen`, `OptionsManagementScreen` |
| Delete a client / an employee | phone lock when "Eliminar empleados y clientes" on | `CustomerCrmScreen.kt:761`, `TeamManagementScreen.kt:146` |
| Remove an extra charge at checkout | phone lock only when "Quitar cargos" on | `CartSheetContent.kt:232, 956-962` — "¿Quitar el cargo?" / "Confirma para quitar el cargo" |
| Refund a sale | 4-digit PIN pad (owner or staff with refund permission) | as restaurant §4d |
| Cancel an appointment | no gate (nothing was charged) | `AppointmentDetailScreen.kt:373-382` — "¿Cancelar esta cita?" / "Se sale de la agenda y la hora queda libre. No se cobra nada. Queda en Ventas marcada como Anulada." |
- Services seed NO service charge (`SeedStore.kt:201-205`), so the "Quitar cargos" prompt is only seen after the owner creates an extra charge in "Descuentos y combos" › "Cargos extra" (ideas offered to services: card surcharge, late-night).
- "Bloquear la caja después de" matters for the staff phone (Mini POS: services use it with Agendar/Cobrar) and the counter tablet; the owner phone never auto-locks.
- Same for all four trades.

## 6. UX/UI problems
1. **Deleting a service skips the phone lock.** `ServiceEditScreen.kt:282-290` calls `viewModel.deleteItem(product)` straight after the "¿Eliminar?" box; the product form's delete asks `showBiometricPrompt` when Security is on (`ItemsScreen.kt:400-407`). With "Eliminar datos" ON, anyone holding the unlocked owner phone can delete "Corte de dama". Small (not on the demo path). Fix: same gate as `ItemsScreen` (`isSecurityEnabled && protectDeleteData` → `showBiometricPrompt`, title "¿Eliminar servicio?").
2. The service page's delete button says "Eliminar artículo" (`items_delete_item`, `ServiceEditScreen.kt:274`). Small. Fix: "Eliminar servicio" / "Excluir serviço" / "Delete service".
3. "Eliminar datos" subtitle "Artículos, categorías, modificadores y opciones" (`SecurityScreen.kt:169`) — a services owner has services, products and add-ons, and cannot manage categories. Small. Fix: per-type subtitle ("Servicios, productos, adiciones y opciones").
